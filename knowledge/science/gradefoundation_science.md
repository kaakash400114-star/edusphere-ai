# Foundation Science (Pre-KG)


## My body and my five senses
We learn about our body and our five senses. We touch soft toys and hard blocks to feel with our hands. We play a game where we close our eyes and guess a sound we hear. We sing a song pointing to our eyes, ears, nose, mouth, and hands. We smell different fruits like a banana and an orange. We look around the room and talk about all the beautiful colors we see. The teacher helps us do all these fun things so we can learn with our bodies.

## Animals: big and small friends
We learn about big and small animal friends. The teacher shows us stuffed animals, like a big bear and a little mouse. We use our hands to show big and small by stretching wide and making a tiny circle. We sing a song, "The Big Bear Walks and the Little Mouse Squeaks." We pretend to be the animals, walking big and small around the room. We feel soft fur and listen to animal sounds. We love learning about our animal friends together.

## Plants: how a seed grows
Children learn that a tiny seed needs water and sun to grow into a big plant. We use real beans and a clear cup with a wet paper towel to see a sprout pop out. We sing a slow song about the seed sleeping in the dark, then waking up with a stretch. Children pretend to be a small seed, curling up on the floor and slowly growing tall like a plant. We play a game where they give the seed a pretend drink of water and a warm sun hug every day. These fun activities help them understand how amazing and patient nature is.

## Water and weather
Little children learn about water and weather by playing with it. They splash in water trays and feel rain drops on their hands. The teacher sings sunny, rainy, and windy songs while children move their bodies like the weather. Children use toy boats to float on water and blow feathers to feel the wind. The teacher helps them guess if a cloud feels fluffy or if a puddle is deep. They learn weather words like sunny, wet, and windy by doing fun actions every day.

## Day and night: sun, moon, and stars
Little learners discover the sun wakes us up for playtime while the moon lets us rest at night. The teacher uses a bright yellow ball to be the sun, showing how it hides behind a soft blue blanket when it's bedtime. A smaller, dark ball becomes the moon and children can hold a sprinkle of glitter for twinkling stars. We sing a simple song about the sun going up and the moon going down. Then we play a game: when the teacher shines the "sun" light, we jump and play; when she uses the "moon" light, we quietly pretend to sleep. This helps them understand the big change from day to night with things they can see and touch.

## Things that move: vehicles
Children learn about vehicles by looking at them and making them move. A teacher shows them toy cars and trucks, and helps push them across the floor. They sing a song about cars going "Vroom, vroom!" and trucks going "Beep, beep!" The teacher might hide a toy car and let a child find it, making a happy sound when it is found. They roll the toys down a small ramp to watch them go fast. This play helps children learn the names of things and understand how they move.

## Keeping clean and healthy
Little friends learn germs are tiny and can make us sick. We show them with a glitter shaker—like invisible germs on our hands before washing. Teacher sings a "Wash, wash, wash your hands" song while we scrub with soap. We play "I Spy" to find yucky things, like used tissues or muddy shoes. Then we throw them in the bin! We pretend to sneeze into our elbow like a superhero shield. Finally, we cheer for clean hands to stay healthy and strong.
