## PHYSICS - Units and Measurements

- **Physical quantities** are measured in base units (SI): metre, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole, candela.
- **Dimensional analysis** checks equations: [F] = MLT⁻^2, [energy] = ML^2T⁻^2.
- **Significant figures** and errors: absolute error, relative error, percentage error; errors add for sums, percentage errors add for products.
- Accuracy = closeness to true value; precision = reproducibility.

## PHYSICS - Motion in a Straight Line

- Distance/displacement, speed/velocity, acceleration - instantaneous vs average.
- v = dx/dt; a = dv/dt; slopes of x-t and v-t graphs.
- Equations for uniform acceleration: v = u + at, s = ut + ½at^2, v^2 = u^2 + 2as.
- Relative velocity: v_AB = v_A − v_B.

## PHYSICS - Motion in a Plane

- **Vectors**: addition by triangle/parallelogram law; components Ax = A cos θ, Ay = A sin θ.
- **Projectile motion**: independent horizontal (uniform) and vertical (accelerated) motions. Time of flight T = 2u sinθ/g; max height H = u^2sin^2θ/2g; range R = u^2sin2θ/g (max at 45°).
- **Uniform circular motion**: a = v^2/r towards centre (centripetal). Angular velocity ω = v/r.

## PHYSICS - Laws of Motion

- Newton I: inertia; II: F = dp/dt = ma; III: equal-opposite action-reaction pairs on different bodies.
- **Impulse**: J = FΔt = Δp (area under F-t graph).
- **Friction**: static ≥ kinetic; f = μN; rolling friction smallest.
- **Circular dynamics**: banking of roads tan θ = v^2/rg; vertical circle minimum speed at top √(5gr) for string to stay taut.
- Mechanical equilibrium, common forces in nature (weight, normal, tension, spring F = −kx).

## PHYSICS - Work, Energy and Power

- W = F·d·cosθ; work-energy theorem: W_net = ΔKE.
- **KE** = ½mv^2; **PE** = mgh or ½kx^2 (spring).
- **Conservation of mechanical energy** in conservative fields (pendulum, falling bodies).
- **Collisions**: elastic (KE conserved) vs inelastic; 1-D elastic formulas; coefficient of restitution e = relative velocity of separation/approach.
- Power P = dW/dt (watt); 1 hp = 746 W.

## PHYSICS - Gravitation

- Newton's law F = Gm1m2/r^2; G = 6.67×10⁻^1^1.
- g = GM/R^2; value varies with altitude, depth, latitude (9.8 m/s^2 at surface).
- **Escape velocity** ve = √(2GM/R) = 11.2 km/s for earth; orbital velocity vo = √(GM/r); geostationary satellites ~36,000 km, period 24 h.
- Kepler's laws: orbits are ellipses; equal areas in equal times; T^2 ∝ a^3.
- Gravitational PE = −GMm/r; weightlessness in orbit.

## PHYSICS - Thermodynamics

- **Zeroth law**: thermal equilibrium defines temperature.
- **First law**: ΔQ = ΔU + ΔW (energy conservation). Specific heat capacities Cp > Cv; Cp − Cv = R.
- **Second law**: heat flows spontaneously hot → cold; entropy of an isolated system never decreases.
- Processes: isothermal (ΔT=0), adiabatic (ΔQ=0, PV^γ = const), isobaric, isochoric.
- **Heat engines**: efficiency η = 1 − Q2/Q1; Carnot engine (max efficiency between two reservoirs); refrigerators (coefficient of performance).

## PHYSICS - Oscillations and Waves

- **SHM**: a = −ω^2x; x = A sin(ωt + φ); T = 2π√(m/k) (spring), 2π√(l/g) (pendulum).
- Energy in SHM: KE ↔ PE exchange; total E = ½mω^2A^2.
- **Wave motion**: transverse (string) and longitudinal (sound); v = νλ.
- Speed of sound in air ≈ 340 m/s; faster in warmer air. Doppler effect: apparent frequency changes with relative motion.
- **Standing waves**: nodes/antinodes; fundamental and harmonics in strings and pipes; beats (|ν1 − ν2|).

## CHEMISTRY - Some Basic Concepts of Chemistry

- Matter, mixtures, pure substances; SI units; uncertainty in measurement.
- **Mole concept**: 1 mol = 6.022×10^2^3 entities; molarity M = mol/L; molality m = mol/kg solvent.
- Empirical vs molecular formula; stoichiometry, limiting reagent, mass-percent, yield.

## CHEMISTRY - Structure of Atom

- Subatomic particles; atomic number Z, mass number A; isotopes/isobars/isotones.
- Developments: Planck's quantum theory, photoelectric effect, Bohr model (energy En = −13.6/n^2 eV for H), de Broglie λ = h/mv, Heisenberg uncertainty Δx·Δp ≥ h/4π.
- **Quantum mechanical model**: orbitals (s, p, d, f), quantum numbers (n, l, m, s), Aufbau, Pauli exclusion, Hund's rule; electronic configurations.

## CHEMISTRY - Classification of Elements and Periodicity

- Modern long form: 18 groups, 7 periods; s/p/d/f blocks.
- Periodic trends with reasons: atomic radius (down ↑, across ↓), ionisation enthalpy (across ↑), electron gain enthalpy, electronegativity; anomalies (Be/B, N/O) explained by subshell stability.
- Periodic properties: valence, metallic character, diagonal relationship (Li-Mg, Be-Al).

## CHEMISTRY - Chemical Bonding

- **Ionic bond**: electron transfer; lattice enthalpy; properties.
- **Covalent bond**: sharing; Lewis structures; formal charge.
- **VSEPR theory**: linear, trigonal planar, tetrahedral, trigonal bipyramidal, octahedral shapes; lone-pair repulsions (H2O bent, NH3 pyramidal).
- **Valence bond theory**: overlap, sigma/pi bonds; hybridisation sp, sp^2, sp^3 (examples CH4, C2H4, BF3); MO theory basics (bond order, magnetic behaviour of O2).

## CHEMISTRY - States of Matter

- **Gases**: measurable properties; **ideal gas equation PV = nRT**; Dalton's partial pressures; Graham's diffusion ∝ 1/√M; kinetic theory postulates; real gas deviations - van der Waals equation; compressibility factor.
- **Liquids**: vapour pressure, boiling point, surface tension, viscosity; hydrogen bonding in water.

## CHEMISTRY - Thermodynamics (Chemical)

- System/surroundings; state functions; internal energy, enthalpy H = U + PV.
- ΔH = ΔU + ΔngRT; Hess's law of constant heat summation; bond enthalpies; enthalpies of formation, combustion, solution, lattice.
- **Spontaneity**: ΔG = ΔH − TΔS; ΔG < 0 spontaneous; entropy as disorder (ΔS).

## CHEMISTRY - Equilibrium

- **Physical equilibria** (vapour-liquid, solid-liquid) and **chemical equilibrium** (dynamic, reversible).
- **Law of mass action**, equilibrium constant Kc, Kp (Kp = Kc(RT)^Δn); Le Chatelier's principle (shift with concentration, pressure, temperature).
- **Ionic equilibrium**: acids/bases (Arrhenius, Brönsted-Lowry, Lewis); pH = −log[H⁺]; buffers; Ksp solubility product; common ion effect; hydrolysis of salts.

## CHEMISTRY - Redox Reactions

- Oxidation number rules; identify redox in displacement, combustion, disproportionation.
- Balancing redox: oxidation-number and ion-electron (half-reaction) methods.
- Types: combination, decomposition, displacement, disproportionation; redox in titrations (KMnO4, K2Cr2O7) and everyday processes (corrosion, respiration).

## CHEMISTRY - Organic Chemistry: Some Basic Principles

- **Tetravalence, catenation**; structural representations; classification (acyclic/cyclic, aromatic).
- **IUPAC nomenclature** rules; functional groups priority.
- **Isomerism**: chain, position, functional, metamerism; **tautomerism**.
- **Reaction mechanisms**: fission (homolytic → free radicals; heterolytic → carbocations/carbanions), electrophiles/nucleophiles; inductive, resonance, hyperconjugation effects; types: substitution, addition, elimination, rearrangement.

## CHEMISTRY - Hydrocarbons

- **Alkanes**: preparation (hydrogenation, decarboxylation, Wurtz); reactions - halogenation (free-radical), combustion, cracking; conformations.
- **Alkenes**: preparation (dehydration, dehydrohalogenation - Saytzeff); electrophilic addition (Markovnikov's rule, peroxide effect), ozonolysis, polymerisation.
- **Alkynes**: acidity of terminal H; addition reactions; formation of benzene.
- **Aromatic hydrocarbons**: benzene structure (resonance, Huckel rule); electrophilic substitution - nitration, halogenation, sulphonation, Friedel-Crafts; directive influence of groups; carcinogenic polynuclear hydrocarbons.

## BIOLOGY - The Living World

- **Biodiversity**: 1.7+ million described species; definition of life needs taxonomy.
- **Taxonomic hierarchy**: Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species; binomial nomenclature (Mangifera indica - Carolus Linnaeus).
- Tools: herbarium, botanical garden, museum, zoological park, key, catalogue, monograph.

## BIOLOGY - Biological Classification

- **Five kingdoms** (Whittaker): Monera (bacteria - archaebacteria, eubacteria, cyanobacteria; mycoplasma), Protista (amoeba, paramoecium, euglena, slime moulds), Fungi (saprophytic; chytrids, zygomycetes, ascomycetes, basidiomycetes; lichens symbiosis), Plantae, Animalia.
- Viruses (crystallisable, inert outside host) and viroids (RNA only) - acellular.

## BIOLOGY - Plant Kingdom

- **Algae** (chlorophyceae, phaeophyceae, rhodophyceae - habitats, pigments, uses: agar, algin, protein-rich chlorella).
- **Bryophytes**: amphibians of the plant world (mosses, liverworts); peat.
- **Pteridophytes**: first vascular plants (ferns); heterospory precursor to seed habit.
- **Gymnosperms**: naked seeds (Pinus, Cycas; conifers).
- **Angiosperms**: covered seeds, double fertilisation, flowers; monocots vs dicots.
- Life cycles: haplontic, diplontic, haplo-diplontic; alternation of generations.

## BIOLOGY - Animal Kingdom

- Basis of classification: levels of organisation, symmetry, germ layers, coelom, segmentation, notochord.
- Non-chordates: Porifera (sponges), Cnidaria (cnidoblasts), Platyhelminthes (flatworms), Aschelminthes (roundworms), Annelida (metameric), Arthropoda (largest phylum), Mollusca (second largest), Echinodermata (water vascular system).
- Chordates: notochord, dorsal hollow nerve cord, pharyngeal gill slits, post-anal tail.
- Classes: Cyclostomata, Chondrichthyes, Osteichthyes, Amphibia, Reptilia, Aves, Mammalia - with key identifying features.

## BIOLOGY - Morphology of Flowering Plants

- **Root**: tap, fibrous, adventitious; regions; modifications (storage, prop, pneumatophores).
- **Stem**: nodes/internodes; modifications (rhizome, tuber, tendril, thorn).
- **Leaf**: parts, venation (reticulate vs parallel), phyllotaxy; modifications (spines, tendril).
- **Inflorescence**: racemose, cymose.
- **Flower**: parts, symmetry, aestivation, androecium/gynoecium; placentation types.
- **Fruit and seed**: true/false fruits; dicot vs monocot seed structure; floral formulae and diagrams (mustard, pea, tomato, onion families).

## BIOLOGY - Anatomy of Flowering Plants

- **Tissue systems**: epidermal, ground, vascular.
- Monocot vs dicot root, stem, leaf anatomy (xylem arrangement, bundle sheath, stomata).
- **Secondary growth**: cambium, annual rings, periderm (dicot stems/roots).

## BIOLOGY - Structural Organisation in Animals

- **Animal tissues**: epithelial (types + locations), connective (loose, dense, specialised - cartilage, bone, blood), muscular (skeletal, smooth, cardiac), nervous (neuron structure, neuroglia).
- **Earthworm**: morphology, digestive/excretory (nephridia) systems, economic importance.
- **Cockroach**: external features, digestive system with crop/gizzard, tracheal respiration, open circulatory system, nervous system, malpighian tubules.
- **Frog**: morphology, organ systems (respiration through skin/lungs; closed circulation; urinary system; frog as a laboratory animal).

## BIOLOGY - Cell: The Unit of Life

- Cell theory (Schleiden, Schwann, Virchow); prokaryotic vs eukaryotic cells.
- **Cell organelles**: cell wall, membrane (fluid mosaic model, transport - diffusion/osmosis/active), ER, Golgi, lysosomes, vacuoles, mitochondria, plastids, ribosomes (70S/80S), cytoskeleton, cilia/flagella, centrioles, nucleus (chromatin, nucleolus).

## BIOLOGY - Biomolecules

- **Primary metabolites**: carbohydrates (mono/di/poly-saccharides), lipids, amino acids/proteins (structure levels), nucleotides (DNA/RNA), enzymes (apoenzyme + coenzyme; lock-and-key/induced fit; inhibition, temperature/pH effects).
- **Metabolic pathways**: anabolism/catabolism; ATP as energy currency.

## BIOLOGY - Cell Cycle and Cell Division

- **Cell cycle**: interphase (G1, S, G2) + M phase.
- **Mitosis**: prophase, metaphase, anaphase, telophase + cytokinesis - growth and repair; significance.
- **Meiosis I & II**: pairing (synapsis), crossing over (variation), reduction to haploid - significance in sexuality and evolution.

## BIOLOGY - Photosynthesis in Higher Plants

- Chloroplast pigments (chl a, b, carotenoids); absorption/action spectra.
- **Light reaction** (thylakoid): photosystems PS I & II, Z-scheme, photolysis, non-cyclic and cyclic photophosphorylation, ATP + NADPH.
- **Dark reaction** (stroma): Calvin cycle (carboxylation, reduction, regeneration - 3 ATP + 2 NADPH per CO2), C4 pathway (Hatch-Slack - Kranz anatomy, maize), photorespiration (RuBisCO + O2 - wasteful), CAM in succulents.
- Factors affecting photosynthesis: light, CO2, temperature, water (law of limiting factors).

## BIOLOGY - Respiration in Plants

- **Glycolysis** (cytoplasm): glucose → pyruvate, 2 ATP net.
- **Fermentation**: alcoholic (yeast) and lactic acid (muscle) - anaerobic, low ATP.
- **Aerobic respiration** (mitochondria): oxidative decarboxylation (pyruvate → acetyl CoA), Krebs/TCA cycle, ETS + oxidative phosphorylation (chemiosmotic hypothesis), RQ values.
- Amphibolic pathway: respiration of fats and proteins too.
