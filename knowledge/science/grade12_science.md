## PHYSICS - Electric Charges and Fields

- **Charge**: quantised (q = ne), conserved, additive; like charges repel.
- **Coulomb's law**: F = k q1q2/r^2; k = 1/4πε0 = 9×10^9 N·m^2/C^2; permittivity of medium reduces force.
- **Electric field** E = F/q; field lines (never cross, leave + enter −).
- **Dipole**: p = q(2a); field on axis/ equator; torque τ = pE sinθ in a uniform field.
- **Gauss's theorem**: flux Φ = q_enclosed/ε0; applications - field of infinite wire (λ/2πε0r), infinite sheet (σ/2ε0), sphere (as point charge outside).

## PHYSICS - Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance

- **Potential** V = W/q (volt); potential due to point charge kq/r; potential difference independent of path (conservative field).
- **Equipotential surfaces** ⊥ field lines; potential energy of a system of charges; PE of dipole = −pE cosθ.
- **Capacitance** C = Q/V (farad). Parallel plate: C = ε0A/d (dielectric increases it K×).
- **Combinations**: series 1/C = Σ1/Ci; parallel C = ΣCi.
- **Energy stored** U = ½CV^2 = Q^2/2C. Applications: flash cameras, defibrillators; Van de Graaff generator principle.

## PHYSICS - Current Electricity

- **Current** I = Q/t; drift velocity; I = neAv_d; Ohm's law V = IR; resistivity ρ, conductivity σ; temperature dependence of resistance (metals ↑, semiconductors ↓).
- **EMF and internal resistance**: terminal voltage V = ε − Ir.
- **Kirchhoff's rules**: junction rule (current), loop rule (potential) - Wheatstone bridge balance P/Q = R/S.
- **Meter bridge, potentiometer** (comparing EMFs, internal resistance).
- **Cells in series/parallel**; electrical energy and power.

## PHYSICS - Moving Charges and Magnetism

- **Biot-Savart law**: dB = (μ0/4π) I dl sinθ / r^2 - field of a circular loop B = μ0NI/2R.
- **Ampere's circuital law**: field of a long straight wire B = μ0I/2πr; solenoid B = μ0nI; toroid.
- **Force on a charge** F = qvB sinθ (Lorentz); circular motion radius r = mv/qB; cyclotron frequency.
- **Force on a wire** F = BIL sinθ; force between parallel currents (definition of ampere); torque on a loop τ = NIAB sinθ - moving-coil galvanometer.
- Conversion: ammeter (shunt in parallel), voltmeter (high R in series).

## PHYSICS - Magnetism and Matter

- Bar magnet as equivalent solenoid; magnetic field lines (closed loops); magnetic dipole moment; earth's magnetism (elements: declination, dip, horizontal intensity).
- Classification: dia-, para-, ferromagnetic materials; Curie temperature; hysteresis (soft vs hard magnets, permanent magnets and electromagnets).

## PHYSICS - Electromagnetic Induction

- **Faraday's law**: ε = −N dΦ/dt; Lenz's law (opposes change - energy conservation).
- **Motional EMF**: ε = Blv.
- **Self-inductance** (ε = −L dI/dt; solenoid L = μ0n^2Al) and **mutual inductance**; energy stored U = ½LI^2.
- **AC generator** principle; eddy currents (induction furnaces, magnetic braking, induction cooktops).

## PHYSICS - Alternating Current

- V = V0sin ωt; average and RMS values (V_rms = V0/√2).
- Circuit elements with AC: pure R (in phase), pure L (current lags 90°), pure C (leads 90°); reactances X_L = ωL, X_C = 1/ωC.
- **LCR series circuit**: impedance Z = √(R^2 + (X_L − X_C)^2); phase angle; resonance ω = 1/√(LC) - maximum current; sharpness = Q factor.
- **Power**: P = V_rms I_rms cos φ (power factor); wattless current.
- **Transformer**: εs/εp = Ns/Np; step-up/step-down; energy losses (copper, eddy, hysteresis, flux leakage) - why AC is transmitted at high voltage.

## PHYSICS - Electromagnetic Waves

- Maxwell's displacement current completes Ampere's law.
- **EM wave properties**: transverse; E ⊥ B ⊥ propagation; speed c = 1/√(μ0ε0) = 3×10^8 m/s; carry energy and momentum (radiation pressure).
- **Spectrum** (increasing frequency): radio, micro, IR, visible, UV, X-rays, gamma - sources and uses of each band.

## PHYSICS - Ray Optics

- **Reflection**: mirrors, sign conventions; mirror formula 1/v + 1/u = 1/f; magnification.
- **Refraction**: Snell's law; refractive index; TIR (critical angle) - optical fibres, mirage, diamonds' sparkle; refraction through a prism (A + D = i + e).
- **Lenses**: lens-maker's formula, thin-lens formula 1/v − 1/u = 1/f; combinations; power (dioptre).
- **Optical instruments**: simple microscope M = 1 + D/f; compound microscope; telescope (angular magnification fo/fe).
- Scattering: blue sky, reddish sun (qualitative).

## PHYSICS - Wave Optics

- **Huygens' principle** explains reflection and refraction.
- **Young's double slit**: coherent sources; fringe width β = λD/d; constructive/destructive conditions; intensity distribution.
- Diffraction at a single slit (central maximum width 2λD/a); contrast with interference; resolving power basics.
- Polarisation: only transverse waves; Brewster's law μ = tan i_p; uses (sunglasses, LCDs).

## PHYSICS - Dual Nature of Radiation and Matter

- **Photoelectric effect**: instantaneous emission; threshold frequency; KEmax = hν − φ0 (Einstein's equation); failure of wave theory.
- Photocell uses. **de Broglie**: λ = h/mv - matter waves; Davisson-Germer verification; electron microscope.

## PHYSICS - Atoms

- **Rutherford model**: alpha scattering → nucleus; limitations (instability, line spectra).
- **Bohr model**: quantised orbits, energy levels En = −13.6/n^2 eV; spectral series of hydrogen (Lyman UV, Balmer visible, Paschen/Brackett/Pfund IR).
- de Broglie's justification of quantisation; limitations of Bohr's model.

## PHYSICS - Nuclei

- Nuclear size, density (constant ~2.3×10^1^7 kg/m^3); mass defect and **binding energy** E_b = Δm c^2; BE/nucleon curve - peak at Fe-56 (fission of heavy, fusion of light nuclei release energy).
- **Radioactivity**: α (He nucleus), β (electron), γ (photon) decay; α-decay reduces A by 4, Z by 2; β increases Z by 1.
- **Decay law**: N = N0e^(−λt); half-life T½ = 0.693/λ; activity (becquerel/curie); carbon dating; radiotherapy and hazards.

## PHYSICS - Semiconductor Electronics

- **Energy bands**: conductors, insulators, semiconductors; intrinsic (Si, Ge) vs extrinsic doping.
- **n-type** (pentavalent - electrons majority) and **p-type** (trivalent - holes majority).
- **p-n junction diode**: depletion region, barrier potential; forward bias (current flows), reverse bias (tiny leakage); rectifier diode.
- **Half-wave and full-wave rectifiers**: working with waveforms; uses of diodes (LED - energy saving, photodiode - light sensors, solar cell).
- Logic gates (NOT, OR, AND, NAND, NOR) as building blocks of digital circuits.

## CHEMISTRY - Solutions

- Types of solutions; expressing concentration: molarity, molality, mole fraction, ppm.
- **Raoult's law**: vapour pressure of solutions; ideal vs non-ideal (positive/negative deviations - azeotropes).
- **Colligative properties**: relative lowering of vapour pressure, boiling-point elevation ΔTb = Kb·m, freezing-point depression ΔTf = Kf·m, osmotic pressure π = CRT (isotonic, hypertonic, hypotonic - reverse osmosis desalination); abnormal molar mass, van't Hoff factor (association/dissociation).

## CHEMISTRY - Electrochemistry

- **Electrochemical (Galvanic) cells**: electrode potential, standard hydrogen electrode; **Nernst equation** E = E° − (0.059/n) log Q; EMF and spontaneity (ΔG = −nFE).
- Conductance, conductivity, molar conductivity (variation with concentration; Kohlrausch's law).
- **Electrolysis**: Faraday's laws; products at electrodes; batteries - primary (dry cell, mercury), secondary (lead-acid, Ni-Cd, Li-ion), fuel cells (H2-O2 clean energy).
- **Corrosion**: rusting electrochemistry; prevention (galvanising, anodic protection).

## CHEMISTRY - Chemical Kinetics

- **Rate of reaction**: average/instantaneous; rate law, order vs molecularity; units of k.
- **Integrated rate laws**: zero order, first order (t½ = 0.693/k independent of concentration); pseudo-first order (ester hydrolysis).
- **Arrhenius equation** k = Ae^(−Ea/RT); activation energy; effect of temperature; collision theory basics.
- Catalysis: lowers Ea; examples in industry.

## CHEMISTRY - d- and f-Block Elements

- **Transition elements**: electronic configuration (n−1)d^1⁻^1^0 ns^1⁻^2; variable oxidation states (Mn highest), coloured ions (d-d transitions), magnetic properties (unpaired electrons), catalytic behaviour (Fe, V2O5, Ni), alloy formation, interstitial compounds.
- **Lanthanoid contraction** and consequences; lanthanoid vs actinoid chemistry; some applications (steel, catalysts, magnets).

## CHEMISTRY - Coordination Compounds

- **Werner's theory**; definitions: central metal, ligands (mono/poly-dentate, chelation), coordination number, sphere of ionisation; IUPAC naming.
- **Werner/VBT/CFT explanations**: hybridisations and geometries (sp^3, dsp^2, sp^3d^2, d^2sp^3); crystal field splitting Δo in octahedral fields; weak vs strong field ligands; high/low spin; colour and magnetism from d-d transitions.
- **Isomerism**: structural (ionisation, hydrate, linkage, coordination) and stereoisomerism (geometric cis/trans; optical).
- Importance: chlorophyll (Mg), haemoglobin (Fe), vitamin B12 (Co), cis-platin (medicine), analytical chemistry, electroplating.

## CHEMISTRY - Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

- Classification and nomenclature; nature of C-X bond (polarity, bond length/strength trend).
- **Preparation**: from alcohols (HCl/ZnCl2 - Lucas test, PBr3, SOCl2), halogen exchange (Finkelstein, Swarts).
- **Reactions**: nucleophilic substitution SN1 (tertiary, carbocation, racemisation) vs SN2 (primary, inversion - Walden); elimination (β-hydrogen, Saytzeff) vs substitution competition; reaction with metals (Grignard, Wurtz).
- **Reactions of haloarenes**: low reactivity (resonance, sp^2 C); electrophilic substitution (o,p-directing); Dow process.
- Environmental: DDT persistence, freons and ozone.

## CHEMISTRY - Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers

- **Alcohols**: preparation (hydration of alkenes - Markovnikov, hydroboration, reduction of carbonyls, fermentation); acidity trend; reactions - with Na, HX (Lucas test reactivity 3°>2°>1°), PX3/PX5, dehydration (alkenes), oxidation (1°→acid, 2°→ketone, 3° no oxidation); esterification.
- **Phenols**: acidity (resonance-stabilised phenoxide) vs alcohols; electrophilic substitution (nitration, halogenation - white precipitate with bromine water test), Kolbe and Reimer-Tiemann reactions; uses (antiseptic).
- **Ethers**: Williamson synthesis; cleavage with HI; low reactivity; anaesthetic use.
- Commercial alcohols: methanol (denatured, poisonous - wood spirit), ethanol (absolute - azeotrope issue).

## CHEMISTRY - Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids

- **Nomenclature and structure**: carbonyl C (sp^2); nucleophilic addition centre.
- **Preparation**: oxidation/dehydrogenation of alcohols, ozonolysis, hydration of alkynes, Rosenmund reduction, Friedel-Crafts acylation.
- **Reactions of aldehydes/ketones**: HCN, NaHSO3, Grignard, alcohol (hemiacetal/acetal), ammonia derivatives (oxime, hydrazone), haloform reaction (methyl ketones), aldol condensation (α-H), Cannizzaro (no α-H).
- **Tests**: Tollens' (silver mirror), Fehling's (red ppt), iodoform (yellow).
- **Carboxylic acids**: acidity (resonance, EWG increase it); preparation (oxidation, hydrolysis of nitriles, Grignard + CO2); reactions - esterification (Fischer), acid chlorides (SOCl2), anhydrides, amides, Hell-Volhard-Zelinsky (α-halogenation); decarboxylation.

## CHEMISTRY - Amines

- Classification (1°, 2°, 3°); structure - pyramidal; basicity trends (aliphatic > ammonia; aqueous order 2°>1°>3°>NH3; aromatic much less - resonance).
- **Preparation**: reduction of nitro compounds/nitriles, Hofmann bromamide degradation (one C less), Gabriel phthalimide (pure 1°), ammonolysis.
- **Reactions**: alkylation, acylation, carbylamine test (1° only - foul smell), Hinsberg test (separating amines), reaction with nitrous acid (1° aliphatic → alcohol + N2; aromatic diazonium at 273-278 K).
- **Diazonium salts**: synthetic importance - Sandmeyer (Cl, Br, CN), azo dyes coupling (p-Hydroxyazobenzene orange dye), replacement by H/I/F (Balz-Schiemann).

## CHEMISTRY - Biomolecules

- **Carbohydrates**: mono (glucose - open + cyclic structure, anomer), disaccharides (sucrose = glucose + fructose; lactose), polysaccharides (starch, cellulose - structural; glycogen); reducing vs non-reducing; glycosidic linkage.
- **Proteins**: amino acids (essential/non-essential, zwitterion); peptide bond; structure levels (primary→quaternary); denaturation; enzymes as biocatalysts.
- **Vitamins**: fat-soluble (A, D, E, K) vs water-soluble (B, C); deficiency diseases (A-night blindness, B1-beriberi, C-scurvy, D-rickets).
- **Nucleic acids**: DNA/RNA building blocks (sugar + base + phosphate); Watson-Crick double helix, base pairing A-T, G-C; heredity and protein synthesis; DNA fingerprinting.
- **Hormones** (brief): steroid/protein classes and roles.

## BIOLOGY - Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants

- Flower structure: anther (microsporangia, pollen), pistil (megasporangium - embryo sac formation, 7 cells 8 nuclei).
- **Pollination**: types (autogamy, geitonogamy, xenogamy), agents (wind, water, insects - adaptations); pollen-pistil interaction; artificial hybridisation (emasculation, bagging).
- **Double fertilisation**: syngamy + triple fusion → zygote (2n) + PEN (3n endosperm).
- **Post-fertilisation**: endosperm, embryo development (dicot vs monocot embryo), seed and fruit formation; apomixis and polyembryony.

## BIOLOGY - Human Reproduction

- **Male system**: testes (seminiferous tubules - spermatogenesis; Leydig cells - testosterone), ducts, accessory glands (seminal vesicles, prostate, bulbourethral), penis.
- **Female system**: ovaries (oogenesis - ova + hormones), fallopian tubes (fertilisation - ampulla), uterus (myometrium, endometrium), cervix, vagina; mammary glands.
- **Gametogenesis**: spermatogenesis vs spermiogenesis vs spermiation; structure of sperm; oogenesis (prenatal start, ovulation at puberty - Graafian follicle, corpus luteum).
- **Menstrual cycle**: 28 days - menstrual, follicular, ovulatory (LH surge day 14), luteal phases; hormonal control (FSH, LH, oestrogen, progesterone).
- **Fertilisation and development**: acrosomal reaction, implantation, placenta (exchange + hormones hCG, hPL, relaxin), gestation, parturition (oxytocin), lactation (colostrum - antibodies).

## BIOLOGY - Reproductive Health

- Problems and strategies: population explosion, sex-ed awareness.
- **Contraceptive methods**: natural/periodic abstinence, withdrawal, barrier (condoms - also prevent STDs), IUDs (CuT, hormone-releasing), oral pills (Saheli), emergency, surgical (vasectomy/tubectomy); MTP (legalised up to 20 weeks); amniocentesis misuse is banned.
- **STDs**: gonorrhoea, syphilis (bacterial); genital herpes, HIV-AIDS, hepatitis B, HPV (viral); early detection, complete treatment, condoms.
- **Assisted reproduction**: IVF-ET (test-tube babies), ZIFT, GIFT, ICSI, AI - a brief survey.

## BIOLOGY - Principles of Inheritance and Variation

- **Mendel's laws**: dominance, segregation, independent assortment - monohybrid (3:1) and dihybrid (9:3:3:1) ratios; test cross; incomplete dominance (snapdragon), codominance (AB blood group); multiple alleles (ABO).
- **Chromosomal theory** (Sutton-Boveri); linkage and recombination (Morgan's Drosophila - sex-linked white eye).
- **Sex determination**: XX-XY, XO, ZW; haplodiploidy in honeybee.
- **Mutations**: point (frameshift - insertion/deletion), mutagens; Mendelian disorders (haemophilia, sickle-cell anaemia - recessive; thalassemia; colour blindness - X-linked; phenylketonuria).
- **Chromosomal disorders**: Down's (trisomy 21), Turner's (XO female), Klinefelter's (XXY male); pedigree analysis.

## BIOLOGY - Molecular Basis of Inheritance

- **DNA as genetic material**: Griffith's transforming principle, Avery-MacLeod-McCarty, Hershey-Chase blender experiment.
- **Structure**: Watson-Crick double helix (antiparallel, A-T/G-C, 2 nm, 3.4 nm per turn); central dogma DNA → RNA → protein.
- **Replication**: semi-conservative (Meselson-Stahl), DNA polymerase, leading/lagging strand, DNA ligase.
- **Transcription**: promoter, structural gene, terminator; RNA polymerase + sigma; exons/introns; capping and tailing; splicing.
- **Genetic code**: triplet, degenerate, unambiguous, universal, Wobble; tRNA adapter; translation - initiation, elongation, termination; lac operon (inducible regulation).
- **Human Genome Project**: goals, salient findings, bioinformatics impact.
- **DNA fingerprinting**: VNTR/STR, applications in forensics and paternity.

## BIOLOGY - Evolution

- Origin of life (Oparin-Haldane - primordial soup; Miller-Urey experiment).
- **Darwinism**: natural selection, survival of the fittest; branching descent.
- **Evidences**: fossils, homology vs analogy, vestigial organs, embryology, biochemical similarities.
- **Lamarckism** (inheritance of acquired characters - mostly discredited), Darwin's finches (adaptive radiation), industrial melanism.
- **Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium** p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1; forces disturbing it - mutation, gene flow, genetic drift, natural selection.
- Human evolution summary (Australopithecus → Homo habilis → H. erectus → Neanderthal → H. sapiens); out-of-Africa.

## BIOLOGY - Human Health and Disease

- Pathogens: typhoid (Salmonella), pneumonia (Streptococcus/Pneumococcus), common cold (rhinovirus), malaria (Plasmodium - female Anopheles), amoebiasis, ascariasis, elephantiasis (Wuchereria - vectors), ringworm (fungi).
- **Immunity**: innate (barriers, phagocytes) vs acquired (B-lymphocytes - antibodies IgA/G/M/E/D; T-lymphocytes - cell-mediated); active (vaccines) vs passive (antiserum, mother's milk) immunity; allergy (histamine); autoimmunity.
- **AIDS**: HIV (retrovirus), transmission (blood, sexual, mother-child), diagnosis (ELISA), prevention.
- **Cancer**: oncogenes, carcinogens, types (benign vs malignant, metastasis), detection (biopsy, MRI), treatment (surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, immunotherapy).
- **Substance abuse**: drugs (opioids, cannabinoids, cocaine), alcoholism - effects and prevention; adolescence and peer pressure.

## BIOLOGY - Microbes in Human Welfare

- Fermentation products: curd (Lactobacillus), bread (yeast), cheese, toddy.
- **Industrial**: alcoholic beverages, antibiotics (penicillin - Fleming), chemicals (citric acid, acetic acid - acetobacter).
- **Sewage treatment**: primary (physical), secondary (biological - activated sludge, flocs), biogas (methanogens - Methanobacterium in biogas plants).
- **Biocontrol agents**: Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt cotton), Trichoderma, ladybird/aquatic fern biocontrol of pests.
- **Biofertilisers**: Rhizobium, Azotobacter, mycorrhiza (Glomus), cyanobacteria (Anabaena - biofertiliser in paddy fields).

## BIOLOGY - Biotechnology: Principles and Processes

- **Tools**: restriction enzymes (EcoRI - palindromic sites, sticky ends), DNA ligase, cloning vectors (pBR322 features - ori, selectable markers, cloning sites), competent host (CaCl2, biolistics, micro-injection).
- **PCR**: denaturation-annealing-extension (Taq polymerase) - amplification of DNA.
- **Recombinant DNA technology steps**: isolation, cutting, insertion, transfer, selection, scaling up in bioreactors (stirred-tank) - downstream processing.

## BIOLOGY - Biotechnology and its Applications

- **Bt crops**: Bt toxin genes (cry proteins) - insect resistance (Bt cotton, Bt corn).
- **RNA interference (RNAi)**: melon fly/pest control via dsRNA; nematode-resistant tobacco (Meloidogyne).
- **Gene therapy**: ADA deficiency treatment (first clinical gene therapy).
- **Transgenic animals**: Rosie (human protein-enriched milk), disease models, safety testing.
- **Ethical issues**: GEAC regulation, biopiracy (basmati, turmeric, neem patents), patent disputes.

## BIOLOGY - Organisms and Populations

- **Ecology levels**: organism, population, community, ecosystem.
- **Abiotic factors**: temperature (eurythermal/stenothermal), water, light, soil; responses - regulate, conform, migrate (Siberian cranes), suspend (diapause, aestivation, hibernation).
- **Adaptations**: desert (camel, kangaroo rat), altitude (more RBC), aquatic (fish gills).
- **Populations**: size, density, natality, mortality, immigration/emigration; **growth models** - exponential dN/dt = rN (J-curve), logistic dN/dt = rN(K−N)/K (S-curve, carrying capacity K).
- **Interactions**: mutualism (+,+), competition (−,− - Gause's principle, competitive exclusion), predation (roles), parasitism, commensalism (+,0), amensalism.

## BIOLOGY - Ecosystem

- Structure: biotic + abiotic; **energy flow** - 10% law, trophic levels, food chains (grazing/detritus), food webs; standing crop; ecological pyramids (number, biomass, energy - upright; inverted biomass in ocean).
- **Nutrient cycling**: gaseous (C, N) and sedimentary (P, S) cycles; carbon cycle details.
- **Decomposition**: fragmentation, leaching, catabolism, humification, mineralisation.
- **Succession**: primary (lichen → moss → grass → shrub → forest - hydrarch/xerarch), secondary; climax community; ecological services (purification, pollination, climate) - economic value.

## BIOLOGY - Biodiversity and Conservation

- **Biodiversity levels**: genetic, species, ecological; global species estimates; latitudinal gradients (tropics richest - speciation time, productivity, stability).
- **Importance**: ecosystem services, narrow-utilitarian to ethical arguments; species-area relation S = CA^z.
- **Loss causes**: habitat loss/fragmentation (the biggest), over-exploitation, alien invasive species (Lantana, water hyacinth), co-extinctions.
- **Conservation**: in situ (hotspots - Western Ghats, Eastern Himalaya; national parks, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves, sacred groves) and ex situ (zoos, seed/gene banks, tissue culture, cryopreservation).
- **Rio Convention / CBD**: in-situ priority; India's biological diversity act.
