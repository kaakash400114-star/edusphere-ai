# South Korean (NCCE) Grade 4 English

## Daily Life and Routines
Students learn to describe their daily activities and schedules. They use simple present tense to talk about habits, for example, "I wake up at 7 a.m." and "I eat kimchi for breakfast." They practice asking and answering questions about daily routines using "What time do you...?" and "I... at..." They also learn to use sequence words like "first," "next," and "then" to order their day. This helps them structure their speaking and writing to clearly present their personal schedule, such as "First, I go to school. Next, I study."

## Describing People and Places
This topic focuses on using descriptive language to talk about people and places around them. Students learn to use adjectives like "kind," "tall," "old," and "beautiful." For example, "My teacher is very kind" or "Seoul is a big and exciting city." They practice forming sentences to describe the appearance and character of their family and friends, and the features of famous landmarks like Gyeongbokgung Palace. This builds their vocabulary for detailed and accurate descriptions.

## Food and Culture
Students explore Korean cuisine and cultural traditions related to food. They learn names of foods like bibimbap, bulgoki, and kimchi. They use the modal verb "can" to express ability, such as "I can eat spicy food." Lessons include phrases for ordering food, like "I would like to order some bulgoki, please." They also learn to use "and" to combine food items, for example, "I like rice and soup." This connects language learning to their everyday cultural experiences.

## Shopping and Money
This unit teaches students to navigate shopping scenarios using correct terminology for Korean currency, the won (₩). They learn to ask for prices with "How much is this?" and respond with "It's 10,000 won." They practice using "I would like..." to make requests, such as "I would like to buy this hanbok." Lessons also cover differentiating between "need" and "want" when making purchasing decisions, reinforcing functional language for real-world transactions.

## Transportation and Travel
Students learn to talk about different modes of transportation used in South Korea. They study vocabulary like "subway," "bus," "car," and "taxi." They practice using the verb "go" to describe travel, for example, "I go to school by bus." They also learn phrases to ask for directions, such as "How do I get to Seoul Station?" and understand responses like "Take the subway Line 2." This develops their ability to discuss movement and navigate their environment.

## Hobbies and Free Time
This topic focuses on discussing free-time activities and personal interests. Students learn to use the present continuous tense to talk about actions happening now, for example, "I am playing badminton." They practice asking "What are you doing?" and responding with "I am reading a book." Lessons include vocabulary for hobbies such as "watching TV," "listening to music," and "playing the piano," enabling students to share their personal interests with peers.
