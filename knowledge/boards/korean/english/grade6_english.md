# South Korean (NCCE) Grade 6 English
## Narrative Writing Structure
Students will learn to compose multi-paragraph narratives using a clear plot structure. They will master the five key elements: exposition to introduce characters and setting in Seoul, rising action with a central conflict (e.g., losing a family heirloom), a climax, falling action, and a resolution. The writing must include descriptive details about a hanbok or local market to create vivid imagery. They will apply specific transition words to sequence events logically and conclude with a reflective statement.

## Grammatical: Complex Sentence Formation
Grade 6 students will focus on constructing complex sentences to express detailed ideas. They will learn to combine independent clauses with dependent clauses using subordinating conjunctions like 'because', 'although', and 'when'. For example, "We decided to visit Gyeongbokgung Palace because we wanted to learn more about Korean history." This skill is essential for writing more sophisticated essays and improving analytical reasoning for future CSAT preparation.

## Reading Comprehension: Analyzing Textual Evidence
Students will develop the skill of analyzing texts to find supporting evidence. They will read passages, such as a short story about a student saving won to buy a gift, and identify explicit and implicit information. The goal is to formulate arguments by directly quoting or paraphrasing evidence from the text. This method strengthens critical thinking and prepares students to answer detailed questions on standardized assessments like the CSAT.

## Vocabulary: Idioms and Cultural Context
The curriculum requires students to understand and use common English idioms related to emotions and situations. They will learn the meaning and appropriate usage of phrases like "spill the beans" or "cost an arm and a leg." For example, "A trip to Seoul's famous theme park can cost an arm and a leg." This includes recognizing their figurative nature versus literal meanings to improve comprehension in authentic English texts and conversations.

## Oral Presentation: Structured Argument Delivery
Students will prepare and deliver a formal, structured presentation on a given topic. They must organize their speech with an introduction stating a clear thesis, at least three main points with supporting examples (e.g., discussing the benefits of eating kimchi), and a conclusion. Presentations must be delivered with a clear voice, appropriate pace, and eye contact, demonstrating confidence and effective public speaking skills.

## Research Project: Synthesizing Information
Grade 6 students will conduct a short research project to gather and synthesize information from multiple sources. The topic will relate to South Korea, such as the history of the Korean won or the traditions of wearing a hanbok. Students will learn to identify relevant facts, organize their findings into a coherent report, and cite their sources. This foundational skill is crucial for building the advanced research abilities needed for senior high school studies and the CSAT.
