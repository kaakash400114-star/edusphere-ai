# South Korean (NCCE) Grade 9 Mathematics

## Quadratic Functions and Equations
Students explore quadratic functions, represented as $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$, analyzing their parabolic graphs. They master finding the vertex, axis of symmetry, and intercepts. The discriminant, $D = b^2 - 4ac$, determines the nature of the roots. Students solve quadratic equations by factoring, completing the square, and using the quadratic formula, $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$. Real-world applications include calculating the profit curve for a local kimchi business or determining the optimal dimensions for a rectangular field near Seoul to maximize area.

## Trigonometric Ratios and Applications
This section introduces the sine, cosine, and tangent ratios for acute angles in right-angled triangles: sinθ = opposite/hypotenuse, cosθ = adjacent/hypotenuse, tanθ = opposite/adjacent. Students use these to solve problems involving indirect measurement. The concept is applied to calculating the height of Namsan Tower from a given distance or determining the angle of elevation to see the top of a skyscraper in Seoul. The foundational skills are essential for the CSAT mathematics section.

## Geometry of Circles
Students delve into the properties of circles, understanding theorems related to chords, arcs, and angles. Key concepts include the relationship between a central angle and its intercepted arc, and the properties of tangents and secants. Theorems state that angles subtended by the same arc are equal and that the angle in a semicircle is a right angle. These principles are used to solve for unknown lengths and angles in complex diagrams, such as those found in traditional Korean architecture like the curves of a hanbok sleeve.

## Probability and Statistics
Students study probability using the formula P(A) = Number of favorable outcomes / Total number of outcomes. They calculate the probability of single and compound events. In statistics, they analyze data by calculating mean, median, mode, range, and standard deviation. A practical application is determining the probability of winning a prize in a traditional Korean lottery or analyzing the average cost of kimchi in different districts of Seoul to find the most economical choice.

## Proofs in Geometry
This topic focuses on constructing formal geometric proofs. Students learn to write logical, step-by-step arguments using previously established definitions, postulates, and theorems. They practice proving geometric statements about triangles, such as the Pythagorean theorem, and properties of parallel lines and transversals. Mastery of this deductive reasoning is crucial for achieving a high score in the CSAT, which heavily features proof-based questions.

## Rational Expressions and Equations
Students learn to simplify, add, subtract, multiply, and divide rational expressions, which are fractions containing polynomials. They solve rational equations by finding a common denominator and eliminating extraneous roots. A key application involves calculating the average speed of a train traveling between Seoul and Busan or determining the mixing ratios for ingredients in a large batch of kimchi, ensuring precise mathematical accuracy for the final product.
