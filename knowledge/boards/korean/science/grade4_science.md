# South Korean (NCCE) Grade 4 Science

## Living Things and Their Habitats
Living things have basic needs for survival, including food, water, air, and a suitable environment. Plants make their own food through a process called photosynthesis, using sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide. Animals depend on other living things for food. A habitat is the natural place where a plant or animal lives and finds its needs. For example, a frog's habitat might be a rice paddy (논) in the countryside, providing water for laying eggs and insects for food. Different habitats, such as forests or oceans, support different kinds of life.

## The Properties of Matter
Matter is anything that has mass and takes up space. It can exist as a solid, liquid, or gas. Solids have a definite shape and volume, like a traditional Korean rock (석재) statue. Liquids have a definite volume but take the shape of their container, similar to how soup is served in a bowl. Gases have no definite shape or volume and fill their container, much like the steam rising from a hot bowl of kimchi-jjigae. The state of matter can change, for example, when water freezes into ice or boils into steam.

## Forces and Motion
A force is a push or a pull that can change the motion of an object. Forces can make objects start moving, stop moving, or change direction. Gravity is a force that pulls objects toward the Earth. Friction is a force that opposes motion between surfaces. For example, when you slide a book across a desk, friction between the book and desk slows it down. Students can explore forces by observing how a toy car moves on different surfaces, like the smooth floor of a Seoul subway station versus a rougher surface.

## Light and Sound
Light travels in a straight line and can be reflected off surfaces, allowing us to see objects. A mirror is an example of a smooth surface that produces a clear reflection. Sound is a form of energy that travels in waves through a medium like air. Sound needs a medium to travel and cannot travel through a vacuum. Pitch is how high or low a sound is. For instance, a small bell makes a high-pitched sound, while a large gong makes a low-pitched sound, as heard in traditional Korean music.

## Earth and Sky
The Earth is a sphere that rotates on its axis, causing day and night. It also revolves around the Sun. The Moon revolves around the Earth and appears to change shape in the night sky, creating the lunar cycle we see from places like Seoul. Weather is the condition of the atmosphere at a certain time and place, including temperature, precipitation, and wind. For example, South Korea experiences four distinct seasons, with cold, dry winters and hot, humid summers perfect for making kimchi for storage.

## Electricity and Magnetism
Electricity is a form of energy that can produce light, heat, sound, and motion. It flows through materials called conductors, like copper wires, but not through insulators, like plastic. A simple circuit requires a power source, wires, and a device to use the electricity. Magnets have two poles, north and south, and can attract certain metals like iron. Magnets can also exert a force without touching an object. For example, you can use a magnet to separate magnetic materials from a mixture, a principle used in some recycling centers.
