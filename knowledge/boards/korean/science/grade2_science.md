# South Korean (NCCE) Grade 2 Science

## Living Things and Their Environments
Students observe and classify animals and plants based on basic characteristics. They learn that living things need air, water, food, and a suitable place to live. Children identify common local animals like cats and dogs, and plants like pine trees and roses. They understand that animals move to find food and shelter, while plants grow towards sunlight. The concept of seasonal changes, such as cherry blossoms in spring and persimmons in autumn, is introduced as a factor affecting living things.

## Materials and Their Properties
This topic focuses on identifying and grouping materials based on their properties, such as hardness, flexibility, and transparency. Students explore everyday objects made from wood, metal, plastic, and glass. They learn that some materials, like metal spoons, are hard and conduct heat, while others, like rubber bands, are stretchy. Simple experiments may involve testing whether objects float or sink in water. The cost of materials is introduced using the South Korean currency, the won.

## Changes in States of Matter
Students explore the three states of matter: solid, liquid, and gas. They observe how ice (solid) melts into water (liquid) when heated and how water turns into steam (gas) when boiled. Through experiments, they learn that heat causes changes in state. Examples include seeing steam rise from a hot bowl of kimchi soup or frost forming on a cold window. Children understand that these changes are reversible, like melting and freezing water.

## Our Earth and Sky
Learners identify the basic components of Earth, including land, water, and air. They observe and describe the daily appearance of the Sun and Moon. Students learn about the four seasons in South Korea—spring, summer, autumn, and winter—and associate each with weather patterns, such as hot summers and snowy winters. They recognize major geographical features, like the Han River in Seoul, and understand that the Sun provides light and warmth for living things on Earth.

## Sound and Light
Students investigate how sounds are made and how they travel. They identify that sounds come from vibrating objects and can be loud or soft. For light, they explore how it allows us to see and travels in straight lines. Children create simple shadows and learn that the Sun is a natural source of light. Examples include hearing a traditional drum or seeing the bright lights of a Seoul shopping district at night. They understand that sound can be blocked, while light makes objects visible.

## Health and Our Bodies
This topic teaches students about the basic parts and functions of their bodies, such as the senses, skeleton, and muscles. They learn the importance of good hygiene, like washing hands before meals. Healthy habits are emphasized, including eating nutritious foods like rice and kimchi, getting enough sleep, and exercising. The role of doctors and hospitals is introduced, helping children understand how to take care of their bodies to stay strong and healthy, like wearing a hanbok during a cultural festival.
