# South Korean (NCCE) Grade 3 Science

## The Properties of Water
Water is a liquid that is essential for life. It has three main states: solid (ice), liquid (water), and gas (water vapor). Water changes between these states through the processes of melting, freezing, evaporation, and condensation. For example, when ice melts, it turns into liquid water. The boiling point of water is 100 degrees Celsius, which is a high temperature compared to other liquids like alcohol. In South Korea, we use clean tap water for drinking and cooking.

## Understanding Magnets
Magnets are objects that create a magnetic field, which can push or pull on other magnetic objects. Every magnet has two poles: a north pole and a south pole. Like poles (north-north or south-south) repel each other, while opposite poles (north-south) attract. Some common magnetic materials are iron, nickel, and cobalt. You can find magnets in many household items, such as refrigerator doors. The Earth itself is a giant magnet with a magnetic north and south pole.

## The Human Body and Senses
Our body has many parts that work together to keep us healthy. We use our five senses to understand the world around us. Our eyes help us see, our ears help us hear, our nose helps us smell, our tongue helps us taste, and our skin helps us feel things like temperature and touch. Food provides the energy our body needs to grow and work. For instance, eating nutritious meals like rice, kimchi, and vegetables helps us stay strong and active.

## Living Things and Their Environments
Living things, or organisms, need certain things to survive, such as food, water, air, and a suitable place to live. Plants and animals are the main types of living things. Plants make their own food through a process called photosynthesis, using sunlight. Animals get their food by eating plants or other animals. For example, a butterfly lives in a garden environment and gets nectar from flowers, showing how living things depend on their surroundings.

## Light and Shadows
Light is a form of energy that travels in straight lines. When an object blocks light, it creates a shadow. The size of a shadow depends on the object's distance from the light source and the screen where the shadow appears. If the object is closer to the light, the shadow is larger. If it is farther away, the shadow is smaller. A sundial is an old tool that uses the sun's light and shadows to tell time.

## Weather and Seasons
Weather describes the conditions of the atmosphere, like temperature, rain, wind, and sun. In South Korea, there are four distinct seasons: spring, summer, autumn, and winter. Summer is hot and rainy, while winter is cold and sometimes snowy. We use tools like a thermometer to measure temperature. For example, the temperature in Seoul might be 5°C in winter and 30°C in summer, showing how the weather changes with the seasons.
