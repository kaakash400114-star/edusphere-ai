# Finnish (FNBE) Grade 8 English
## Social Interaction and Communication
Students learn to navigate formal and informal social situations, discussing topics such as school projects, hobbies, and Finnish traditions. They practise active listening and expressing opinions respectfully. Key phrases include "I agree with you, but..." and "Could you clarify that, please?" Activities involve role-playing scenarios like planning a sauna evening with friends or organizing a group trip to see the Northern Lights, focusing on clear, polite communication to build confidence in various social contexts.

## Media Literacy and Critical Analysis
Students critically evaluate different media sources, identifying bias, propaganda, and factual accuracy. They learn to distinguish between news reports, advertisements, and opinion pieces. Lessons use examples from Finnish media, analyzing articles about local events or global issues like climate change. Students create their own media products, such as a short video report on a school event or a blog post reviewing a Moomins film, ensuring information is sourced and presented responsibly.

## Creative Writing and Storytelling
Students develop creative writing skills by crafting narratives, poems, and descriptive texts. They explore literary devices like metaphors and personification, using examples from Finnish literature. Assignments include writing a short story inspired by a Finnish myth or a descriptive piece about a winter landscape. Students also practise scriptwriting for a play or video, focusing on character development, dialogue, and setting, enhancing their ability to engage audiences with imaginative and structured content.

## Language Structures and Grammar
Students deepen their understanding of English grammar, focusing on complex sentence structures, verb tenses (including perfect and continuous), and conditional clauses. They analyse sentence errors in texts and practise correcting them. Exercises include transforming simple sentences into more complex ones and using conditional language to discuss hypothetical situations, such as "If I were to see the Northern Lights, I would take a photo," ensuring accuracy and fluency in written and spoken English.

## Cultural Studies and Global Awareness
Students explore cultural similarities and differences between Finland and English-speaking countries, comparing traditions, festivals, and daily life. They research topics like Finnish sauna culture, the Moomins, or Finnish design, presenting their findings in reports or presentations. Discussions include global issues such as sustainability and multiculturalism, fostering an appreciation for diverse perspectives and encouraging students to connect local Finnish examples to broader international contexts.

## Research and Project Skills
Students learn to conduct research using digital and library resources, evaluating sources for relevance and credibility. They practise note-taking and summarising information, using a topic like the history of the Euro in Finland as a case study. Projects include creating a presentation, poster, or short documentary on a chosen subject, requiring students to structure their work logically, cite sources correctly, and present their findings clearly to an audience.
