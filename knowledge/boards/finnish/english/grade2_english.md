# Finnish (FNBE) Grade 2 English

## Basic Daily Routines and Activities
Students learn vocabulary for daily routines using simple present tense. They will identify and use words like 'wake up', 'eat breakfast', 'go to school', 'play outside', and 'have dinner'. They practice forming questions with 'Do you...?' and answers with 'Yes, I do' or 'No, I don't'. For example, they ask 'Do you play outside?' and answer based on their day. Activities include creating a picture diary of their weekend, using flashcards for actions, and role-playing simple dialogues about their daily lives.

## Describing People and Family
This topic focuses on simple adjectives to describe people and basic family vocabulary. Students learn words such as 'tall', 'short', 'happy', 'kind', 'mother', 'father', 'sister', and 'brother'. They practice using the verb 'to be' in sentences like 'My father is tall'. A key activity involves drawing their family and writing three simple descriptive sentences. They also play games to match adjectives to pictures of people, building a foundational understanding for character descriptions in stories.

## Simple Toys and Games
Students learn common toy and game vocabulary. Words like 'ball', 'doll', 'car', 'puzzle', 'hide-and-seek', and 'tag' are introduced. They practice asking and answering 'What is this?' and 'Do you have a...?'. A practical example is creating a poster of their favourite toys and writing a sentence for each one, such as 'I have a red car'. Lessons include sorting toys into categories and playing simple games to reinforce new vocabulary through active, hands-on play.

## Nature and Weather in Finland
This unit covers nature and weather vocabulary relevant to the Finnish climate. Students learn words for 'snow', 'rain', 'sun', 'wind', 'lake', 'forest', and 'Northern Lights'. They practice using simple phrases like 'It is sunny' or 'I see snow'. A key task is matching weather icons to corresponding words. Students also learn a simple chant about the seasons, using examples from their local environment, like describing a snowy winter day or a bright summer 'kesä'.

## Food and Eating Habits
Students expand their vocabulary related to food and meals. They learn food names like 'bread', 'milk', 'berries', 'porridge', 'potato', and 'fish'. They practice using the verb 'like' to form sentences, for example, 'I like berries' or 'He likes porridge'. An activity involves sorting pictures of food into breakfast, lunch, and dinner categories. They also learn about Finnish foods, such as 'mämmi' at Easter, and practice simple dialogues about what they eat for different meals.

## Moomin Characters and Stories
Students are introduced to the beloved Moomin characters from Finnish literature. They learn character names like 'Moomintroll', 'Snorkmaiden', 'Little My', and 'Snufkin'. They practice describing characters using simple adjectives ('Moomintroll is friendly'). A key activity is listening to a short Moomin story and identifying the characters and setting. Students then draw their favourite character and write a simple sentence to describe them, connecting language learning with a significant piece of Finnish culture.
