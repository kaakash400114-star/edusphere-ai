# Finnish (FNBE) Grade 4 English

## Greetings and Introductions
Students learn to introduce themselves and others using phrases like "My name is..." and "This is...". They practice formal and informal greetings, such as "Hello, how are you?" and "Hei, miten menee?". They learn to ask about origin using "Where are you from?" and answer with "I come from..." or "Olen kotoisin...". Example dialogues involve exchanging names, ages, and places, using Finnish cities like Helsinki or Tampere. They also learn to say goodbye politely with phrases like "Goodbye!" or "Näkemiin!".

## Daily Activities and Routines
Students learn to describe their daily routines using phrases like "I get up at seven o'clock" and "I eat breakfast at eight o'clock". They use verbs such as "wake up", "eat", "go to school", and "do homework". They practice asking classmates about their routines with questions like "What time do you go to bed?". Example sentences include "Minä menen kouluun kello kahdeksan" and "Käyn saunaa illalla". They create simple timetables using a 12-hour clock format.

## Describing People and Places
Students learn vocabulary to describe people's appearance and clothing, using words like "tall", "short", "long hair", and "blue eyes". They describe places such as homes, schools, and famous Finnish landmarks like the Moominvalley or Helsinki Cathedral. They practice using adjectives and prepositions, for example, "The red house is next to the lake". They write short descriptions of family members or their favourite room, incorporating specific details like "a big window" or "many books".

## Hobbies and Free Time Activities
Students learn to talk about hobbies using the present tense phrases “Pidän [verbistä]” and “Minun harrastukseni on [noun]”, supported by vocabulary for activities like uida, lukea, maalata and kuunnella musiikkia. They practice asking classmates “Mitä teet viikonloppuisin?” to initiate conversations about free time. The construction of simple past tense sentences, for example “Pelasin jalkapalloa lauantaina” or “Loin maalauksen”, is taught to describe recent activities. The learning process culminates in a project where students create posters visually and textually presenting their favourite leisure activities, demonstrating their ability to use key language structures in context.

## Food and Shopping
Students learn vocabulary for common foods and drinks, such as "bread", "milk", "berries", and "riesling". They practice ordering food in a simple café using phrases like "Can I have a coffee, please?". They discuss Finnish foods like rye bread, salmon, and cloudberry jam. They use euros for simple price comparisons, for example, "An apple costs one euro fifty". They write shopping lists and role-play dialogues at a market, asking "How much does this cost?".

## Nature and Seasons in Finland
Students learn vocabulary for seasons, weather, and nature, such as "spring", "summer", "autumn", "winter", "sunny", "snowy", "forest", and "lake". They describe seasonal activities like "swimming in summer" or "building a snowman in winter". They discuss natural phenomena such as the Northern Lights ("revontulet") and midnight sun. Example sentences include "Talvella on pakkasta ja lumentulo" and "Kesällä on aurinkoista ja lämmin". They create drawings or posters showing different Finnish seasons and weather.
