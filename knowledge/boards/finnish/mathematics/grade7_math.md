# Finnish (FNBE) Grade 7 Mathematics  
## Proportional Relationships and Percentages  
Students explore proportional relationships and their applications using real-world contexts. They learn to solve problems involving direct proportionality, such as calculating ingredient amounts for a traditional pulla recipe. Percentages are applied to practical situations like calculating VAT (24%) on a new sauna stove purchase or determining discounts on Moomin-themed merchandise. The concept of interest is introduced, with exercises involving savings accounts in euros. Students develop skills to convert between fractions, decimals, and percentages, and to solve problems involving percentage change, such as population growth in Northern Lights viewing areas.  

## Equations and Inequalities  
In this topic, students learn to formulate and solve simple linear equations and inequalities with one variable. They apply methods like balancing both sides of an equation, using inverse operations. Real-world examples include solving for the cost of a cinema ticket or determining the time needed to save for a trip. Inequalities are used to model constraints, such as the minimum temperature required to see the Northern Lights. Students practice translating word problems into mathematical expressions and understand that a solution to an inequality can be a range of values, reflecting real-life conditions like acceptable sauna temperatures.  

## Geometry: Angles, Circles, and Solids  
This unit focuses on geometric properties and calculations related to angles, circles, and three-dimensional solids. Students calculate the area and circumference of circles, applying formulas with π, such as designing a circular ice-skating rink. They study the properties of prisms and pyramids, calculating their surface area and volume, which relates to designing a sauna cabin. Angle relationships, such as those in triangles and intersecting lines, are analyzed. The Pythagorean theorem is introduced to solve problems involving right-angled triangles, like determining the diagonal distance across a Moominvalley map.  

## Statistics and Probability  
Students learn to collect, organize, and interpret data using statistical methods. They calculate measures of central tendency, such as the mean and median, for datasets like daily temperatures during winter or visitor numbers to the Arctic Circle. Probability concepts are introduced through experiments and simulations, such as calculating the likelihood of observing the Northern Lights on a given night. Students create and interpret bar graphs, pie charts, and histograms to represent data, like the distribution of favorite Moomin characters among their peers. The unit emphasizes how statistics are used to make informed decisions in daily life.  

## Functions and Their Graphs  
This topic introduces the concept of a function as a relationship between two quantities. Students learn to represent functions using tables, graphs, and algebraic rules. They study linear functions, plotting lines on a coordinate plane to model scenarios like the cost of a sauna rental over time. Real-world examples include tracking the growth of a savings account in euros or the distance traveled to see the Northern Lights. Students interpret graphs to understand rates of change and trends, such as the increasing popularity of Moomin-themed products. The focus is on recognizing patterns and using functions to describe and predict real-world phenomena.  

## Financial Literacy  
Students develop practical financial skills, focusing on budgeting, saving, and understanding interest. They calculate simple and compound interest for savings accounts, seeing how their euros grow over time. The unit covers budgeting for expenses like a sauna night out or a Moominvalley trip, distinguishing between needs and wants. Students learn about taxes, deductions, and net pay, using examples from the Finnish system. They also explore currency exchange rates for international travel, such as converting euros to other currencies. The goal is to foster responsible financial decision-making, emphasizing long-term planning and understanding the value of money.
