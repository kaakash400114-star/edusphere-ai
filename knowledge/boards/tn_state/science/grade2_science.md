# Tamil Nadu State Board Class 2 Science

## Food groups and health

Food is divided into different groups: energy-giving food like rice and chapati, body-building food like milk, eggs, and dal, and protective food like fruits and vegetables. Each food group gives our body special things to keep us strong and healthy. Energy food gives us power to play and work, body food helps us grow tall and big, and protective food keeps us from getting sick. We must eat a little food from each group every day to stay well and grow up healthy. Eating different coloured foods, like green spinach and red carrots, helps us get all the good things our body needs.

## Body and movement

Our body helps us to move and do many things. We learn about different body parts like hands, legs, head, and feet, and how we use them. For example, we use our legs to walk and run, and our hands to eat and play. Our body also helps us to feel things like soft and hard with our fingers. Every body part is special and helps us in different ways to live and play.

## Animal homes and young ones

Animals live in different homes to stay safe and protected. Homes like nests, burrows, and holes protect them from danger and bad weather. Many animals take good care of their young ones by bringing them food and keeping them warm. For example, birds build nests for their chicks, and dogs have puppies. Mothers feed their babies and teach them how to find food. All baby animals grow into big animals with the help of their parents.

## Plant types and uses

Plants are living things that grow in soil and need sunlight and water to live. Some plants are small and have soft stems, like grass in a garden, while other plants are big and have hard, woody stems, like trees in a park. Plants give us many useful things, such as fruits like mangoes and bananas to eat, vegetables like carrots and spinach for cooking, and flowers like jasmine and marigold that are nice to smell. Trees also give us wood to make furniture and paper, and their leaves give us shade to play under. Different plants grow in different places, like cacti that grow in hot, dry places and water lilies that grow in ponds.

## Water: sources and safety

Water comes from different places like rivers, ponds, taps, and rain. We get drinking water from taps, but we must boil it or clean it with a cloth before drinking to stay healthy and not get sick. We must not put dirty things like leaves or plastic in our drinking water. Water can be dirty and unsafe if animals bathe in it, so we always use safe and clean water. Saving water is very important because it is useful for drinking, bathing, and cleaning our home.

Water is found in many places like rivers, lakes, and ponds. We also get water from taps and wells at home. Water is important for drinking, cooking, and keeping our body clean. We must drink clean and safe water to stay healthy and not fall sick. Always wash your hands with clean water before eating food after playing.

## Air around us

Air is all around us, even though we cannot see it. We need air to live, just like we need food and water. When we blow air from our mouths, we can feel it moving. Wind is moving air, and it can help dry clothes or make trees and leaves sway. Air can move things like a paper boat on water or a kite in the sky. Some animals like fish live in water, and they breathe the air that is dissolved in the water to live.

## Seasons of Tamil Nadu: monsoon

Tamil Nadu has different seasons, and one important season is the monsoon. The monsoon brings heavy rains that fill the rivers, ponds, and lakes with water. These rains help the farmers grow crops like rice and make the land green with many plants. Sometimes the monsoon causes strong winds, and children should stay safe indoors when it rains heavily. The cool air after the rain makes the weather pleasant, and we see many beautiful flowers blooming everywhere.

## Safety and first aid

Children learn to recognise common dangers at home and school, like fire, hot objects, and sharp tools. They understand simple rules to stay safe, such as not playing with matches, touching hot stoves, or running with scissors. For first aid, they learn to tell an adult immediately if they get a small cut, and that bleeding should be cleaned gently with water. They also know not to put any strange things in their mouth or touch electrical wires with wet hands. These basic rules help prevent accidents and keep them healthy.

## Our neighbourhood

In Class 2 Science, we learn about our neighbourhood and the people who help us. We identify important places like the school, park, market, and clinic, and understand their uses. We also learn about community helpers such as the postman, policeman, and doctor, and how they help us every day. The children discuss the rules to follow in public places, like waiting in a queue and not littering. Simple examples of safe behaviour, such as looking both ways before crossing the road, are taught to ensure a happy and secure neighbourhood.

## Festivals and clean surroundings

Festivals and clean surroundings teach us to keep our homes and schools neat. We learn to throw trash in dustbins and not litter the ground. During festivals like Diwali, we make colourful decorations and use natural things like flowers and leaves. We should not burst loud firecrackers that make smoke and noise. Keeping our surroundings clean helps us stay healthy and happy. We wash our hands before eating and after playing outside to stay clean.

We learn about different festivals like Pongal and Diwali. We celebrate these festivals by cleaning our homes and classrooms. Clean surroundings help us stay healthy and happy. We do not throw waste on the ground; we use dustbins. We also learn to keep our parks and streets clean for everyone.
