# Tamil Nadu State Board Class 1 Science

## Living and non-living

Living things are also called organisms and they need air, water, food, and shelter to live. Non-living things like stones, books, and toys do not need air, water, or food to stay as they are. We can find living things such as plants, animals, and insects around us. We can find non-living things in our classroom and home like the blackboard, chair, and pencil. Living things can grow and move, but non-living things cannot grow or move on their own.

## Our body and sense organs

We learn about our body and its five sense organs: eyes, ears, nose, tongue, and skin. Our eyes help us see things like a ball or a book, our ears help us hear sounds like a bell, our nose helps us smell flowers, and our tongue helps us taste food like sweet candy. Our skin helps us feel things like soft fur or hard stone, and it also tells us if something is hot or cold. We must always take care of our sense organs to stay healthy and safe.

## Animals around us

Animals are living beings that need food, water, and air to live. They are found in different places like the forest, water, and our homes. Animals have different body parts like tails, legs, and wings to help them move and eat. Some animals give us milk and eggs, which are good for our health. We must be kind to all animals and take care of them.

## Plants around us

Plants grow in different places like our homes, gardens, and forests. They have roots, a stem, leaves, and sometimes flowers, each with a job to do. The roots hold the plant in the ground and drink water, the stem carries water to all parts, and leaves make food for the plant using sunlight. Some plants give us fruits and vegetables that we eat, like mangoes and carrots, and some give us flowers that make our surroundings beautiful. Plants also help us by giving us fresh air to breathe and keeping the environment clean.

## Food and water

Food is what we eat to grow and stay strong. We eat rice, dal, vegetables, fruits, and chapati to get energy. Water is very important to drink every day to keep our bodies healthy. We get food from plants like mangoes and from animals like milk and eggs. We must always wash our hands with soap before eating food to stay clean and avoid germs.

Food gives us energy to work and play. Water keeps our bodies clean and healthy. We drink water when we are thirsty and eat food like rice, vegetables, and fruits to grow. Clean water helps us stay safe from sickness, and washing hands with soap before eating keeps germs away. Healthy food makes our strong.

## Clothes we wear

We wear clothes to protect our body from heat, cold, rain, and dust. Different clothes are worn for different seasons, like woollen clothes in winter and cotton clothes in summer. We also wear clothes to keep our body clean and covered. Some special clothes are worn for festivals and functions, like new dresses or traditional attire. Clothes come in many colours, designs, and sizes to suit everyone.

## Home and school

We learn about our home and school as two safe places. Our home has rooms like the bedroom and kitchen where we live with our family. Our school has classrooms, a playground, and a library where we learn with our teachers and friends. We must follow rules like keeping our classrooms clean and speaking softly in the library. Our teacher shows us how to take care of our belongings at home and in school.

## Air, water, weather

We learn about the air all around us that helps us and plants live. Water is very important for drinking, bathing, and keeping ourselves clean. We see different types of weather like sunny, rainy, windy, and cloudy days. Rain helps plants grow and gives us water to drink. The sun makes us feel warm and helps dry our wet clothes after rain.

## Safety and health

Children learn about simple rules to stay safe and healthy at school and home. They must wash their hands with soap and water before eating and after playing outside to keep germs away. It is important to walk carefully inside the classroom and not run on stairs to avoid accidents. We should not put dirty things or fingers in our mouth, as they can make us sick. Drinking clean water and eating fresh fruits helps our bodies grow strong and keeps us healthy.

## Helpers and transport

We learn about different people who help us in our daily life, like doctors, teachers, and police officers. We also learn about the vehicles they use to help us, such as ambulances, school buses, and police vans. Children understand that different vehicles are used for different jobs, like a fire engine for putting out fires. We learn to name these helpers and their vehicles correctly. We also know that we must follow simple rules to keep ourselves safe, like sitting quietly in a bus and crossing the road carefully.
