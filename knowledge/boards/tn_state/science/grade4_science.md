# Tamil Nadu State Board Class 4 Science

## Green plants make food

Green plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and air. This process is called photosynthesis. The green leaves have tiny parts called chlorophyll that help them catch sunlight. Plants take in water from the soil through their roots and air through tiny holes in their leaves. They use these things to make food for themselves and give out oxygen which all living beings need to breathe. For example, a mango tree uses sunlight, water, and air to make its own food and grow big and strong.

## Plant and animal adaptations

Plants and animals have special features to live in their homes. Desert plants like cactus have thick stems to store water and sharp spines to protect themselves. Animals like the camel have humps to store fat and thick fur to keep them warm in the cold desert nights. Birds like the peacock have bright feathers to attract mates and strong legs to walk on the ground. These special features help plants and animals find food, protect themselves, and survive in their surroundings.

## Teeth and digestion

Teeth are hard, white parts in our mouth that help us bite and chew food. We have different types of teeth like incisors for biting, canines for tearing, and molars for grinding. Food is broken down into small pieces by chewing and mixed with saliva in our mouth to make it soft. This soft food then goes to our stomach where it is digested to give us energy. Good food like fruits, vegetables, and milk help keep our teeth strong and our body healthy.

## Safety-first aid-cleanliness

We learn about staying safe and keeping ourselves clean. Always wash your hands with soap before eating food and after using the toilet. For small cuts, clean the area gently with water and cover it with a clean bandage. Never touch or play with sharp objects like knives or scissors, and tell an adult if you get hurt. Keep your home and school clean by not littering and putting trash in the dustbin. Following these rules helps us stay healthy and safe.

## Air-water pollution basics

Air is all around us and keeps us alive, but dirty smoke from factories and vehicles makes it polluted. Water pollution happens when people throw garbage or chemicals into rivers and lakes, making the water dirty. Harmful things like plastic bags and soap get mixed in water, and animals and fish cannot live in it. We must not burn waste or throw waste in water to keep our environment clean. Dirty air and water can make people and animals sick, so we all help to keep them pure.

## Sun-earth-moon

The Sun is a big, bright star that gives us light and heat. The Earth is a round planet that moves around the Sun, and it also spins on its own, which causes day and night. The Moon is Earth’s small, natural satellite that moves around the Earth, and we see its different shapes called phases, like the new moon and full moon. We experience day when our part of the Earth faces the Sun and night when it is away from the Sun. The Moon does not make its own light; it shines because it reflects the light from the Sun.

## Food and nutrients

Food is what gives us energy to play and grow. There are different kinds of food, like rice, dal, vegetables, and fruits. Our body needs all these foods to stay healthy and strong. Some foods give us energy, like rice and bread. Some help us grow, like milk and eggs. Fruits and vegetables protect us from getting sick. We must eat different kinds of food every day to get all the nutrients we need.

## Force and simple machines

Force is a push or pull that makes things move or stop moving. Simple machines help us to do work with less effort; the six types are lever, pulley, wheel and axle, inclined plane, screw, and wedge. A lever, like a see-saw or a nutcracker, helps us lift heavy things by using a pivot point. An inclined plane, like a ramp or a staircase, makes it easier to carry things up or down. Pulleys are used to lift heavy loads, like buckets from a well, by changing the direction of the force.

## Our state's resources

Our state has many useful things called resources. Some resources are natural things like trees, water, and rocks that we get from nature. We also have man-made resources like buildings, roads, and toys made by people. It is important to use our resources carefully so they do not finish. For example, we must save water by not letting it run waste. We also grow new trees when we cut old ones to have enough wood for the future. Using resources wisely helps everyone in our state.

## Water saving

Water is a very important resource for all living things. We must save water because it is not always available. We can save water by turning off taps tightly after use. Fixing leaking taps helps stop water wastage. Using a bucket instead of a pipe to wash the car saves water. Taking short showers also helps in saving water. Everyone should try to use water carefully so that we always have enough for drinking, cooking, and other needs.
