# Tamil Nadu State Board Class 3 Science

## From farm to plate: our food

We learn about the journey of our food from where it grows to our plates. Fruits and vegetables come from plants, such as mangoes from trees and carrots from the soil. Rice, wheat, and dal grow in farms and are cooked to make our meals like idli and dal. We must wash fruits and vegetables well before eating them to keep germs away. It is important to eat different healthy foods to grow big and strong.

## Plant parts and jobs

Plants have many parts that help them grow and stay healthy. The roots hold the plant in the soil and take in water from the ground. The stem carries the water to all parts of the plant, like a pipe, and holds the leaves up to the sun. Leaves are green and make food for the plant using sunlight, air, and water. Flowers are colourful and make seeds, which grow into new plants. Some plants have fruits that are sweet and yummy for us to eat, like bananas and mangoes.

## Animals: food and coverings

Animals need food to live and grow. Some animals eat plants and are called herbivores, like cows and goats. Other animals eat other animals and are called carnivores, like lions and tigers. Animals have different coverings on their bodies to protect themselves. A dog has fur to keep it warm, while a tortoise has a hard shell for safety. Some animals, like frogs, have smooth skin. These coverings help them stay safe from harm and the weather.

## Birds: beaks and nests

Birds have different types of beaks to help them eat food. Birds use their strong beaks to eat seeds, worms, or fruits. Some birds make nests with twigs, leaves, and mud to lay their eggs. The nests are safe places for baby birds to stay warm and grow. Nests can be found in trees, on buildings, or even on the ground.

## Bones and muscles

Our bodies have strong bones inside us that help us stand and move. Muscles are soft and strong parts that help us bend our arms and legs. We must eat healthy food like milk and vegetables to keep our bones and muscles strong. When we run and play, our muscles work hard to help us move. Some bones protect our important body parts, like the skull that protects our brain.

## Sense organs

We learn about our five sense organs: eyes, ears, nose, tongue, and skin. The eyes help us see colours and shapes, like a red ball or a green tree. The ears let us hear sounds, such as a bell ringing or a dog barking. The nose helps us smell nice things, like flowers or food cooking. The tongue lets us taste sweet, salty, sour, and bitter things, like candy, chips, a lemon, or chocolate. Our skin helps us feel things, like soft fur or hard stones, and tells us if something is hot or cold.

## Water cycle basics

Water changes its form, like turning from liquid into gas when it gets hot. This gas is called water vapour and it goes up to the sky. In the sky, the water vapour becomes cold and turns back into tiny drops of water. These drops come together to make clouds. When the clouds get heavy, the rain falls down on the earth. The rain water goes back to rivers, lakes and ponds. This way, water keeps moving from the land to the sky and back again. This is called the water cycle.

## Air and weather

Air is all around us, but we cannot see it. It helps us to breathe, just like we need food and water to live. Air moves and we call this moving air wind. Wind makes things like trees and flags move. Wind can also help dry clothes faster. When we feel hot, the wind feels cool and nice. Sometimes, we see clouds in the sky and later it may rain. Rain gives water to the plants and helps them grow. Different types of weather make our world a beautiful and interesting place.

## Earth, day-night

The Earth is a big, round planet that we live on. The Sun is a bright star that gives us light and heat. The Earth spins around itself like a spinning top, which takes one whole day. When our side of the Earth faces the Sun, it is day time. When the other side faces away from the Sun, it is night time. This is why we have day and night every day.

## Caring for environment

We learn about keeping our environment clean by not littering plastic bags and banana peels. We understand that throwing waste in dustbins helps our home and school stay neat and beautiful. We plant small trees and water them every day so they grow strong and give us fresh air. We are taught to turn off taps tightly to save water, just like turning off a toy after playing. We do not harm animals and birds because they are our friends and live in our environment.
