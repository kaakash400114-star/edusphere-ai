# Tamil Nadu State Board Class 5 Science

## Plant and animal interdependence

Plants and animals depend on each other for their survival. Plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and air, and they give us oxygen to breathe. Animals help plants by spreading their seeds through their waste, and some animals help plants grow by moving pollen from one flower to another. When animals eat fruits, they help new plants grow by dropping the seeds in new places. Bees and butterflies are important friends of plants because they help them make new seeds. Without each other, plants and animals cannot live happily on the earth.

## Seed germination and dispersal

Seeds are tiny baby plants that need water, air, and warmth to grow into big plants. This process is called germination, and the first thing that grows is a tiny root, followed by a small shoot that pushes up towards the sunlight. Seeds spread to different places through different methods like wind, water, and animals, so that new plants can grow far away from the parent plant. For example, the seeds of the cotton plant have soft, fluffy fibres that help them float on the wind to new places, while the seeds of the lotus plant are carried away by the water in ponds and lakes.

## Water conservation

Water is a precious resource that we must save every day. We learn to turn off the tap tightly when not using water, like while brushing teeth or washing hands. Rainwater can be collected in big pots or tanks during the rainy season to use later for plants and cleaning. Taking short baths and using a bucket instead of a shower helps save lots of water. Fixing leaky taps and sharing water-saving tips with family are simple ways to protect this important resource for everyone.

## Simple experiments with water

In Class 5 Science, children learn to perform simple experiments with water to understand its properties. They observe how water flows in different directions and can change shape to fit containers. They also learn about solubility by seeing which materials like salt and sugar dissolve in water, and which like stones do not. By testing objects, they discover that some things float while others sink in water. These experiments help children understand that water is essential for life and can be found in three states: solid, liquid, and gas.

## Health and hygiene

We learn why keeping our bodies and surroundings clean is very important for good health. We must wash our hands with soap and water before eating and after using the toilet to stop germs from making us sick. Brushing our teeth twice a day, in the morning and at night, keeps our teeth clean and strong. We also learn that eating healthy food like fruits, vegetables, and dal gives us energy to study and play. It is necessary to cut our nails short and keep our hair tidy to stay clean and healthy.

## Crops and farmers of our state

Farmers in Tamil Nadu grow different crops like paddy, sugarcane, and maize depending on the weather and soil. Paddy needs a lot of water and is grown in fields called paddies, while sugarcane grows in warm, sunny places. Farmers use tools like ploughs and tractors to prepare the land and sow seeds. They water the crops and protect them from insects to help them grow well. Different seasons are good for different crops, so farmers plan when to plant and harvest them. This helps in getting food for everyone in the state.

## Simple machines

Simple machines are tools that help us do work with less effort. The six simple machines are the lever, pulley, wheel and axle, inclined plane, screw, and wedge. A lever has a rod and a fulcrum, like a seesaw, to lift heavy objects. Pulleys use ropes and wheels to pull things up, like a flag hoisting. An inclined plane is a sloped surface, like a ramp, that makes it easier to carry loads. Wheel and axle help in moving things smoothly, like the wheels of a bicycle. Screws and wedges help in fixing or splitting things, like a screw in a door or a knife cutting fruit.

## Solar system tour

The Sun is at the centre of our solar system and all the planets move around it in their own paths called orbits. There are eight main planets in our solar system: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. The four planets closest to the Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars, are made of rock and are called inner planets. The other four planets, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune, are very big and made of gas, so we call them outer planets. Earth has one moon that moves around it, just like Earth moves around the Sun.

## Energy saving

Energy means the ability to do work and we should save it to avoid wastage. We can save energy by switching off lights and fans when not in use. We should also turn off televisions and computers after watching to save electricity. Using a broom instead of a vacuum cleaner saves energy because it does not use electricity. Saving energy helps us use less electricity and keeps our homes and Earth healthy for everyone.

## Forests and wildlife

Forests are homes for many animals like tigers, elephants, and birds, which need trees, water, and space to live. We must protect forests by not cutting down too many trees and by not harming wild animals. The government has rules called wildlife sanctuaries and national parks to keep animals safe in their natural homes. Plants in forests give us fresh air, wood, and fruits, while animals help in spreading seeds and keeping the environment healthy. We should always remember that forests are important for us and all living things, so we must take care of them.
