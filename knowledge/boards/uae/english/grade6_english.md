# UAE Curriculum (MOE) Grade 6 English
## Reading Comprehension
Students will analyse a variety of literary and informational texts. They will identify explicit and implicit information, including main ideas, supporting details, and a writer's purpose. For example, they will read an article about the conservation of falcons in the UAE, explaining how the writer uses facts to persuade readers. Students will also infer character motivations from a short story set in a Dubai souk and use context to define unfamiliar vocabulary like 'souq' or 'dirham'.

## Grammar and Syntax
The focus will be on mastering complex sentence structures. Students will correctly use a range of subordinate clauses (e.g., 'although it was hot, they visited the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque') and punctuate them accurately. They will learn to identify and use the passive voice for objectivity, for example: 'The desert road was constructed by engineers from Abu Dhabi.' Furthermore, they will expand their knowledge of verb tenses, including the past perfect, to sequence events correctly in their writing.

## Vocabulary Development
Students will actively build their academic and domain-specific vocabulary. They will learn to analyse words by identifying prefixes, suffixes, and roots, such as in 'prehistoric' or 'archaeological'. They will also explore synonyms and antonyms to enhance precision in writing, for example, using 'arid' or 'scorching' as alternatives for 'hot'. Contextual learning is key, where new words are introduced through texts on topics like the UAE's maritime history or its desert ecosystem.

## Writing
Students will learn to write a range of formal and informal texts for specific purposes. They will plan, draft, and edit a persuasive essay on a topic like protecting local beaches, using arguments supported by evidence. They will also learn to compose formal letters of inquiry, such as asking a museum for information on cultural artefacts. Key skills include structuring an introduction, body, and conclusion, and ensuring appropriate tone and formality for each audience.

## Speaking and Listening
This unit develops formal oral presentation and interactive listening skills. Students will prepare and deliver a short, structured presentation on an aspect of UAE culture, such as the importance of 'wasta' or the design of a modern skyscraper. They will learn to speak clearly, use appropriate volume, and make eye contact. As listeners, they will practice asking and answering relevant questions to clarify understanding, demonstrating respect for the speaker's viewpoint in a classroom discussion.

## Study and Research Skills
Students will be taught to locate, select, and synthesise information from multiple reliable sources. They will use digital libraries and approved websites to research a topic, for example, the life of Sheikh Zayed. They will learn to distinguish between fact and opinion and to cite sources correctly using a simple format. This skill is applied to creating a short report, ensuring that all key information is gathered and organised logically before drafting the final piece of writing.
