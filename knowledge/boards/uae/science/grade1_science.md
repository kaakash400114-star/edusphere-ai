# UAE Curriculum (MOE) Grade 1 Science
## Living Things and Their Needs
Children learn that living things, like plants and animals including camels and falcons, need air, water, and food to survive. They observe how plants grow towards sunlight and their roots take in water from the soil. Animals, including pets in a home or animals in a zoo, need to drink water and eat food like dates or grass. Children learn that all living things need a safe place to live, such as a bird’s nest in a tree or a rabbit’s burrow in the desert.

## Materials and Their Properties
Children explore different materials like wood, plastic, metal, and fabric. They learn to describe materials by their properties, such as hard, soft, shiny, or rough. For example, a dirham coin is made of metal and feels hard and smooth, while a traditional ghutra is soft and can be folded. They sort objects, like toys or fruits in a souk, into groups based on what they are made of, understanding why a glass bottle is different from a plastic one.

## My Body and the Senses
Children identify the main parts of the body, including the head, arms, legs, and senses like sight, hearing, touch, smell, and taste. They learn that their eyes see, their ears hear, their hands touch to feel if sand is hot or cool, their nose smells like perfume or spices in a souk, and their tongue tastes sweet like honey or salty like food from the sea. They understand that the senses help us to understand and explore the world around us.

## Light and Dark
Children learn that we need light to see. They identify sources of light, such as the sun, lamps, and torches, and understand that the sun is a big source of light for the UAE. They observe how a shadow is made when an object blocks the light, like seeing their shadow on the ground on a bright sunny day at the beach. They learn that without light, as in a dark room, it is difficult to see and identify objects around them.

## Sound and Hearing
Children learn that sounds are made when things vibrate, like a string on a tambourine. They identify different sounds they hear every day, such as the call to prayer from a mosque, the horn of a car on the road, or the sound of water in a fountain. They learn that their ears are used for hearing and that sound can be loud, like a plane flying overhead, or soft, like a whisper in a library.

## The Weather and the Seasons
Children observe the daily weather in the UAE and learn to describe it using words like sunny, hot, cloudy, or windy. They learn that the weather can change, like a sudden sandstorm. They understand that the UAE has a hot summer and a milder winter. They discuss how people and animals adapt, by wearing light clothes and seeking shade in the summer, or by visiting places like the air-conditioned Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque when it is very hot outside.
