# UAE Curriculum (MOE) Grade 6 Science

## The Particle Theory of Matter
Matter is made up of tiny particles that are always moving. The way these particles are arranged and how strongly they attract each other determines whether a substance is a solid, liquid, or gas. In solids like a desert rock, particles are tightly packed in a fixed structure. In liquids, such as the briny water in the Arabian Gulf, particles are close but can move past one another, taking the shape of their container. Gases, like the hot air rising from a Dubai street, have particles that are far apart and move quickly and freely in all directions.

## Energy Forms and Transfer
Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transferred from one form to another. Common forms include light energy from the sun, which travels in straight lines at 300,000,000 metres per second, and electrical energy, which powers devices in a modern UAE home. Energy is often transferred as heat; for example, heat from a radiator warms the surrounding air through convection, or the hot sand of a UAE desert transfers thermal energy to your feet by conduction.

## Electricity and Magnetism
Electricity is the flow of electrical charge through a conductor, like copper wire. For a circuit to work, it must be a complete, closed loop containing a power source such as a battery. The strength of an electromagnet, which can be made by coiling wire around an iron nail, depends on the number of coils and the current flowing through it. These principles are used in everyday UAE life, from the magnetic stripes on a Dubai Metro card to the large electromagnets used in the country’s advanced recycling facilities.

## Forces and Motion
A force is a push or a pull that can change an object's motion. When unbalanced forces act on an object, like a camel starting to walk in the desert, it will accelerate. Friction is a force that opposes motion; for instance, the friction between a car’s tyres and the Sheikh Zayed Road allows it to grip the surface. Gravity is a force that pulls all objects towards the Earth’s centre, giving an object with a mass of 1 kilogram a weight of approximately 9.8 newtons.

## Classification of Living Things
All living organisms can be classified into groups based on shared characteristics. The main groups are animals, plants, fungi, and micro-organisms. Within the animal kingdom, vertebrates have a backbone, while invertebrates do not. In the UAE, the Arabian oryx is a mammal with a backbone, and the date palm is a flowering plant. This system of classification helps scientists understand the diversity of life in the UAE, from the coral reefs of the Gulf to the animals of the Liwa desert.

## Weather and Climate
The weather describes short-term atmospheric conditions, such as the temperature in Dubai which can reach 45°C in summer. Climate is the average weather pattern over a long period, and the UAE has a hot desert climate. The climate is influenced by factors such as its location near the Tropic of Cancer and the surrounding seas. Wind patterns, like the cool Shamal, and low annual rainfall of less than 100mm are key features that shape life in this Gulf region.
