# UAE Curriculum (MOE) Grade 11 Science

## Atomic Structure and the Periodic Table
Electrons occupy discrete energy levels or shells around the nucleus. The maximum number of electrons in a shell is given by 2n², where n is the shell number. For example, the first shell (n=1) holds a maximum of 2 electrons, while the second shell (n=2) holds 8. In the UAE's dry desert air, materials like silicon are crucial for electronics; its atomic number is 14, meaning it has 14 protons and 14 electrons in a neutral atom, with its electron configuration being 2, 8, 4.

## Chemical Bonding and Reactions
Chemical bonds form when atoms achieve a stable electron configuration, typically a full outer shell of eight electrons (the octet rule). Ionic bonding involves the complete transfer of electrons, as seen when sodium (Na) donates an electron to chlorine (Cl), forming NaCl, common in table salt used in UAE souks. Covalent bonding involves the sharing of electrons, such as in methane (CH₄), a gas found in natural reserves vital to the UAE's economy. A balanced chemical equation must have the same number of each type of atom on both sides.

## Motion and Forces
Newton's second law states that the acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on it and inversely proportional to its mass (F=ma). For example, a force of 20 newtons applied to a 2 kg object produces an acceleration of 10 m/s². Friction is a force that opposes motion, and in the UAE, this is critical for vehicle safety on hot desert highways. When multiple forces act on an object, the net force is their vector sum. Unbalanced forces cause a change in an object's velocity.

## Energy, Work, and Power
Work is done when a force causes an object to move (Work = Force × Distance). Its unit is the joule (J). Power is the rate at which work is done or energy is transferred (Power = Work / Time), measured in watts (W). A crane lifting a 500 kg steel beam 20 meters does 98,000 joules of work (using g = 9.8 m/s²). If this takes 10 seconds, the power output is 9,800 watts. This principle is applied in the UAE's rapidly growing construction sector and desalination plants, which consume vast amounts of energy to produce fresh water.

## Waves and Sound
A wave is a disturbance that transfers energy without transferring matter. The speed of a wave (v) is the product of its frequency (f) and wavelength (λ): v = f × λ. Sound is a mechanical wave that requires a medium; it travels faster in denser materials like steel (≈5000 m/s) than in air (≈340 m/s). In the extreme heat of the UAE, sound travels slightly faster. Acoustics in buildings like the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque are carefully designed using principles of reflection and absorption to create clear and reverberant sounds for prayer calls.

## Electricity and Magnetism
Ohm's Law states that the current (I) flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference (V) across it and inversely proportional to its resistance (R): I = V / R. For example, applying a 12-volt potential across a 4-ohm resistor results in a current of 3 amperes. The UAE's vast solar farms use photovoltaic cells to convert sunlight into electrical energy, which is then distributed to homes and businesses, where voltage is often 220-240 volts for standard appliances.
