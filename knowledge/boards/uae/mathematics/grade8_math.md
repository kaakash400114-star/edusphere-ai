# UAE Curriculum (MOE) Grade 8 Mathematics

## Algebraic Manipulation and Equations
Grade 8 students expand their algebraic skills to solve complex linear and quadratic equations. They learn to manipulate algebraic expressions, including expanding brackets using the distributive property and simplifying by combining like terms. Pupils solve equations with variables on both sides, apply the transposition method, and use factorisation to solve quadratic equations of the form x² + bx + c = 0. Word problems involving age, money, and distance are modelled and solved algebraically, ensuring students can apply these methods to real-life scenarios common in the UAE.

## Geometry and Angle Properties
Students explore the properties of 2D and 3D geometric shapes, focusing on angle relationships and congruence. They identify and calculate angles in parallel lines, including corresponding, alternate, and co-interior angles. The concept of similarity and congruence in triangles is covered using the criteria for congruent triangles (SSS, SAS, ASA). Real-world examples include calculating angles in the geometric patterns of the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and designing layouts for souks, where understanding symmetry and proportion is essential for architectural balance.

## Number Systems and Percentages
This unit delves into rational and irrational numbers, their properties, and operations on them. Students learn to convert between fractions, decimals, and percentages, with an emphasis on applying percentages to real-world contexts like calculating VAT, discounts in Dubai malls, and currency exchange rates involving Dirhams. Problems involving profit, loss, and simple interest are solved using percentage formulas, ensuring students can make informed financial decisions. The use of Dirhams and fils provides practical, regionally relevant application for these concepts.

## Data Handling and Probability
Grade 8 students learn to collect, organise, and interpret data using various methods, including surveys and experiments. They construct and interpret statistical diagrams such as bar charts, pie charts, and frequency tables, often using data related to UAE population or climate. The basics of probability are introduced, with students learning to calculate the probability of single events. Examples include finding the chance of rain in a desert climate or the probability of picking a specific type of date from a local souk, making abstract concepts tangible.

## Mensuration: Area and Volume
Students apply formulas to find the perimeter, area, and volume of various 2D and 3D shapes. They calculate the area of triangles, parallelograms, trapeziums, and circles, and the volume of prisms, cylinders, and spheres. Real-life applications include determining the amount of paint needed to cover a wall in a modern Dubai apartment or the volume of a water tank required for a villa in the desert. These skills are essential for understanding space and capacity in both urban and rural settings across the UAE.

## Graphs and Coordinate Geometry
This unit covers the Cartesian plane, where students plot and read coordinates. They learn to draw linear graphs from equations in the form y = mx + c, identifying the gradient and y-intercept. Real-world applications include plotting the distance-time graph for a journey from Dubai to Abu Dhabi or analysing temperature changes in a desert over 24 hours. Students use graphs to model relationships and solve practical problems, reinforcing their understanding of how algebra and geometry intersect in everyday life.
