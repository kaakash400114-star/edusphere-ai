# UAE Curriculum (MOE) Grade 6 Mathematics


## Number and Algebra
Students will extend their understanding of whole numbers to include integers, learning to represent them on a number line and perform operations like addition and subtraction. They will multiply and divide decimals up to three decimal places, applying these skills to solve real-world problems involving money, such as calculating costs in dirhams. Students will also explore factors and multiples, finding the greatest common factor and least common multiple for pairs of numbers, which helps with tasks like dividing items into equal groups at a souk.

## Geometry and Measurement
In this unit, students will calculate the perimeter and area of various 2D shapes, including squares, rectangles, triangles, and parallelograms, using correct formulas. They will also learn to convert between different metric units of length, mass, and capacity. To connect with their local environment, they might calculate the area of a courtyard in metres or determine the volume of a water tank in litres, applying these concepts to understand spatial relationships in architecture like the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.

## Ratio and Proportion
Students will understand ratio as a comparison of two quantities and simplify them to their lowest terms. They will learn to express ratios in different forms and use them to solve problems involving sharing or scaling. For example, they can determine the ratio of boys to girls in a class or calculate the quantities of ingredients needed to scale a recipe. They will also connect ratios to currency conversions, such as finding the exchange rate between UAE dirhams and other currencies.

## Percentages
This section covers the concept of percentage and its relationship with fractions and decimals. Students will learn to convert between these forms and calculate simple percentages of quantities. They apply this skill to practical situations like calculating discounts on items in a souk, finding the VAT amount on a purchase, or determining the percentage of land covered by desert in the UAE. Real-world examples make the concept of percentage tangible and relevant to daily life.

## Patterns and Algebra
Students will analyse and extend numerical patterns, identifying and applying the constant difference or ratio between consecutive terms to determine subsequent values. They will represent unknown quantities using variables to formulate algebraic expressions such as 2d + 3 for the cost of 2 kilograms of dates plus 3 dirhams. Students will translate word problems into one-step equations, like x - 7 = 15, and solve for the unknown variable using inverse operations, maintaining balance on both sides of the equation. These concepts are applied to practical UAE contexts, such as calculating the total cost of multiple items or determining the duration of a journey from a starting point. This foundational work builds essential algebraic reasoning and problem-solving skills.

## Data Handling
Students will collect, organise, and interpret data using various methods, including tally charts and frequency tables. They will construct and interpret bar graphs and line graphs to represent information, such as the average monthly temperatures in the UAE or the number of visitors to a local landmark like a museum or souk. They will also learn to find the mean, median, mode, and range of a set of data to understand its central tendency and spread.
