# UAE Curriculum (MOE) Grade 12 Mathematics


## Functions and Graphs
Students study advanced functions, including polynomial, rational, exponential, and logarithmic functions. They master transformations such as translations, reflections, and stretches of graphs. Key topics include finding domains and ranges, asymptotes, and the effects of parameters on function shapes. Students learn to sketch complex graphs and analyse their behaviour, including intercepts and turning points. Real-world examples involve calculating population growth in Dubai or decay of desert artefacts, using functions to model practical scenarios relevant to the UAE's dynamic environment.

## Calculus: Differentiation and Integration
This module covers advanced differentiation techniques, including implicit and parametric differentiation, and the differentiation of inverse trigonometric functions. Students apply differentiation to solve problems involving rates of change, optimisation, and kinematics. Integration focuses on methods like substitution, parts, and partial fractions. Applications include calculating the area under the curve of a skyscraper's elevation or determining the volume of a traditional Arabic dome, linking calculus to architectural marvels in the UAE.

## Trigonometry and Complex Numbers
Students delve deeper into trigonometric identities, equations, and their applications in solving complex problems. The unit covers the extension of trigonometric functions to complex numbers using Euler's formula. Topics include De Moivre's Theorem and the use of polar forms. Practical examples involve calculating the height of the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque's minarets or analysing wave patterns in the Persian Gulf, demonstrating how trigonometry and complex numbers model real-world phenomena in the UAE.

## Vectors and Matrices
This section explores vector operations in two and three dimensions, including dot and cross products, and their geometric interpretations. Students learn to use vectors to solve problems involving lines and planes in 3D space. Matrices cover operations, determinants, inverses, and solving systems of linear equations. Applications include analysing forces in modern Emirati construction projects or optimising logistics for goods transported between souks, showcasing vectors and matrices as tools for engineering and commerce.

## Probability and Statistics
Students learn to apply Bayes' Theorem to update probabilities using new information, such as re-evaluating the likelihood of a disease given a positive test result. They utilise probability mass functions for the binomial distribution to model discrete outcomes, like the number of successes in a fixed number of trials, and the probability density function for the normal distribution to model continuous data, such as student heights. Statistical methods include constructing confidence intervals for population means and hypothesis testing to make inferences about data, for instance, comparing the average battery life of two phone brands. The curriculum also teaches correlation analysis to quantify the strength of a linear relationship between two variables, such as linking advertising spend to monthly sales figures in a UAE retail company.

## Mechanics
This module applies mathematics to physical systems, covering statics, dynamics, and moments. Students learn to resolve forces, calculate equilibrium, and analyse motion under gravity and friction. Topics include projectiles, circular motion, and work-energy principles. Practical problems involve designing a sand dune buggy's trajectory or calculating the structural integrity of a wind tower in a desert climate, illustrating how mechanics principles are applied to engineering challenges unique to the UAE's environment.
