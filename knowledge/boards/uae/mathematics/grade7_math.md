# UAE Curriculum (MOE) Grade 7 Mathematics  
## Integers and Number Theory  
Students learn to perform operations with positive and negative integers, including addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. They apply the order of operations (BODMAS/PEDMAS) to solve multi-step problems. Concepts like absolute value and the rules for multiplying/dividing negatives are mastered. Students find factors and multiples of numbers, including prime factorisation, and determine the highest common factor (HCF) and lowest common multiple (LCM). An example is calculating the HCF of 36 and 48, which is 12.  

## Algebraic Expressions and Equations  
Students simplify and expand algebraic expressions using the distributive property and combine like terms. They solve one- and two-step linear equations, including those with fractions and decimals. Formulas for perimeter, area, and volume are used to solve for unknown variables. For instance, solving for `x` in the equation `3x + 5 = 20` yields `x = 5`. Word problems involving rates, such as calculating the cost of dirhams per kilogram of dates, reinforce practical application.  

## Fractions, Decimals, and Percentages  
Students convert between fractions, decimals, and percentages and perform operations with these forms. They solve problems involving percentage increase and decrease, such as calculating a 15% discount on a purchase of 500 AED, resulting in 75 AED off. Converting repeating decimals to fractions is covered, as well as applying ratios in real-world contexts like mixing paint in a 3:2 ratio. Currency exchange problems, such as converting dirhams to US dollars, are common.  

## Geometry and Measurement  
Students classify angles and triangles, calculate perimeters and areas of rectangles, triangles, and circles, and find volumes of prisms and cylinders. They apply the Pythagorean theorem to solve for unknown sides in right-angled triangles. A practical example is finding the area of a circular garden with a radius of 5 meters, which is 25π square meters. Converting between metric units (e.g., meters to centimeters) and understanding scale drawings of UAE landmarks are included.  

## Data Handling and Probability  
Students collect, organise, and interpret data using frequency tables, bar graphs, and pie charts. They calculate measures of central tendency, such as mean, median, and mode. Probability concepts include calculating the likelihood of simple events, like drawing a red card from a standard deck, which is 1/2. Real-world examples include analyzing the number of visitors to Dubai Mall or the probability of rain in the desert during summer.  

## Practical Problem Solving  
Students apply mathematical concepts to solve multi-step problems in everyday contexts. They use ratios and proportions to share resources, such as dividing a 200 AED grocery budget among family members in a 2:3 ratio. Distance-time problems involve calculating travel times between Abu Dhabi and Dubai, a journey of approximately 150 km at an average speed of 120 km/h. Financial literacy, like calculating simple interest on a savings account of 10,000 AED at 3% per annum, is emphasized.
