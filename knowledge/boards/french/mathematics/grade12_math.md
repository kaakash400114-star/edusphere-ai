# French (Éducation nationale) Grade 12 Mathematics  
## Analysis and Functions  
Students master the study of functions using concepts of limits, continuity, and differentiability. They learn to compute limits at infinity and identify discontinuities. The derivative is used to study the variations of a function, find tangent lines, and solve optimization problems, such as maximizing the area of a pastry display in a boulangerie for a given perimeter. Students also work on primitives to determine areas under curves, applying integration to solve practical problems involving rates of change in real-world scenarios.  

## Sequences and Series  
This section covers arithmetic and geometric sequences, deriving explicit formulas and recursive definitions. Students explore the limits of sequences, particularly those related to financial models or recurring events like the annual Tour de France route adjustments. They are introduced to series, focusing on convergence criteria for geometric and harmonic series. This knowledge is applied to solve problems involving accumulated values or infinite processes, such as calculating the total distance covered in an infinite number of diminishing laps around a circuit.  

## Probability and Statistics  
Students extend their understanding of probability to continuous random variables, studying probability density functions and their associated expectations and variances. They work with normal distributions to model real-world phenomena, such as the distribution of metro arrival times or student test scores. The course also covers conditional probability and independence, with applications to reliability analysis. In statistics, students use sampling techniques and confidence intervals to make inferences about populations, like estimating the average cost of a baguette across Parisian bakeries.  

## Geometry in Space  
The study of vectors and geometry in three-dimensional space is central. Students learn to define lines and planes using vector equations and parametric forms. They study the relative positions of geometric objects (intersection, parallelism, orthogonality) and calculate distances and angles between them. This knowledge is applied to solve problems in physics and engineering, such as determining the optimal angle for a satellite dish to receive signals from a specific point over the French countryside.  

## Complex Numbers  
Students are introduced to the set of complex numbers, learning their algebraic properties and geometric representation in the Argand plane. They master operations, including addition, multiplication, and finding roots of complex numbers. The exponential form (Euler's formula) is used to simplify calculations and solve equations, such as those describing alternating current in electrical circuits. Applications include modeling periodic phenomena, like the daily temperature fluctuations in different French regions.  

## Algorithmics and Programming  
While not a separate subject, programming is integrated using Python. Students write algorithms to solve mathematical problems, such as simulating the probability of drawing specific Métro tickets or sorting data from a statistical survey. They use loops, conditional structures, and functions to automate calculations and visualize results. This practical skill reinforces theoretical concepts, allowing students to explore large datasets or perform iterative calculations efficiently for their final projects.
