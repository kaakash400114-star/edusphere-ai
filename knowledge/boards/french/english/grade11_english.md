# French (Éducation nationale) Grade 11 English
## Global Issues and Media Literacy
Students analyze how media shapes public perception of global issues, such as climate change or migration, using critical frameworks. They identify bias and propaganda in various sources, including French and international news outlets like Le Monde or France 24. They evaluate the reliability of online information, distinguishing between fact and opinion. Students practice deconstructing advertising, particularly campaigns from major French corporations, to understand persuasive techniques. They explore the ethical responsibilities of journalists in reporting on sensitive topics, like the Tour de France, and how media can influence public policy.

## British and American Literary Traditions
Students study major literary movements from both traditions, such as the British Romantic era and the American Transcendentalist movement. They analyze key texts like *Frankenstein* or *Walden*, focusing on themes of individualism, nature, and societal rebellion. They compare how similar themes, like the quest for freedom, are expressed differently across cultural contexts. Students examine the stylistic devices, from metaphors to irony, used by authors like Shelley or Thoreau to create meaning. They explore the historical and cultural influences that shaped these works, connecting them to the values of their respective societies.

## Structured Argumentation and Debate
Students learn to construct formal arguments using the *thèse, antithèse, synthèse* model. They practice writing persuasive essays on complex topics, such as the merits of the four-day work week, by developing a clear thesis, anticipating counter-arguments, and providing evidence. They refine public speaking skills through structured debates on issues relevant to contemporary French society, such as pension reform or environmental policy. Students learn to rebut opposing points logically and to synthesize different perspectives into a cohesive final statement, demonstrating advanced reasoning and rhetorical skill.

## Economic and Social Systems
Students compare different economic models, focusing on the French social market economy with its emphasis on social protection (sécurité sociale) and worker rights. They learn key terminology like *chômage* (unemployment), *inflation*, and *PIB* (GDP). They analyze case studies, such as the impact of the Euro on European trade or the role of small businesses (*petites entreprises*) like a local *boulangerie* in the economy. They discuss the social implications of economic policies, including taxation and welfare systems, and how they aim to balance market efficiency with social equity within the European Union.

## Scientific and Technological Advancement
Students explore the language of scientific progress, focusing on innovations in fields like renewable energy, space exploration, and artificial intelligence. They learn to comprehend and summarize complex texts from scientific journals, such as reports on advancements in nuclear energy. They study case studies of major projects, like the ITER fusion reactor in Cadarache or the development of the Airbus A380. They debate the ethical considerations of new technologies, from data privacy in the *métro*'s ticketing system to the environmental impact of the automotive industry, connecting scientific principles to real-world applications.

## Intercultural Communication and Francophone Cultures
Students analyze the concept of *francophonie* as a cultural and linguistic community spanning five continents. They explore the rich diversity of Francophone cultures through literature, cinema, and music from regions like Quebec, West Africa, and the Indian Ocean. They study themes of identity, colonial legacy, and cultural exchange in works by authors from varied backgrounds. Students practice navigating cross-cultural communication, understanding potential misunderstandings related to concepts like *laïcité* or personal space, and they learn to appreciate the nuanced perspectives that exist within the broader French-speaking world.
