# Japanese (MEXT) Grade 2 Science


## The Seasons and Living Things
In spring, the weather becomes warmer and plants like cherry blossoms bloom. Summer is hot and brings the rainy season. Autumn cools down, and leaves change to red and gold. Winter is cold, and some trees lose their leaves. Animals change their behaviour with the seasons, like squirrels gathering nuts. A bento box might have different foods depending on the season, like cooling cucumber in summer and warm stew in winter.

## Properties of Matter
Objects are made from different materials that have unique properties, which we can observe through sight and touch. We group materials by their hardness, such as hard metals and rocks versus soft wood and paper. Texture is another key property, describing how a surface feels, like the smoothness of plastic or the roughness of sandpaper. We also classify materials by thermal properties, distinguishing between warm materials like wool and cold materials like metal. By understanding these properties, we select the most suitable material for a job, using strong wood for a chair frame to ensure it is sturdy and safe.

## Light and Shadows
Light travels from a source in straight lines. When these light rays are blocked by an opaque object, a shadow is formed on the surface behind it. The size of a shadow depends on the object's position relative to the light source: a closer object creates a larger shadow, while a distant object makes a smaller one. For example, a cherry blossom tree near a picnic blanket will cast a very large shadow, while the same tree further away will cast a smaller one. This explains why your own shadow is long in the morning or evening when the sun is low, and short at noon when it is high overhead.

## Sound and Vibration
All sounds are produced by vibrations, which are rapid back-and-forth movements. These vibrations travel as sound waves through the air, a medium, to our ears where they are heard. The loudness of a sound is determined by the amplitude, or strength, of the vibrations; a loud sound has strong vibrations, while a quiet sound has weak vibrations. The pitch of a sound is determined by the frequency of the vibrations; a high-pitched sound, like a whistle, has very fast vibrations, while a low-pitched sound, like a drum, has slower vibrations. For example, plucking a guitar string causes it to vibrate, creating sound, while striking a block of wood makes it vibrate to produce a different sound.

## Forces and Movement
A force is a push or a pull that makes an object move or stop moving. You use a push to start a toy car rolling and a pull to open a door. Gravity is a force that pulls everything towards the ground. The shinkansen uses a powerful push from the tracks to move very fast without wheels slipping. When you put money into a vending machine, you apply a push to the button.

## Looking After Our Environment
We must care for our environment to stay healthy and happy. We can sort our trash into bins for plastic, paper, and food waste to be recycled. Plants and animals need clean air, water, and soil. We should not litter on the street or in a park. By keeping our school and neighbourhood clean, we help make a beautiful place for everyone to live, like a clean park for hanami.
