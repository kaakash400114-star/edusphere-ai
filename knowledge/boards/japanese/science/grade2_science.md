# Japanese (MEXT) Grade 2 Science

## The Seasons and Living Things
In spring, the weather becomes warmer and plants like cherry blossoms bloom. Summer is hot and brings the rainy season. Autumn cools down, and leaves change to red and gold. Winter is cold, and some trees lose their leaves. Animals change their behaviour with the seasons, like squirrels gathering nuts. A bento box might have different foods depending on the season, like cooling cucumber in summer and warm stew in winter.

## Properties of Matter
Materials can be sorted into different groups based on how they feel and look. Some materials, like wood and paper, are soft and can be bent. Others, like metal and rock, are hard and strong. Materials can also feel smooth, rough, warm, or cold. We use these properties to choose the right material for an object, like using hard wood for a bento box to hold food safely.

## Light and Shadows
Light travels in straight lines and makes shadows. When an object blocks a light source, a shadow forms. The shadow's size changes depending on where the object is placed. A closer object makes a bigger shadow. On a sunny day, your shadow is long in the morning and evening, but short at noon. During hanami, the cherry blossom tree casts a shadow on the picnic blanket.

## Sound and Vibration
Sound is made by vibrations. When something vibrates, it makes a sound that travels to our ears. A loud sound has strong vibrations, and a quiet sound has weak vibrations. Different objects make different sounds because they vibrate in different ways. A train whistle makes a loud sound from the vibrations of the air, and a small bell makes a high, tinkling sound from its fast vibrations.

## Forces and Movement
A force is a push or a pull that makes an object move or stop moving. You use a push to start a toy car rolling and a pull to open a door. Gravity is a force that pulls everything towards the ground. The shinkansen uses a powerful push from the tracks to move very fast without wheels slipping. When you put money into a vending machine, you apply a push to the button.

## Looking After Our Environment
We must care for our environment to stay healthy and happy. We can sort our trash into bins for plastic, paper, and food waste to be recycled. Plants and animals need clean air, water, and soil. We should not litter on the street or in a park. By keeping our school and neighbourhood clean, we help make a beautiful place for everyone to live, like a clean park for hanami.
