# CBSE Class 8 Mathematics — NCERT

## Rational numbers
Properties of operations on rationals; role of 0 and 1; negative of a number; reciprocal; distributive law; representation between any two rationals.

## Linear equations in one variable
Solve with variables on both sides; fractional equations; reduce to linear form; word problems: ages, numbers, perimeter, money.

## Understanding quadrilaterals
Polygon angle-sum; exterior angles; kinds of quadrilaterals; parallelogram properties (diagonals); special parallelograms: rhombus, rectangle, square.

## Data handling
Organise raw data; grouped frequency; histogram; pie chart central angles; probability of simple events (cards, dice, coins).

## Squares and square roots
Properties of square numbers; patterns; find square root by repeated subtraction, prime factorisation, long division; decimals in square roots.

## Cubes and cube roots
Cube numbers and patterns; cube root by prime factorisation; units digits tricks.

## Comparing quantities
Percentage increase-decrease; discount, marked price; profit-loss percent; VAT/GST idea; compound interest by yearly and formula method; growth and decay.

## Algebraic expressions and identities
Multiply monomials-binomials; identities: (a+b)², (a−b)², a²−b², (x+a)(x+b); apply identities to multiply and simplify.

## Mensuration
Area of trapezium, general quadrilateral, polygon; surface area of cube-cuboid-cylinder; volume of cube-cuboid-cylinder; capacity vs volume.

## Exponents and powers
Negative exponents; laws extended to negative powers; very small/large numbers in standard form.

## Direct and inverse proportions
Recognise types; constant of proportionality; applications: speed-time, workers-days, food for days.

## Factorisation
Common factors; grouping; using identities; split the middle term; division of a polynomial by a monomial and binomial.

## Introduction to graphs
Linear graphs; coordinates; reading distance-time and simple conversion graphs; plotting from equations (y = x, y = 2x + 1).

## Playing with numbers
Number puzzles; digits and place value generalisation (2-digit = 10x + y); divisibility reasons (why 3's rule works).
