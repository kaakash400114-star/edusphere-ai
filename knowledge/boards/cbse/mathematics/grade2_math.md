# CBSE Class 2 Mathematics - NCERT (Math-Magic 2)

## Counting in groups and tens-ones to 99
Bundles of ten; 47 = 4 tens + 7 ones. Order and compare two-digit numbers (greater/smaller, before/after/between).

## Addition of two-digit numbers (with carry)
Add ones first, carry to tens: 47 + 38 = 85. NCERT method: story sums with shop money and objects.

## Subtraction of two-digit numbers (with borrow)
47 - 29: borrow a ten. Word problems: "Ravi had 52 stamps, gave 27 away."

## Introduction to multiplication as repeated addition
3 + 3 + 3 = 9 = 3 twos... no: 3 groups of 3. Tables of 2, 5 and 10 by skip counting on the number line.

## Introduction to division as sharing
Share 12 laddoos among 4 friends → 3 each. Division as equal sharing and equal grouping (no remainder here).

## Money: rupees and paise
Coins and notes to ₹100; add and subtract money in simple shopping; paise make rupees (100 paise = ₹1).

## Length, weight and capacity (non-standard to standard)
Hand-span, cubit, foot → introduce metre and centimetre; heavier/lighter with kg idea; a cup, a litre.

## Time: days, months, the clock (half hours)
Calendar months in order; reading half past; o'clock; how long activities take (minutes/hours).

## Shapes: 2D and solids around us
Circle, triangle, square, rectangle; cube (dice), cylinder (can), cone (birthday cap), sphere (ball). Edges and corners of solids.

## Patterns and data handling
Growing and repeating patterns; picture graphs: "How many children like mango best?"
