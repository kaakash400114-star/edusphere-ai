# CBSE Class 6 Mathematics — NCERT (Ganita Prakash)

## Knowing our numbers
Indian vs international place value; compare large numbers; estimation and rounding; Roman numerals; brackets and the order of operations (BODMAS).

## Whole numbers
Number line; successor/predecessor; properties of addition and multiplication (closure, commutative, associative, identity); patterns.

## Playing with numbers: factors, multiples, HCF-LCM
Tests of divisibility (2,3,4,5,6,8,9,10,11); primes, composites, prime factorisation; HCF and LCM by prime factorisation; word problems (bells, tiles, capacities).

## Basic geometrical ideas
Point, line, ray, segment; intersecting vs parallel lines; angles and types; triangles classified by sides and angles; quadrilaterals; circles: centre, radius, diameter, chord, arc.

## Understanding elementary shapes
Measuring and comparing line segments; angles with protractor; perpendiculars; classification of triangles and quadrilaterals; 3D shapes: faces, edges, vertices; polyhedra vs non-polyhedra.

## Integers
Negative numbers on the number line; ordering; add and subtract integers with movement-model; connect to temperature and lifts.

## Fractions
Proper, improper, mixed; equivalent; compare; add/subtract like and unlike; multiply a fraction by a whole number; fraction division (by whole number, intro).

## Decimals
Tenths, hundredths, thousandths; compare, order; add/subtract; multiply by 10, 100; decimals in money, length, weight.

## Data handling
Tally, pictograph, bar graph; organise data; sectors of a circle graph (reading); mean as "equal share" (intro).

## Mensuration
Perimeter of rectilinear figures; area of rectangles, squares; area by counting squares; simple composite rectilinear areas.

## Algebra: the language of unknowns
Variable idea with matchstick patterns; expressions; terms, coefficients; add/subtract like terms; simple equations by trial and balance (one operation).

## Ratio and proportion
Ratio as comparison (3:2); simplify; equivalent ratios; proportion as equal ratios; unitary method with direct examples.
