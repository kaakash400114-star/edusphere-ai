# CBSE Class 9 Mathematics - NCERT

## Number systems
Rational-irrational; real numbers and the number line; surds and laws of exponents with real powers; decimal expansions and rationality tests.

## Polynomials
Degree, types; zeroes of a polynomial; remainder theorem; factor theorem; factorising quadratics and cubics using theorems; identities: (a+b)^3, (a−b)^3, a^3+b^3, a^3−b^3.

## Coordinate geometry
Cartesian plane; quadrants; plot points; abscissa-ordinate; graph of a linear equation preview.

## Linear equations in two variables
Solution as ordered pair; graph of ax + by + c = 0; equations of axes; word problems with two unknowns (graphical sense).

## Introduction to Euclid's geometry
Axioms, postulates, theorems; definitions; equivalent versions of the fifth postulate (idea).

## Lines and angles
Angle pairs; parallel lines and a transversal; angle-sum property of a triangle; exterior angle theorem; proofs introduced formally.

## Triangles
Congruence criteria: SAS, ASA, SSS, RHS; properties of isosceles triangles; inequalities (side-angle links).

## Quadrilaterals
Parallelogram properties with proofs; midpoint theorem and its converse; diagonal-based properties of special quadrilaterals.

## Circles
Chords and their distance from centre (equal chords theorem); angle subtended; cyclic quadrilateral opposite angles 180°.

## Heron's formula
Area of a triangle from sides; applications to quadrilaterals split into triangles.

## Surface areas and volumes
Right circular cone: CSA, TSA, volume; sphere and hemisphere; cube-cuboid-cylinder recap; combined solids (basic).

## Statistics
Raw to grouped data; bar graphs, histograms, frequency polygons; mean (direct), median (ungrouped), mode (observation-based).

## Probability (empirical)
Trial, outcome, event; empirical probability P(E) = favourable/total trials; coin-dice experiments with data.
