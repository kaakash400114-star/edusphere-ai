# CBSE Class 11 Mathematics - NCERT

## Sets
Representation, types (empty, finite-infinite, equal); subsets-power set; Venn diagrams; operations and laws; practical problems on union-intersection.

## Relations and functions
Ordered pairs, Cartesian product; domain-codomain-range; kinds of relations (reflexive, symmetric, transitive); functions: identity, constant, modulus, signum, greatest integer; graphs.

## Trigonometric functions
Angles in radians-degrees; functions with domains-ranges; identities (fundamental, sum-difference, multiple-half angles); general solutions of trig equations; sine-cosine laws.

## Principle of mathematical induction
Statement, inductive hypothesis, inductive step; standard divisibility and inequality problems.

## Complex numbers and quadratic equations
i and powers; algebra of complex numbers; modulus-conjugate; argand plane; quadratic with negative discriminant; square root of a complex number (statements).

## Linear inequalities
Algebraic solutions in one variable; graphical in two variables; system of inequalities; feasible-region introduction.

## Permutations and combinations
Factorial; fundamental counting principle; nPr vs nCr; arrangements with repetitions; mixed selection problems (committees, words, digits).

## Binomial theorem
Expansion for positive integer index; general and middle terms; simple applications (approximations preview).

## Sequences and series
AP-GP recap at depth; nth term and sums; AM-GM relation; sum to n terms of special series (Σn, Σn^2, Σn^3); GP infinite sum condition.

## Straight lines
Slope; forms of line equations; angle between lines; distance point-line; family of lines idea.

## Conic sections
Circle (centre-radius forms); parabola, ellipse, hyperbola standard forms with focus-directrix-eccentricity definitions.

## Introduction to three-dimensional geometry
Coordinate axes-octants; distance and section formulae in 3D.

## Limits and derivatives
Intuitive limit idea; algebra of limits; standard limits; derivative as rate/ slope; derivatives of polynomials and sin-cos by first principles plus rules.

## Mathematical reasoning
Statements, negation, compound statements, quantifiers, implications, contrapositive-converse, validating arguments.

## Statistics and probability
Dispersion: range, mean deviation, variance, standard deviation; probability: sample space, events, addition rule (mutually exclusive), axiomatic view.
