# CBSE Class 12 Mathematics - NCERT (Part I & II essentials)

## Relations and functions
Types of relations (reflexive, symmetric, transitive, equivalence); one-one, onto functions; composition; inverse of a function.

## Inverse trigonometric functions
Domain-range; principal value branch; graphs; properties: sin⁻^1 + cos⁻^1 = π/2 family; simplifications.

## Matrices
Types; equality, addition, scalar and matrix multiplication (non-commutativity); transpose; symmetric-skew symmetric; elementary operations idea.

## Determinants
Determinant of order up to 3; properties; area of triangle; adjoint-inverse; solving systems by matrix method (consistent, inconsistent, unique).

## Continuity and differentiability
Continuity checks; differentiability; chain rule; implicit functions; inverse trig derivatives; exponential-logarithmic derivatives; log-differentiation and parametric idea.

## Application of derivatives
Rate of change; increasing-decreasing; maxima-minima (first and second derivative tests); tangents-normals; approximation; word problems (optimisation).

## Integrals
Indefinite integration: standard results, substitution, partial fractions, by parts (ILATE); definite integrals and properties.

## Applications of the integrals
Area under curves: lines, parabolas, circles (simple bounded regions).

## Differential equations
Order-degree; variable separable; homogeneous type (idea); linear equations with integrating factor; modelling statements (growth-decay).

## Vector algebra
Vectors and scalars; direction cosines; addition, scalar-vector multiplication; dot-cross products; projections; area of parallelogram-triangle via cross product.

## Three-dimensional geometry
Direction cosines-ratios; line equations (cartesian-vector); angle between lines; shortest distance (skew lines idea); plane equations; angle line-plane.

## Linear programming
Constraints, objective function, feasible region, corner point method; diet-manufacturing problems; unbounded-infeasible recognition.

## Probability
Conditional probability; multiplication theorem; independence; Bayes' theorem; random variables and probability distributions; mean-variance of distributions; binomial distribution (statements).
