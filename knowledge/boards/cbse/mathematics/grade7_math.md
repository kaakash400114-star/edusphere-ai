# CBSE Class 7 Mathematics — NCERT

## Integers
Properties of addition/subtraction/multiplication of integers; division; word problems; number-line reasoning; properties (closure, commutative, distributive over addition).

## Fractions and decimals
Multiply fractions (fraction × fraction); divide fractions; multiply/divide decimals; applications in money, measurement.

## Data handling
Collect, organise, representative values: mean, median, mode; double bar graphs; chance and probability as likelihood (fair die, coin).

## Simple equations
Setting up equations from statements; solving with balance (two operations); checking solutions; transfer (transposing) method.

## Lines and angles
Related angles: complementary, supplementary, adjacent, linear pair, vertically opposite; parallel lines and transversal angle pairs.

## The triangle and its properties
Angle sum (180°); exterior angle; types by sides/angles; Pythagoras property (statement and simple use); median and altitude.

## Comparing quantities
Percentage idea; convert fraction-decimal-percent; profit/loss (simple); simple interest: P, R, T formula and use.

## Rational numbers
Meaning; on number line; equivalent; compare; add/subtract/multiply/divide rational numbers; properties.

## Perimeter and area
Squares, rectangles, triangles; parallelogram (base × height); circles: circumference and area with π; conversion of units; applications.

## Algebraic expressions
Terms, factors, coefficients; like/unlike; add/subtract expressions; find value by substitution; use patterns.

## Exponents and powers
Base-index; laws: product, quotient, power of power, zero exponent; large numbers in standard (scientific) form.

## Symmetry and visualising solid shapes
Line and rotational symmetry; nets of solids; drawing solids on isometric paper; faces-edges-vertices relationships.
