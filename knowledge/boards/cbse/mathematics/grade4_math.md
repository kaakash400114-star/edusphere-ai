# CBSE Class 4 Mathematics - NCERT (Math-Magic 4)

## Large numbers: 5- and 6-digit, Indian place value
Lakh and ten-thousand places; read/write numerals and number names; compare, order, round to nearest 1000; introduction to the international system (thousand, million).

## Addition and subtraction of large numbers
Column work to 6 digits; estimation by rounding first; multi-step word problems (population, money, distances between cities).

## Multiplication: 2-digit × 2-digit/3-digit
Lattice/array idea then algorithm; multiply by 10, 100, 1000 by shifting; word problems (tickets, buses, collections).

## Division: long division by 1- and 2-digit numbers
Dividend, divisor, quotient, remainder; check by multiply-back; problems with sharing and grouping.

## Factors and multiples, prime numbers
Factors come in pairs; common factors; multiples and common multiples; primes to 100 (2, 3, 5, 7, 11...); divisibility by 2, 5, 10.

## Fractions: equivalent, comparing, simple addition
Equivalent fractions by multiplying top and bottom; compare with same numerator/denominator; add like fractions; fraction of a collection (1/4 of 20).

## Decimals: tenths and hundredths
Money and length as decimals (₹12.50, 3.6 m); place value of tenths/hundredths; compare and add simple decimals.

## Measurement conversions and word problems
km↔m, m↔cm, kg↔g, l↔ml; add/subtract measures with conversion; perimeter by counting/walking the boundary.

## Perimeter and area (counting squares)
Perimeter as the walk around; area as cover with unit squares of rectangles/squares; compare perimeters of same-area shapes.

## Time, speed as distance in unit time, money problems
Read timetables; duration across hours; simple "how far in one hour" beginnings; profit-free buying-selling stories with bills.
