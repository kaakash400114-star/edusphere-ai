# CBSE Class 12 English - Core approach

## Reading comprehension: note-making and summarising
Two unseen passages with note format (title, keys, abbreviations), 80-word summary; MCQ pattern practice.

## Writing: notice, invitation and replies
School notice; formal invitation card + reply; wording constraints.

## Writing: letters to editor and job application
Argument letter structure; application with CV; formal register discipline.

## Writing: article and report
Article 150-200 words with heading; newspaper report: headline-byline-lead-body.

## Literature: Flamingo prose
Extract-based MCQs; character-theme analysis with evidence; author's craft questions.

## Literature: Flamingo poetry
Devices at depth: irony, paradox, symbolism; central idea 120-word answers; comparison tasks.

## Literature: Vistas
Supplementary reader themes; alternate endings; inferential answers.

## Creative and analytical writing
Story completion; review writing (book-film); analytical paragraphs on quotes.

## Language refinement
Common errors at board level; phrasal-collocational precision; punctuation for clarity.

## Examination strategy
Time allocation across sections; presentation standards; attempt sequencing; revision planner.
