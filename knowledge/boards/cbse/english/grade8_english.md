# CBSE Class 8 English - NCERT (Honeydew) approach

## Determiners, articles and quantifiers revisited through texts
Precise use with uncountables; zero article cases (school, hospital as purpose).

## Tense system review: narration harmony
Keep time consistency in long narrations; perfect forms in reporting.

## Active-passive and reported speech mastery
Multi-tense transformations; instructions and requests in passive (imperatives: Let the work be done).

## Clauses and complex sentences
Identify noun-adjective-adverb clauses; combine simple sentences; punctuate complex sentences.

## Non-finites: infinitives, gerunds, participles
To-swim is fun; enjoy swimming; the crying baby, a broken chair.

## Modals for deduction: may-might-could-must have
"must have eaten" certainty about past; possibility ladders.

## Vocabulary: prefixes-suffixes, idioms, phrasal verbs
un-, dis-, -able, -tion; idiom meanings in context; separable-inseparable phrasals.

## Comprehension: literary devices
Simile, metaphor, personification, alliteration; tone and mood; author's purpose.

## Writing: formal letters, emails, article, speech
Formal letter of complaint/enquiry; email etiquette; article with heading-byline-ideas; speech with opening-hook-close.

## Listening-speaking integration
Take dictation of a paragraph; debate basics: for-against with reasons; pronounce multisyllabic academic words clearly.
