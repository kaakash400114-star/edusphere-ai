# CBSE Class 9 English - Language & Literature approach

## Reading: unseen passages (factual, literary, discursive)
Skim-scan strategies; MCQ + short answers; vocabulary in context; 20-mark practice format.

## Writing: descriptive paragraph, diary, story
Person-place description with sensory detail; diary tone honesty; story with given beginnings/endings, 150-200 words.

## Grammar: tenses in depth
All 12 tenses with signal words; errors in tense consistency across a passage; editing tasks.

## Grammar: clauses and sentence transformation
Noun-adjective-adverb clauses; transform simple↔complex↔compound; do-as-directed items.

## Grammar: reported speech, voice, determiners, modals
Board-exam style transformations; gap-filling format; error-correction format.

## Literature: theme-character-plot devices
Beehive/Moments texts: infer traits with evidence; irony, humour, twist; title justification answers.

## Poetry comprehension
Central idea, imagery, rhyme scheme, poetic devices (simile, metaphor, personification, repetition); write a short appreciation.

## Vocabulary building
Synonyms-antonyms in context; one-word substitutions; collocations; phrasal verbs for exams.

## Speaking-listening skills
Pronunciation: word stress, intonation in questions; formal vs informal register; 1-minute talk structure.

## Exam writing formats
Letter to the editor; analytical paragraph (data interpretation) preview; notice and email formats as per CBSE pattern.
