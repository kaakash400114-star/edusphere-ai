# CBSE Class 2 English — NCERT (Marigold 2) approach

## Naming words: common and special (proper) nouns
Common: boy, city, river. Special names start with capital letters: Ravi, Chennai, Ganga.

## Describing words (adjectives)
Big elephant, small ant, red rose, sweet mango. Use two describing words in one sentence.

## Action words and simple tenses
Today I play; yesterday I played; tomorrow I will play. -ed ending and will.

## Am, is, are; has, have
He is, they are; she has, we have. Agreement practice in speech and writing.

## Prepositions: in, on, under, near, between
The cat is under the table; the ball is between two boxes. Position words in pictures.

## Plurals with -es and irregular pairs
Boxes, mangoes, buses; man-men, child-children, tooth-teeth.

## Articles a, an, the
A dog barks; an owl hoots; the sun rises (special nouns take the).

## Punctuation: capital letters, full stop, question mark
Names, sentence starts; ask questions with ? — "Where is your school?"

## Reading comprehension (short passage)
Read a 6-8 line story; answer who, what, where questions in full sentences.

## Simple composition: my family, my pet, my school
5-6 sentences with describing words and full stops; read aloud with clear pronunciation.
