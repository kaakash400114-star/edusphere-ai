# CBSE Class 4 English - NCERT (Marigold 4) approach

## Nouns: countable-uncountable, gender, number
Much/many with uncountables (water, chairs); masculine-feminine-neuter pairs; plural spellings (-ies, -ves: leaf→leaves).

## Pronouns: reflexive and possessive
myself, yourself, himself, themselves; mine, yours, hers, theirs; possessive adjectives vs pronouns.

## Adjectives: order and comparison
Comparatives/superlatives; longer words use more/most (beautiful-more beautiful); than with comparatives.

## Verbs: simple present habit vs present continuous action
Habits with do/does negatives-questions; -ing actions now; signal words (usually, now, today).

## Simple past vs past continuous
Was/were + -ing for background, simple past for events; when/while sentences.

## Future forms: will and going to (intro)
Predictions with will; plans with going to.

## Adverbs: time, place, manner, frequency
Yesterday, here, bravely, always - position in sentence; frequency scale (always→never).

## Conjunctions: and, but, or, because, so
Join words, sentences; show reason and result; punctuation with but/because.

## Punctuation: question mark, exclamation, quotation marks
Direct speech with speaker tags; commas in direct address; exclamation for strong feeling.

## Comprehension, dictionary use and formal writing
Skim a passage, scan for facts; alphabetical order and guide words; write a short letter and notice with format.
