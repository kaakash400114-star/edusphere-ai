# CBSE Class 7 English - NCERT (Honeycomb) approach

## Tense mastery: perfect and perfect continuous
Present/past perfect with since-for-already-yet; continuous perfects; timeline reading of tenses.

## Modals: shall-should, will-would, can-could, may-might, must
Obligation, advice, possibility, permission; polite forms; short answers.

## Voice: active-passive across tenses
Simple present/past/future and continuous passives; agent with by; when passive is better.

## Reported speech: statements, questions, commands
Say-tell-ask; tense, pronoun, place-time shifts; reporting questions (if/whether; wh-word) and orders (to + verb).

## Clauses: noun, adjective, adverb
"I believe (that) he is right." "The book (which) you gave..." "She ran (because she was late)." Identify and use.

## Determiners and quantifiers
a-an-the rules; some-any, much-many, few-little, each-every, either-neither; articles with special nouns (the sun, an hour).

## Prepositions: fine distinctions
in-into, on-onto, at-in for places, since-for, between-among; preposition + gerund (good at swimming).

## Editing and error correction
Subject-verb agreement, tense slips, spelling traps; punctuate a passage; omission exercises.

## Comprehension: poetry and prose
Theme, tone, imagery in poems; character sketch in prose; infer motive; justify with lines.

## Writing: story, dialogue, descriptive, informal letter
Plot mountain (beginning-conflict-climax-resolution); dialogue punctuation; describe a place with senses; letter to a cousin.
