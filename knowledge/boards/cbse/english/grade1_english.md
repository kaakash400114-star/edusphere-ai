# CBSE Class 1 English — NCERT (Marigold 1) approach

## Alphabet and phonics A-Z
Letter names and sounds (a as in apple, z as in zebra). Capital and small letters. Vowels a, e, i, o, u; the rest are consonants. Rhyming songs build sound awareness.

## Naming words (naming myself, things)
Naming words = nouns in child language: Amma, school, ball, mango. Point-and-name practice around home and class.

## One and many (singular-plural)
One cat, two cats; one mango, many mangoes. Add -s for most plurals. Irregular pairs appear later.

## A and an
'a' before a sounding consonant: a ball, a cat. 'an' before a vowel sound: an apple, an elephant, an umbrella.

## These are / those are
This/these for near, that/those for far. "This is my book." "Those are birds."

## Simple sentences and capital letter, full stop
A sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop. "I am Ria. I am six years old."

## Action words (verbs in use)
Run, jump, eat, sing, sleep, read. Use in sentences: "Birds fly." "Fish swim."

## Rhymes and poems
Recite with rhythm; identify rhyming pairs (cat-hat, sun-fun). Builds listening and pronunciation.

## Opposites (basic)
big-small, hot-cold, day-night, up-down, happy-sad, in-out. Match-and-say practice.

## Picture reading and describing
Say three sentences about a picture: who, what, where. Speak in full sentences; listen to others.
