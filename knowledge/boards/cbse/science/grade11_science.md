# CBSE Class 11 Science — NCERT (Physics, Chemistry, Biology essentials)

## Physics: Units, measurement and motion
Dimensional analysis; errors; vectors and scalars, addition-resolution; kinematics graphs; projectile motion; Newton's laws applied; friction; work-energy-theorem; centre of mass idea; rotational basics (torque, angular momentum statements); gravitation: Kepler's laws, g variation.

## Physics: Properties of matter, thermodynamics, waves
Elasticity basics; pressure, Pascal-Archimedes-Bernoulli; viscosity, surface tension; thermal expansion; calorimetry, heat transfer; kinetic theory preview; oscillations: SHM equation-energy; waves: superposition, beats, Doppler (ideas).

## Chemistry: Basic concepts and structure of atom
Mole concept and stoichiometry; empirical-molecular formula; electron configurations (Aufbau, Hund, Pauli); quantum numbers; periodic trends reasoning.

## Chemistry: Bonding and states
VSEPR shapes; hybridisation (sp, sp2, sp3); MO diagram basics (O2 paramagnetic); gas laws, ideal gas equation, kinetic theory; liquids: vapour pressure, surface tension.

## Chemistry: Thermodynamics, equilibrium, redox
Enthalpy-Hess law; entropy-spontaneity (qualitative); Kc-Kp, Le Chatelier; ionic equilibrium: pH, buffers; redox titrations; hydrogen and s-block highlights; p-block group 13-14 highlights; basic organic: nomenclature, isomerism, reaction mechanisms (fission, electrophile-nucleophile); hydrocarbons reactions.

## Biology: Diversity of the living world
Taxonomic categories; five-kingdom overview; plant and animal kingdom salient phyla/classes with examples.

## Biology: Structural organisation and plant life
Morphology of root-stem-leaf-flower; anatomy: monocot vs dicot; cell structure, organelles; cell cycle: mitosis-meiosis; transport: water ascent, transpiration; photosynthesis (C3-C4 idea); respiration; plant growth hormones.

## Biology: Human physiology essentials
Digestion-absorption; breathing-gas transport; body fluids-circulation; excretory system-nephron; locomotion-muscle contraction idea; neural coordination-neuron, synapse; endocrine glands and hormones; reproduction basics; immunity innate vs acquired outline.
