# CBSE Class 4 Science - NCERT-aligned EVS (Looking Around 4)

## Green plants: the food makers
Green plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and air through a process called photosynthesis. They take in water from their roots and air through tiny holes in their leaves. The green part of the leaf, called chlorophyll, helps to trap sunlight to prepare food. For example, banyan and mango trees use this process to make food and grow big. This food gives them energy to live and helps them make new fruits, flowers, and leaves.

Photosynthesis in grade words: leaves use sunlight, air, water to make food; chlorophyll makes leaves green; plants release oxygen; experiment: covered leaf turns pale.

## Adaptation: how plants suit their home
Plants have special features to help them live in their homes. These features are called adaptations. Desert plants like cactus have thick stems to store water and spines to protect themselves. Water plants like lotus have wide leaves and air spaces to float. Mountain plants have small, waxy leaves to save water and stay warm. These adaptations help plants grow and stay healthy in different places.

Desert cactus stores water with waxy skin; water lily floats with broad leaves; mountain pine has sloping branches; marsh plants with breathing roots.

## Adaptation: animals in their habitats
Animals have special features to live in their homes called habitats. A camel has humps to store water for a long time in the desert, and a fish has gills to breathe under water. A polar bear has thick fur to keep warm in the cold Arctic snow, and a chameleon can change its colour to hide from other animals. These special features are called adaptations, which help animals find food, stay safe, and live happily in their surroundings.

Fish have fins and gills; camel's hump and wide feet; polar bear's fur; frog's webbed feet; camouflage in grasshopper and chameleon.

## Teeth, digestion and care
We have different kinds of teeth to help us eat. Incisors are sharp teeth at the front to bite food, like biting an apple. Molars at the back grind food into small pieces, like grinding chapati. Food breaks down in our stomach with the help of juices. It is important to brush teeth twice a day to keep them clean and strong. We should eat healthy food like fruits and vegetables to stay strong and healthy.

Four types: incisors cut, canines tear, premolars/molars grind; milk vs permanent teeth; the digestion journey mouth→stomach; avoid too many sweets; brush twice.

## Safety, first aid and communicable disease
We learn about keeping safe at home and school, like not touching hot things or running on stairs. First aid helps small hurts; we wash a small cut with clean water and cover it with a bandage. We learn to wash our hands with soap to stop germs from making us sick. Some sicknesses like the common cold or flu spread easily from person to person, so we use a handkerchief when we cough or sneeze. These rules help us stay healthy and not get sick.

Small wounds: clean, antiseptic, bandage; how colds spread; vaccination protects; rest, fluids, doctor's advice.

## Air, water pollution and conservation
We learn that clean air is important for us to stay healthy, but dirty air from vehicles and factories makes it polluted. Dirty water from homes and industries is called water pollution, which is bad for animals and plants. We save water by turning off the tap while brushing and taking short showers. We also save water by not wasting it when we play or wash. To keep air clean, we should use buses or walk instead of going everywhere by car. These small actions help us take care of our environment.

Causes of air and water pollution (smoke, garbage, sewage); effects on people, plants, animals; conservation habits; rainwater harvesting in simple words.

## Our universe: the sun, earth and moon
The Sun is a bright star that gives us light and heat during the day. Earth is our planet, a big round ball made of land and water, where we live and breathe. The Moon is Earth’s satellite that travels around us, shining with the Sun’s light at night. Earth moves around the Sun in a big circle called an orbit, and this takes one full year to complete. The Moon takes about 28 days to go around Earth, and this makes the shape of the Moon look different to us each night, from a tiny sliver to a big circle.

The sun is a star and our light-heat source; the earth spins - day and night; the moon reflects sunlight and shows phases; simple solar-system tour.

## Food and nutrition basics
Food gives us energy to play and grow. We need to eat different kinds of food, like roti, rice, dal, vegetables, fruits, milk, and eggs, to stay healthy. Foods that give us energy are called carbohydrates, like chapati and rice. Foods that help us grow are called proteins, like dal and milk. Fruits and vegetables give us vitamins and minerals to protect us from sickness. We should drink plenty of water every day. We must always wash our hands before eating to stay clean and avoid germs.

Nutrients: carbohydrates give energy (rice), proteins build (dal, egg), fats store energy (ghee), vitamins-minerals protect (vegetables, fruit); balanced plate idea.

## Force, work and simple machines in daily life
In Grade 4 Science, we learn about force, work, and simple machines. Force is a push or pull, like when we push a door open or pull a chair. Work is done when we use force to move an object, such as lifting a school bag. Simple machines make work easier; these include the lever, like a seesaw, and the inclined plane, like a ramp. We also learn about the wheel and axle, which helps in pulling carts easily. Machines help us do heavy tasks with less effort.

Push and pull; things move, stop, change direction; lever (see-saw), pulley (well), inclined plane (ramp) reduce effort.

## Our state, our country: resources and pride (EVS link)
We learn about our country and state, which are places where we live with our families. A country is a big land with its own government, rules, and leaders, like our Prime Minister. Our country has many useful things called resources, such as clean water for drinking, soil for growing food, and trees for fresh air. Different states in India have special resources, like apples in Himachal Pradesh or spices in Kerala. It is our duty to take care of these resources and feel proud of our beautiful country, India.

Water, soil, forests as resources; occupations from nature (farmer, fisher); protect monuments and public property.
