# CBSE Class 7 Science — NCERT

## Nutrition in plants
Photosynthesis: inputs (CO2, water, sunlight, chlorophyll), outputs (glucose, oxygen); starch test; other modes: insectivorous plants (pitcher), parasites (cuscuta), saprotrophs (fungi), symbiosis (lichen, rhizobium in pulses).

## Nutrition in animals
Steps: ingestion, digestion, absorption, assimilation, egestion; human digestive system organs and juices (saliva, gastric, intestinal); amoeba's food vacuole; ruminants' four-chambered stomach, cud chewing.

## Heat
Hot vs cold by temperature, not touch; thermometer (clinical, laboratory) use; conduction (metals good), convection (liquids, gases, sea breeze), radiation (sun's heat); why woollens keep warm.

## Acids, bases and salts
Natural indicators: litmus, turmeric, china rose; acids sour, bases bitter-slippery; neutralisation: acid + base → salt + water; daily life: antacid, soil treatment, ant bite.

## Physical and chemical changes
Physical: reversible, no new substance (ice-melt, shape-change); chemical: new substance, usually not reversible (rusting, burning, curdling); rust prevention; galvanisation.

## Respiration in organisms
Aerobic: glucose + oxygen → CO2 + water + energy; anaerobic in yeast → alcohol; muscle cramps from lactic acid; breathing rate and exercise; fish gills, earthworm moist skin.

## Transportation in animals and plants
Human heart four chambers, arteries-veins-capillaries; blood: RBC, WBC, platelets; pulse; xylem carries water up, phloem carries food both ways; transpiration pull.

## Reproduction in plants
Asexual: budding (yeast), fragmentation (algae), spores (ferns), vegetative (potato, rose stem); sexual: flower parts, pollination (self/cross by wind, insects, water), fertilisation, fruit and seed.

## Motion and time
Speed = distance/time; uniform vs non-uniform; distance-time graph reading; simple pendulum as timekeeper; units.

## Electric current and its effects
Circuit symbols; heating effect (fuse, heater filament); magnetic effect (compass deflects, electromagnet); bell working.

## Light
Laws of reflection (equal angles); plane mirror image (virtual, laterally inverted, same size); concave/convex mirrors and lenses basics; dispersion: prism makes rainbow; the eye's care.

## Forests: our lifeline
Canopy-understorey layers; forests as habitat, water bank, air purifier; food web interdependence; deforestation effects.

## Wastewater story
Sewage and its treatment steps (screening, sedimentation, aeration, chlorination); septic tanks; maintain drains; reuse grey water; our role in clean water.
