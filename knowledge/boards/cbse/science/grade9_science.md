# CBSE Class 9 Science - NCERT

## Matter in our surroundings
States and characteristics; melting-boiling points; latent heat idea; evaporation causes cooling; plasma-BE condensates mention.

## Is matter around us pure
Mixtures-homogeneous-heterogeneous; solutions, colloids, suspensions; concentration by mass percent; separation: centrifugation, sublimation, chromatography; physical vs chemical change.

## Atoms and molecules
Laws: conservation, constant proportions, multiple proportions; Dalton's postulates; atomic-molecular masses; mole concept preview; formula unit mass.

## Structure of the atom
Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr models; subatomic particles; atomic number, mass number; isotopes and isobars and uses.

## The fundamental unit of life
Cell theory; plasma membrane (selective), osmosis-diffusion; cell wall, nucleus, cytoplasm; organelles: ER, Golgi, lysosomes, mitochondria, plastids, vacuoles; plant vs animal cell.

## Tissues
Plant: meristematic, permanent (parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma), xylem-phloem; animal: epithelial, connective, muscular, nervous; structure fits function.

## Diversity in living organisms
Classification basis; hierarchy; major groups: Monera, Protista, Fungi; Plantae divisions; Animalia phyla (porifera to chordata) with examples; nomenclature.

## Motion
Distance-displacement; speed-velocity, acceleration; graphical motion (distance-time, velocity-time); three equations of motion (derivation-free use).

## Force and laws of motion
Balanced-unbalanced forces; Newton's laws; inertia and mass; F = ma; action-reaction; conservation of momentum in collisions.

## Gravitation
Universal law; free fall, g; mass vs weight; thrust and pressure; Archimedes' principle; buoyancy and density.

## Work and energy
Work = force × displacement (with angle idea); kinetic energy (½mv^2), potential energy (mgh); work-energy theorem; law of conservation; power and commercial unit kWh.

## Sound
Production-propagation; longitudinal waves; characteristics: amplitude, frequency, wavelength, speed; reflection: echo, reverberation; ultrasound uses; human ear outline.

## Why do we fall ill
Health vs disease-free; acute-chronic; causes: infectious (pathogens, vectors) and non-infectious; disease basics: TB, malaria, AIDS prevention-society; immune idea, vaccination.

## Natural resources
Air: composition, pollution, role (wind, rain); water: cycles, pollution; mineral riches in soil; biogeochemical cycles: water, nitrogen, carbon, oxygen; ozone layer.

## Improvement in food resources
Crop variety improvement; crop production management; animal husbandry: cattle, poultry, fish (capture-culture), bee-keeping.
