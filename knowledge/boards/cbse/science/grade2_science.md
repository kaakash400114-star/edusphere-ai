# CBSE Class 2 Science - NCERT-aligned EVS

## Food groups and balanced diet
Food is divided into groups like energy-giving foods such as rice and chapatti, body-building foods like milk and dal, and protective foods like fruits and vegetables. A balanced diet has all these food groups to help us grow big and strong and keep us healthy. We must eat different foods every day to get all the nutrients our body needs. For example, eating roti, dal, green vegetables, and a banana gives us the right balance of food. Always remember to drink lots of water to keep your body fit and working well.

Energy food (rice, roti), body-building food (dal, milk, eggs), protective food (vegetables, fruit). Eat on time; avoid too many sweets.

## Parts of the body and movement
We learn about the main parts of our body, such as the head, arms, legs, and hands. We discover how these body parts help us move, like walking with our legs and running with our feet. Our fingers help us to hold and pick up things, and our eyes help us to see. We also learn about the senses on our body, like our ears for hearing and our tongue for tasting. We talk about how we use different body parts for different activities, like speaking with our mouth and eating with our teeth.

Joints let us bend: knee, elbow, neck. Exercise keeps muscles strong; posture while sitting.

## Animal homes, sounds and young ones
Animals live in different kinds of homes called habitats. Birds build nests on trees to lay eggs and keep their babies safe, like the sparrow. Rabbits dig burrows in the ground to live in, while cows and buffaloes live in sheds. Many animals make sounds to talk to each other or their young ones, for example, dogs bark and lions roar. Baby animals have special names, like a calf for a young cow and a kitten for a young cat. They depend on their mothers for food and protection until they grow big.

Nest, kennel, stable, burrow; cow-calf, dog-puppy, hen-chick; sounds: bark, moo, chirp.

## Plant types: tree, shrub, herb, climber, creeper
Plants are of different types like trees, shrubs, herbs, climbers, and creepers. Trees are big plants with thick, woody stems called trunks, like mango and neem trees. Shrubs are smaller plants with many woody stems growing from the ground, like rose and bougainvillea plants. Herbs are small plants with soft, green stems, like wheat and grass plants. Climbers are plants that climb with support using tendrils, like money plants, while creepers spread on the ground, like pumpkin and cucumber plants.

Big trees (banyan), small plants (tulsi), climbers (grape vine) - with support; creepers (pumpkin) spread on ground.

## Uses of plants
Plants are very useful to us and give us many important things. We get food from plants like fruits, vegetables, and grains such as mangoes, carrots, and rice. Plants also give us oxygen, which we need to breathe, and wood from trees is used to make furniture and houses. Some plants, like neem and tulsi, are used in medicines to keep us healthy and strong. Plants also provide homes and shelter to small animals like birds and squirrels.

Food (rice, wheat, fruit), wood, cotton clothes, medicines (tulsi), shade, clean air.

## Water: sources, uses and safety
Water is found in different places like rivers, lakes, and wells. We use water for drinking, bathing, cooking, and cleaning. It is important to drink clean water to stay healthy and not get sick. We should not waste water and always turn off the tap when not in use. Dirty water can make us ill, so we must use clean and safe water for all our needs.

River, pond, well, rain. Drinking water must be clean - boil or filter. Do not waste water; water-borne care.

## Air around us
Air is all around us, filling the empty spaces, even though we cannot see it. We need air to breathe, just like plants and animals do, because it helps us stay alive. When air moves, we call it wind, and it can help cool us down on a hot day. Air can make things like kites and flags move and dance when it blows strongly. We can feel air when it moves past our face or when we blow up a balloon, showing that it is real and all around us.

Moving air is wind; air fills things (balloon, tyre). Air is needed for burning and breathing. Keep air clean.

## Weather and seasons
We learn about the weather outside, like sun, rain, wind, and clouds. The weather changes during the four seasons: summer, rainy, autumn, and winter. In summer, it is very hot and we like to eat ice cream. During the rainy season, we get rain and water helps plants grow. In winter, it gets cold and we wear warm clothes like sweaters. The weather and seasons tell us what clothes to wear and what games to play.

Summer, winter, monsoon - clothes, food and activities of each. Keep a weather chart with symbols.

## Safety rules and first aid basics
Children learn important safety rules like looking both ways before crossing the road and not touching hot objects. They are taught that fire and electricity are dangerous and should be handled only by grown-ups. For first aid basics, they learn to apply a clean bandage if they get a small cut. They understand that if they get hurt, they should tell a teacher or parent immediately. Children also learn to keep their surroundings clean to stay healthy and safe.

On the road, at home, near fire, in water. Small cuts: wash, apply antiseptic, tell an adult.

## Our neighbourhood and festivals
Children learn about the people and places in their neighbourhood, like the doctor, grocer, and post office. They understand how to be good neighbours by sharing and following rules, such as waiting for their turn and using kind words. Children also celebrate important Indian festivals like Diwali, Holi, and Eid, learning about their customs, lights, colours, and special foods. They identify different festivals with their symbols, such as earthen lamps for Diwali and coloured powders for Holi. This helps them respect cultural traditions and celebrate joyfully with their community.

Market, hospital, school, park, place of worship; festivals bring people together; neighbourhood cleanliness.
