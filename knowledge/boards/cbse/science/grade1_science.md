# CBSE Class 1 Science — NCERT-aligned (EVS approach)

## Living and non-living things
Living things eat, grow, breathe and move by themselves; non-living things do not. Examples: a crow is living, a stone is non-living, a car moves but does not grow.

## Our body and sense organs
Five senses: eyes see, ears hear, nose smells, tongue tastes, skin feels. Name major body parts: head, shoulders, knees, fingers. Care: wash hands, clean eyes with water.

## Animals around us
Pets (dog, cat), farm animals (cow, goat), wild animals (lion, elephant), birds (sparrow, crow). Animals have babies and homes; the cow gives milk, the hen lays eggs. Animal sounds in Indian context.

## Plants around us
Plants need water, air, sunlight and soil. Parts: root, stem, leaf, flower. Trees give us shade, fruit and clean air. Simple experiment: a bean sprouting in a wet cotton.

## Food and water
We eat food to grow and stay healthy. Healthy food: fruits, vegetables, milk, dal, roti. Drink clean water; wash fruits before eating.

## Clothes we wear
Clothes protect us: cotton in summer, woollens in winter, raincoats in rain. Uniform for school, festive clothes for celebrations.

## Home, school and neighbourhood
Rooms of a house and their uses; keep home clean. People who help us: teacher, doctor, postman, police, farmer.

## Air, water and weather
Air is everywhere though we cannot see it. Water sources: river, pond, tap, well. Save water: close the tap. Sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy days — how weather changes what we wear and do.

## Keeping safe and healthy
Road safety (look both ways, use the zebra crossing), do not play with fire or sharp things. Exercise, sleep and cleanliness keep us well.

## Our helpers and transport
Vehicles: bus, car, bicycle, train, boat. Which vehicle goes on water, road, rail? Community helpers and their tools.
