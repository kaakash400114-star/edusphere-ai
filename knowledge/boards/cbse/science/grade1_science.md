# CBSE Class 1 Science - NCERT-aligned (EVS approach)

## Living and non-living things
Living things are things that grow, eat, breathe, and have babies. Non-living things do not grow, eat, breathe, or have babies. We can tell if something is living by checking if it needs food and water to grow and stay alive. For example, a cat is a living thing because it eats food and drinks water, and a rock is a non-living thing because it does not need food or water. All living things move in their own way, like a dog walking or a bird flying, while non-living things move only when we or something else pushes them.

Living things eat, grow, breathe and move by themselves; non-living things do not. Examples: a crow is living, a stone is non-living, a car moves but does not grow.

## Our body and sense organs
Our body has different parts like the head, hands, legs, and feet. We have five sense organs: the eyes to see, the ears to hear, the nose to smell, the tongue to taste, and the skin to feel. The eyes help us see things like a ball or a book, the ears help us hear sounds like a bell or a dog barking, and the nose helps us smell things like flowers or food. Our tongue helps us taste sweet, salty, or bitter food, and our skin helps us feel if something is soft or hard. We must keep our body and sense organs clean and safe.

Five senses: eyes see, ears hear, nose smells, tongue tastes, skin feels. Name major body parts: head, shoulders, knees, fingers. Care: wash hands, clean eyes with water.

## Animals around us
Animals are living things that move, eat, and grow, like the dog in our home or the cow on the farm. They have different body coverings: some have fur, like cats, while others have feathers, like parrots, or scales, like fish. Animals are found in many places, such as houses, fields, forests, and water, and they need food, water, and shelter to live. Some animals give us things like milk from cows and eggs from hens, which help us stay healthy. We must be kind to all animals and take care of them because they are important for our world.

Pets (dog, cat), farm animals (cow, goat), wild animals (lion, elephant), birds (sparrow, crow). Animals have babies and homes; the cow gives milk, the hen lays eggs. Animal sounds in Indian context.

## Plants around us
Plants are living things that grow all around us. They have roots to hold them in the ground and a stem to stand straight. Plants have green leaves which make food for them using sunlight. We can see different plants, like a tall tree, a small bush, or a pretty flower. Many plants give us fruits like mango and apple, which we can eat to stay healthy and strong.

Plants need water, air, sunlight and soil. Parts: root, stem, leaf, flower. Trees give us shade, fruit and clean air. Simple experiment: a bean sprouting in a wet cotton.

## Food and water
We learn about different foods like rice, dal, roti, and vegetables that give us energy to play and grow. Good food makes us strong, and we must eat fruits and milk every day to stay healthy. Water is very important for our body, and we should drink clean water from a safe source, like a bottle or a jug. We wash our hands before eating food to stay clean and avoid getting sick. Eating a variety of foods helps us get all the nutrients we need to be healthy and happy.

We eat food to grow and stay healthy. Healthy food: fruits, vegetables, milk, dal, roti. Drink clean water; wash fruits before eating.

## Clothes we wear
We wear clothes to cover our bodies and stay clean. Different clothes are worn for different seasons like woollen clothes in winter and cotton clothes in summer. Some clothes keep us safe, like a helmet for our head and raincoat in the rain. We also wear special clothes for different occasions, like a uniform to school or a kurta for a festival. Clothes must be washed regularly to keep them clean and germ-free.

Clothes protect us: cotton in summer, woollens in winter, raincoats in rain. Uniform for school, festive clothes for celebrations.

## Home, school and neighbourhood
We learn about the places where we live, play, and study. Our home has rooms like bedroom, kitchen, and bathroom; school has classroom, playground, and library. We understand different people in our neighbourhood such as shopkeeper, postman, and doctor. Children learn to follow simple rules at home, school, and on the road for their safety. We also identify familiar places around us like park, temple, market, and bus stop.

Rooms of a house and their uses; keep home clean. People who help us: teacher, doctor, postman, police, farmer.

## Air, water and weather
Air is all around us, we cannot see it but it fills up space. We feel air when it blows, like when a fan moves or when we fly a kite in the sky. Water is a transparent liquid that we drink, we wash our hands with it, and rain gives water to plants. The weather tells us how hot or cold it is outside and if the sun is shining or if it is cloudy and rainy. We wear warm clothes when the weather is cold to stay healthy and happy.

Air is everywhere though we cannot see it. Water sources: river, pond, tap, well. Save water: close the tap. Sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy days - how weather changes what we wear and do.

## Keeping safe and healthy
Children learn important rules to keep their bodies safe and healthy. They must wash their hands with soap and water before eating food and after using the toilet to stay clean. We eat healthy food like fruits, vegetables, and dal to grow strong and get energy. We should not put dirty things or strange objects in our mouth to prevent sickness. It is good to play safely and not run near stairs or water. We also learn to tell a grown-up if we feel hurt or see someone who needs help.

Road safety (look both ways, use the zebra crossing), do not play with fire or sharp things. Exercise, sleep and cleanliness keep us well.

## Our helpers and transport
In Class 1, children learn about helpers like postmen, doctors, and teachers who help us in our daily lives. They also understand different modes of transport such as auto-rickshaws, buses, and trains that take us from one place to another. Children identify the basic roles of these helpers and the sounds made by different vehicles. They learn that transport helps us move around and that helpers like the sweeper keep our surroundings clean. The topic teaches children to appreciate the importance of people and vehicles in making life easy and safe.

Vehicles: bus, car, bicycle, train, boat. Which vehicle goes on water, road, rail? Community helpers and their tools.
