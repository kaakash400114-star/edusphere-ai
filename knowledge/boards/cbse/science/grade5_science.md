# CBSE Class 5 Science — NCERT-aligned EVS (Looking Around 5)

## Super senses: how animals sense the world
Tiger's night vision, eagle's sharp eyes, dog's smell, bat's hearing (echolocation), snake's vibration sense — compare with human senses.

## From tasting to digesting
Tongue taste zones, saliva starts digestion; teeth roles; the journey: food pipe, stomach, small intestine absorbs, large intestine removes; good food habits.

## Seeds and seeds: germination and dispersal
Seed parts (seed coat, baby plant, food store); germination needs water-air-warmth; dispersal by wind, water, animals, explosion (castor).

## Every drop counts: water conservation
Where water comes from; old step-wells to modern tanks; evaporation and clouds; conserve at home and school; rainwater harvesting.

## Experiments with water: solutions
Soluble and insoluble; solution, sediment, filtration; evaporation leaves salt; floating-sinking with shape and weight.

## A treat for mosquitoes: disease and environment
Mosquito life cycle in standing water; malaria/dengue spread; prevent by clean surroundings, nets, covers; community health habits.

## Up you go: mountains and adventure
Mountain landforms, weather there; climbing spirit and teamwork; respect for nature and safety.

## Space travel and the solar system
Planets of the sun's family; the moon's phases; satellites help us (weather, TV); India's space milestones (ISRO) in simple words.

## What if it finishes: fuels and energy
Wood, coal, petrol, LPG are limited; saving energy at home; sun and wind as never-ending sources; small actions matter.

## Forests, wildlife and our duty
Forests as homes and water banks; food chains in a forest (grass→deer→tiger); protect wildlife: parks and sanctuaries; say no to plastic litter.

## Growing food: from field to plate
Crop seasons (kharif, rabi in simple words); irrigation; farmer's work calendar; storing and transport; respect for food and farmers.
