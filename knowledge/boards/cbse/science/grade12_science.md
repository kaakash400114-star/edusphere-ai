# CBSE Class 12 Science — NCERT (Physics, Chemistry, Biology essentials)

## Physics: Electrostatics and current electricity
Coulomb's law, field-dipole; Gauss law idea; potential-capacitance-dielectrics; drift velocity, Ohm's law, resistivity; Kirchhoff rules, Wheatstone, potentiometer-metre bridge ideas.

## Physics: Magnetism, EMI and AC
Moving charge force, Biot-Savart, Ampere law; solenoid-toroid; loop-dipole; EMI: Faraday-Lenz; self-mutual inductance; AC circuits with phasors, LCR resonance; transformer; power lines.

## Physics: Optics, dual nature, atoms-nuclei
Ray optics: mirrors-lenses-instruments; wave optics: Huygens, YDSE fringe idea, diffraction broad view; photoelectric effect; de Broglie; Bohr model; nuclei: mass defect, fission-fusion; semiconductors: diode, LED, photodiode basics; communication preview.

## Chemistry: Solutions and electrochemistry
Concentration terms; Raoult-Henry; colligative properties; Nernst, conductance, Kohlrausch; batteries-fuel cells; corrosion.

## Chemistry: Kinetics, surface chemistry, d-f block
Rate law, order-molecularity, Arrhenius, half-life; adsorption, catalysis, colloids; lanthanoid contraction, coordination compounds: nomenclature, VBT-CFT ideas, isomerism.

## Chemistry: Haloalkanes, alcohols, carbonyls, amines, biomolecules
SN1-SN2 outline; alcohol-phenol acidity; aldehyde-ketone nucleophilic addition; carboxyl acidity; amine basicity; diazonium uses; carbohydrates-proteins-vitamins-nucleic acids structure classes; polymers classes; chemistry in everyday life highlights.

## Biology: Reproduction
Flower-to-fruit sexual reproduction; human male-female systems; gametogenesis; menstrual cycle; fertilisation-pregnancy-parturition; reproductive health-contraception-MTP-ART outline.

## Biology: Genetics and evolution
Mendelian genetics with problems; linkage, crossing over; sex-linked inheritance (haemophilia, colour blindness); genetic disorders (sickle cell, Down); DNA as genetic material; replication-transcription-translation code; lac operon idea; genome-human genome project; evolution: Hardy-Weinberg, origin ideas.

## Biology: Biotechnology, ecology and health
rDNA tools (restriction enzymes, plasmid), PCR; Bt cotton, insulin; ecology: organisms-populations interactions; ecosystems energy flow, pyramids; biodiversity-hotspots; environmental issues; human health-diseases (AIDS, cancer basics); microbes in human welfare.
