# CBSE Class 8 Science - NCERT

## Crop production and management
Agricultural steps: ploughing, sowing, manuring-fertilising, irrigation, weeding, harvesting, storage; kharif-rabi crops; nitrogen fixation by pulses; granaries and silos.

## Microorganisms: friend and foe
Bacteria, viruses, fungi, protozoa; uses: curd (lactobacillus), bread, antibiotics, vaccines; diseases: cholera, TB, malaria, dengue; food preservation (pasteurisation, pickling); nitrogen cycle role.

## Materials: metals and non-metals
Physical: lustre, malleability, ductility, sonority, conductivity; chemical: reaction with oxygen, water, acids; displacement; uses and safety (sodium in water).

## Coal and petroleum
Fossil fuels as exhaustible resources; coal → coke, coal tar, coal gas; petroleum refining products; natural gas, CNG; conservation: alternate sources, efficiency.

## Combustion and flame
Combustion needs fuel + air + ignition temperature; types: rapid, spontaneous, explosion; flame zones (dark, luminous, non-luminous); control fire by removing one need; inflammable substances.

## Conservation of plants and animals
Deforestation causes-effects; conservation: national parks, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves; Red Data Book; migration; reforestation vs afforestation.

## Cell: structure and functions
Discovery; unicellular-multicellular; parts: cell membrane, wall (plants), cytoplasm, nucleus (control centre), vacuole; plant vs animal cell; tissues preview.

## Reproduction in animals
Sexual: male-female gametes, fertilisation internal/external (frog); viviparous-oviparous; metamorphosis in frog; asexual in hydra, amoeba (binary fission); reproductive health basics.

## Reaching the age of adolescence
Puberty changes: height, voice, hair; hormones: testosterone, oestrogen, thyroid; menstrual cycle basics with respect; personal hygiene; balanced diet for teens.

## Force and pressure
Force as push-pull: change state, shape, direction; contact vs non-contact (gravity, magnetic, electrostatic); pressure = force/area (nails, knives); pressure in liquids; atmospheric pressure demonstrations.

## Friction
Causes of friction; depends on surfaces and weight; static-sliding-rolling; advantages (walking, brakes) and reduction (oil, ball bearings); drag in fluids.

## Sound
Sources are vibrations; needs a medium (no sound in vacuum); amplitude-loudness, frequency-pitch; human range 20 Hz-20,000 Hz; noise pollution and control.

## Chemical effects of electric current
Conducting liquids; electrodes-electrolyte; electroplating (copper on iron); testing conductivity safely; LED tester.

## Light
Reflection laws again with angles; human eye parts; dispersion through prism; atmospheric refraction idea; the eye's defects preview; care of eyes.

## Some natural phenomena
Lightning: charges in clouds, safety rules during storms; earthquake: seismic waves, plates, safety (drop-cover-hold); seismograph idea.

## Light (applications) and pollution wrap
Water-air pollution recaps; greenhouse gases and global warming basics; individual action plan.
