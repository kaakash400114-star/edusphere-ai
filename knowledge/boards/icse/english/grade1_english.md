# ICSE Class 1 English — CISCE approach

## Phonics foundation and handwriting
Letter-sound mastery (a-z), blends sh-ch-th; capital-small formation; three-letter word building (cat, pin, sun).

## Sight words and simple sentences
I, am, the, to, in, is, it; read and say: "I am in the red tent."

## Naming and action words
Naming: ball, bird, mummy; action: hop, sing, clap; combine: "The bird sings."

## One-many, a-an, opposites
One sun-two stars; an ant, a dog; big-small, open-shut.

## Rhymes, picture talk and storytelling
Recite 4-line rhymes; describe a picture in three sentences; retell a short story with props.

## Capital letters and full stop
Sentence beginnings, names; end with a full stop; copy and continue a model sentence.
