# ICSE Class 11 English - CISCE

## Language skills
Students in Class 11 learn to identify and use advanced grammatical structures like complex and compound-complex sentences, ensuring accurate subject-verb agreement and tense consistency. They master the art of writing different formats such as formal letters, emails, and reports, focusing on clarity, tone, and audience-specific vocabulary. The curriculum also includes analysing literary devices like irony, foreshadowing, and symbolism in prose and poetry to enhance comprehension. For instance, they might be asked to write a persuasive letter to the editor about local pollution or explain the theme of a given sonnet. Furthermore, students are trained to interpret and evaluate visual and written media critically to understand the intended message.

Advanced grammar in context; error-correction; transformations keeping meaning.

## Comprehension-summary-registry
Students learn to understand and summarise long passages by identifying the main idea and important details, while ignoring extra information. They must write summaries in their own words, keeping them short and to the point, usually one-third of the original length. For example, a summary of a story about a brave prince would focus on his quest and success, leaving out minor descriptions. The rules include using simple language, keeping the sequence of events, and not adding personal opinions. This skill helps them quickly grasp key points from books, articles, and exams.

Discursive passages; précis discipline; formal-informal register selection.

## Composition
In the Composition topic, students learn to write structured essays, letters, and reports with clear introductions, body paragraphs, and conclusions. They master the art of presenting arguments persuasively using facts and examples, such as writing a debate on ‘Technology in Education’ with supporting points. Grammar rules like tense consistency, correct punctuation, and sentence variety are practised to improve clarity. Students also learn to format formal letters correctly, including addresses and dates, and develop their skills in descriptive writing, like penning a vivid description of a bustling Indian market. These lessons help them communicate effectively in various real-life situations.

Essays across four types; proposals-reports; speech-script structure.

## Literature method
In Literature method, students learn to analyse texts by identifying literary devices such as simile, metaphor, and personification, and explaining how these create meaning. They practice structuring answers using the PEE technique: Point, Evidence, Explanation, to support their arguments with specific examples from the text. The curriculum also teaches students to compare and contrast different texts by examining themes, characters, and writing styles. For example, they might compare how two poets use imagery to convey a similar emotion. This method builds skills in close reading and logical expression, which are essential for examinations.

Poetry-prose-drama analysis; critical response writing; compare themes across texts.
