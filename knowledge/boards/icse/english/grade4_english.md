# ICSE Class 4 English — CISCE approach

## Nouns and pronouns expanded
Abstract nouns (honesty, joy); gender sets; pronoun-antecedent agreement.

## Verbs: tense pairs
Simple vs continuous in present and past; future with will; irregular verb bank (bring-brought, teach-taught).

## Adjectives: degrees; adverbs in place
Big-bigger-biggest; more/most for long words; adverbs of time-place-manner.

## Prepositions and conjunctions
Direction prepositions (towards, across); because-although-so joins.

## Punctuation: dialogue and commas
Speech marks with said-asked; comma lists and clauses; apostrophe possession review.

## Comprehension and vocabulary
Unseen passage answers; synonyms-antonyms; idioms in sentences (raining cats and dogs).

## Composition: letters and stories
Informal letter format; story from outlines with moral; diary entry practice.
