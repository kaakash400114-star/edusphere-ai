# ICSE Class 10 English - CISCE (Language + Literature)

## Language paper: grammar precision
All tenses in complex narration; transformation of sentences (all boards-style patterns); phrasal verbs; structures (too-to, so-that, no sooner).

## Language paper: comprehension and summary
Unseen prose: inference-vocabulary; precise summary-writing to word limit; note-taking.

## Writing: composition mastery
Argumentative-descriptive-narrative-reflective essays (400-450 words); picture composition; beginning-middle-end craft.

## Writing: letters
Formal: editor-principal-officials; informal: friendly registers; layouts marked per ICSE convention.

## Literature: prose-drama
Text-based extracts; character sketches with evidence; themes-plot-conflict; author-technique questions.

## Literature: poetry
Central idea; devices: metaphor-simile-personification-irony-alliteration; tone-mood shifts; title significance; compare two poems.

## Examination craft
Time management across Language-Literature papers; presentation: neat paragraphing, quotes selection; revision cycles.
