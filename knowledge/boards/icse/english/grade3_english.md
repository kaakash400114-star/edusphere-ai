# ICSE Class 3 English — CISCE approach

## Grammar: nouns, pronouns, adjectives
Collective nouns (swarm, bouquet); pronouns replace neatly; comparative adjectives with -er-than.

## Verbs: tenses begin
Simple present-past-future; regular -ed forms; am-is-are + -ing.

## Articles, prepositions, conjunctions
a-an-the placement; in-on-under-between; and-but-or-because.

## Punctuation: comma, apostrophe, exclamation
Lists and addresses; belonging ('s); strong feelings with !

## Vocabulary and spelling
Synonyms-antonyms; homophones (hear-here); plurals puzzles (-ies, -ves).

## Composition: guided paragraph and story
5-6 sentence paragraphs with topic sentence; complete-a-story with picture clues; read aloud with expression.
