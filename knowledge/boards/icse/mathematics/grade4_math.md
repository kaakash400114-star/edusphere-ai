# ICSE Class 4 Mathematics - CISCE approach

## Numbers to 1,00,000 and Roman numerals to 100
Place value; compare-order-round; Roman numerals with rules (no repeat beyond three).

## Four operations mastery
Large-number add-subtract; 3-digit × 2-digit; long division by 2-digits; multi-step mixed problems; estimation before solving.

## Factors, multiples, tests of divisibility
Prime-composite; HCF-LCM by listing; divisibility by 2, 3, 5, 9, 10; factor trees.

## Fractions and decimals side by side
Like-unlike compare; add-subtract like fractions; tenths as decimals; money-length decimals.

## Metric system and money
Unit conversions across length-mass-capacity; unit-price shopping problems.

## Time, speed, distance (intro)
Read 24-hour time; distance-time simple cases; timetables.

## Geometry: angles and symmetry
Measure-draw angles with protractor; types; lines of symmetry; grid pictures.

## Perimeter-area-data
Rectilinear perimeter; area by squares; bar graphs with scales.
