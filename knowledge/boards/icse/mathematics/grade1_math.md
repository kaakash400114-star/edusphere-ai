# ICSE Class 1 Mathematics - CISCE approach

## Numbers 0-100 with place value intro
Count, read, write 0-100; before-after-between; compare with < > =; tens and ones with bundles.

## Addition and subtraction to 50 (with carrying idea)
Number-line jumps; two-digit plus one-digit with carry; take-away stories with objects.

## Tables of 2, 5, 10 and skip counting
Group counting by 2s (pairs of shoes), 5s (fingers), 10s (bundles).

## Shapes: plane and solid hunt
Circle-triangle-square-rectangle; cube-cylinder-cone-sphere found at home and class.

## Measurement and money
Longer-shorter, heavier-lighter; Indian coins and ₹10-₹50 notes; simple shopping play with change.

## Time, patterns and data
O'clock and half-past; days-weeks; repeating patterns; picture collection charts.
