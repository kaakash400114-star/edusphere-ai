# ICSE Class 9 Mathematics - CISCE

## Pure arithmetic
In Class 9 Pure Arithmetic, students learn about rational and irrational numbers, understanding that rational numbers can be written as fractions while irrational numbers cannot, like √2. They practice the rules of exponents, including how to multiply and divide powers with the same base, such as 2³ × 2² = 2⁵. The topic also covers simplifying expressions using the laws of indices, like (3²)³ = 3⁶. Students solve problems involving decimals and percentages, converting between them easily, for example, changing 0.75 to 75%. Finally, they study real numbers and their properties, learning how to represent them on a number line and compare them.

Rational-irrational numbers; surds simplification; profit-loss-discount-tax; compound interest; speed-distance-time; time-work-pipes.

## Algebra
In algebra, you learn to use letters like x and y to stand for numbers and solve simple equations. You understand the rules of adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing terms with variables, such as 3x + 2x = 5x. You also expand brackets using the distributive property, like 2(x + 3) = 2x + 6, and factorise simple expressions to find common terms. For example, you learn to write 4a + 8 as 4(a + 2). Finally, you solve word problems by turning them into equations, like finding a number if twice it plus 5 equals 15.

Expansions with identities; factorisation: grouping, quadratic trinomial, sum-difference of cubes; simultaneous linear equations (graphical-algebraic); indices-exponents; logarithms: laws and use.

## Geometry
In Geometry, you will learn about different shapes and their properties. You will study angles, triangles, quadrilaterals, and circles, and how to find their areas and perimeters. For example, you will learn that the sum of angles in a triangle is 180 degrees and how to calculate the area of a rectangle using length times breadth. You will also learn about the properties of parallel lines and transversals, like corresponding angles being equal. These ideas will help you solve problems involving shapes and measurements in real life.

Triangles: congruence, inequalities; mid-point theorem; Pythagoras; parallelograms on same base-equal heights; circle: chord-tangent basics.

## Coordinate geometry
In Coordinate Geometry, students learn to plot points on a graph using two numbers called coordinates, like (3, 4). They discover how to find the distance between two points using a simple formula, such as finding the length between (2, 1) and (5, 3). The topic also teaches how to calculate the midpoint of a line segment, for example, the middle point between (0, 0) and (6, 2). Additionally, they learn about the section formula to divide a line in a given ratio, like cutting it in 1:2. Students practice drawing lines and shapes on the graph paper to understand how points connect.

Distance-section formulae; equation of a line: slope forms; graph plotting of linear-quadratic (standard shapes).

## Mensuration and trigonometry
In Class 9, you will learn to find the area and perimeter of shapes like rectangles, triangles, and circles. You will also study the surface area and volume of cubes, cuboids, and cylinders. For trigonometry, you will explore ratios like sine, cosine, and tangent for specific angles, helping you solve problems involving heights and distances. Simple real-life examples include calculating the area of a playground or the height of a building using these formulas. These concepts build your skills in measuring and solving practical problems using numbers and shapes.

Area-plane figures; surface area-volume: cone-cylinder-sphere; trigonometric ratios, standard angles, simple height-distance.

## Statistics
In Class 9, you learn to collect, group, and show data using tally marks and frequency tables to make numbers easy to understand. You will also find the average, or mean, by adding all numbers and dividing by how many there are, like finding the average marks of your class. Another important thing is the median, which is the middle number when all data is arranged in order, and the mode, the number that appears most often. You will use this to solve simple problems, like finding the most common colour of pencils in a box. Finally, you will learn to read and draw bar graphs to compare data visually, such as showing the number of students in different classes.

Mean-median-mode of raw and grouped data; graphical representation: histogram-ogive intro.
