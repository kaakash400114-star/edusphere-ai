# ICSE Class 3 Mathematics — CISCE approach

## Numbers to 10,000: place value and rounding
Ten-thousands; compare-order; round to nearest 10 and 100; Roman numerals I-L.

## Four operations with word problems
Add-subtract to 5 digits; multiply 2-3 digit by 1-digit; divide by 1-digit with remainder; choose-the-operation stories.

## Fractions on the number line
Unit fractions; compare with pictures; equivalent pairs (1/2 = 2/4); fraction of a set.

## Money: bills, change and simple budgets
Add prices; change from ₹500; saving-goal mini projects.

## Measurement: length, mass, capacity with conversions
m↔cm, kg↔g, l↔ml; measure-and-estimate trails.

## Time and calendar
Minutes-past-to; duration; leap-year idea; reading timetables.

## Geometry and data
Lines-segments; angles as turns; triangles-quadrilaterals; tally-pictograph-bar basics.
