# ICSE Class 3 Science - CISCE approach

## Living things: needs and life processes
Food-air-water-growth-reproduction in plants and animals; life cycles: butterfly, frog, bean.

## Skeletal and muscular system
Bones give shape-protection; major bones; joints move with muscles; posture care.

## Food chains in nature
Producers and eaters: grass→deer→lion; make simple chains; interdependence in a pond.

## Air, water, weather deep dive
Air composition (oxygen for burning demo); water cycle stages; clouds and rain; keep air-water clean.

## Solids, liquids, gases
Shapes and flow; changing states by heating-cooling (ice, water, steam); reversible changes.

## Light, sound and force fun
Shadows change with position; sources of sound, pitch loud-soft; push-pull makes things move-stop.
