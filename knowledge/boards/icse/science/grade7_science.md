# ICSE Class 7 Science - CISCE

## Physics: measurement-motion-force-pressure
In Physics, students learn to measure length with a ruler, mass with a balance, and time with a clock. They understand that motion is when an object changes its place, like a car moving on the road or a ball rolling down a hill. Force is a push or pull, like kicking a football or opening a door, and friction is a force that slows things down, like when a book slides across a table. Pressure is force on a small area, and we see it when a sharp pencil point makes a tiny hole while a blunt end does not, because the sharp end has more pressure.

Physical quantities-SI units; density calculations; speed-average speed; graphs of motion; pressure in solids-liquids; Archimedes idea.

## Physics: light and heat
In Physics, students learn about light and heat as forms of energy. Light travels in a straight line and can be reflected, like when you see your face in a mirror, or bent, like when a straw looks broken in a glass of water. Heat moves from hotter things to colder things, such as when your cold hands warm up near a hot stove. Shadows are formed when light cannot pass through an object, like when your body blocks sunlight to make a shape on the ground. Students also explore how different materials, like metal and wood, feel hot or cold at the same temperature because they conduct heat differently.

Reflection-laws-plane mirrors; images by concave-convex (qualitative); heat vs temperature; transfer: conduction-convection-radiation with examples.

## Chemistry: matter and changes
In Class 7, we learn about matter and its changes. Matter is anything that has weight and takes up space, like your water bottle or the air around you. We see two main changes: physical changes, where the shape or size changes but the material stays the same, like freezing water into ice; and chemical changes, where the material becomes something new, like burning wood to make ash and smoke. We also study the tiny parts of matter called atoms and molecules, which are like the smallest building blocks that join together to make all the things we see.

Element-compound-mixture; physical-chemical change indicators; separation techniques set; acids-bases-salts neutralisation; water of crystallisation (washing soda note).

## Biology: cells to organ systems
Cells are the tiny building blocks of all living things, like small rooms in a big house. In Class 7, we learn that different groups of cells make up tissues, like skin tissue. Then, tissues come together to form organs, such as the heart which helps pump blood, and the stomach which helps digest food. Finally, different organs work together to form organ systems, like the digestive system which breaks down food to give us energy. Every organ system in our body has an important job to keep us healthy and alive.

Cell structure-function; tissues types quick; plant life: photosynthesis-respiration-transpiration balance; human: digestion-nutrition details; respiration-circulation link.

## Environment and health
Students learn about the environment and how it affects our health, understanding that clean air, water, and food keep us strong. They explore sources of pollution, like smoke from cars and factories, which make the air dirty and can cause coughs and breathing problems. Children also study the importance of waste management, learning how throwing plastic and trash in the right bins keeps our surroundings clean and stops germs from spreading. They discover how plants and trees help by giving us fresh air and shade, and why we must not cut them down. Finally, they understand that a healthy environment leads to a healthy body, so keeping our surroundings clean is important for everyone.

Ecosystem-food webs; conservation practices; disease-causing microbes, prevention; first-aid essentials.
