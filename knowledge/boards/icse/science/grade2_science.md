# ICSE Class 2 Science - CISCE approach

## Food and health
In Grade 2 Science, we learn that healthy food keeps us strong and helps us grow. We need to eat different types of food, like fruits (apples, bananas), vegetables (carrots, spinach), and chapatis, to get energy. Foods like chocolates and sweets are treats and should be eaten in small amounts. We also learn that washing hands with soap before eating and after playing keeps germs away. This helps us stay healthy and not fall sick. Eating fresh food every day is good for our bodies.

Energy-food, growth-food, protection-food sorting; food from plants vs animals; good food habits.

## Animals: habitat and protection
Animals live in different places called homes, like forests for tigers and ponds for frogs. Their homes have air, water, and food to help them stay safe and grow. Some animals protect themselves by hiding in burrows or using sharp claws to stay away from danger. We must take care of animals and not hurt their homes, like not littering in parks where they live. Every animal is special and important for keeping nature balanced and healthy.

Land-water-air animals; fur-feathers-shells; how animals protect themselves (turtle shell, porcupine spines).

## Plants: kinds, parts, products
Plants are of different kinds like trees, herbs, and shrubs. Every plant has parts such as roots, stem, leaves, flowers, and fruits, each with a job to do. Roots take water from the ground, the stem carries it to other parts, and leaves make food for the plant using sunlight. Flowers help in making seeds, and fruits protect these seeds. We get many useful products from plants like fruits to eat, wood for furniture, cotton for clothes, and medicines to keep us healthy. Plants are very important for all living things.

Trees-shrubs-herbs; useful products: rubber, cotton, spices (pepper, cardamom); seeds we eat.

## Air, water and weather
Air is all around us and helps us breathe, just like how we blow out candles with air. Water is essential for drinking, bathing, and keeping plants alive, and we can find it in rivers, taps, and rains. Weather changes every day and tells us if it is sunny, rainy, windy, or cloudy, helping us choose what clothes to wear. Rain fills our rivers and helps crops grow in farms, while wind can move things like kites and fans. We learn to take care of air and water by not wasting them and keeping them clean for everyone.

Air takes space (upside-down cup test); three forms of water seen at home; seasons and festivals link.

## Safety and first aid
Children learn about keeping safe at home and outside by following simple rules like not touching hot things and crossing roads carefully. They are taught to identify common dangers such as sharp objects or poisonous plants and ask an adult for help when they see something unsafe. For small cuts or scrapes, they learn to wash the area gently with water, apply a small plaster, and tell a grown-up. This lesson also helps them memorise important emergency numbers like 108 for immediate adult assistance. The main idea is to be careful and always seek help from elders when needed.

Home-school-road safety; small injuries care; emergency numbers; strangers caution.

## Our environment care
We learn to keep our surroundings clean by not littering and using dustbins. We separate our waste into dry things like plastic and paper, and wet things like fruit peels. We must not waste water by keeping taps closed when not in use. We are taught to care for plants and animals and not pluck flowers or hurt them. We also learn to save electricity by switching off lights and fans when we leave a room.

Waste sorting; reuse jars and paper; water-electricity saving habits.
