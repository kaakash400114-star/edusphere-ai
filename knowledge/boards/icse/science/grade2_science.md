# ICSE Class 2 Science — CISCE approach

## Food and health
Energy-food, growth-food, protection-food sorting; food from plants vs animals; good food habits.

## Animals: habitat and protection
Land-water-air animals; fur-feathers-shells; how animals protect themselves (turtle shell, porcupine spines).

## Plants: kinds, parts, products
Trees-shrubs-herbs; useful products: rubber, cotton, spices (pepper, cardamom); seeds we eat.

## Air, water and weather
Air takes space (upside-down cup test); three forms of water seen at home; seasons and festivals link.

## Safety and first aid
Home-school-road safety; small injuries care; emergency numbers; strangers caution.

## Our environment care
Waste sorting; reuse jars and paper; water-electricity saving habits.
