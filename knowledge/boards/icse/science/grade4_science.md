# ICSE Class 4 Science - CISCE approach

## Plant life: parts, functions, reproduction
Root-stem-leaf-flower-fruit-seed functions; seed germination stages; dispersal methods; photosynthesis in simple words.

## Animal life: adaptation and care
Body-covering and habitat matching; migration (birds); care of pets and farm animals; endangered animals awareness.

## Human body: digestion and teeth
Tooth types and care; digestion journey with organ roles; balanced diet planning; food safety.

## Matter and its states
Solid-liquid-gas properties; melting-boiling-condensing-freezing with temperature talk; mixtures and simple separation (sieving, filtering).

## Force, work and energy
Push-pull effects; simple machines: lever-inclined plane; energy sources: sun, food, fuels; saving energy.

## Air, water and weather instruments
Air pressure tricks (sucker, syringe); water cycle; rain gauge and wind-vane idea; weather and farming.

## Our universe
Earth-sun-moon model; day-night; seasons idea; constellations spotting.
