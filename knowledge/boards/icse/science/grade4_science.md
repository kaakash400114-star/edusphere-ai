# ICSE Class 4 Science - CISCE approach

## Plant life: parts, functions, reproduction
Plants have different parts like roots, stems, leaves, flowers, and fruits. Roots help the plant drink water from the soil and hold it tightly in the ground. The stem carries water and food to all parts of the plant, like a straw. Leaves make food for the plant using sunlight, water, and air; they also help the plant breathe. Flowers make seeds for new plants to grow, and fruits protect these seeds and help them spread to new places. For example, a mango tree has roots in the soil, a strong stem, green leaves, fragrant flowers, and sweet fruits with seeds inside.

Root-stem-leaf-flower-fruit-seed functions; seed germination stages; dispersal methods; photosynthesis in simple words.

## Animal life: adaptation and care
Animals have special body parts that help them live in different places, like a camel’s hump for storing water in the desert. Some animals, such as bears, sleep for a long time during cold months to stay safe and save energy. Birds build nests to keep their eggs and babies warm and protected from rain or danger. Lions have sharp teeth and strong claws to catch their food, while cows have flat teeth for chewing plants like grass. Animals take care of their babies by feeding them and keeping them close until they can live on their own.

Body-covering and habitat matching; migration (birds); care of pets and farm animals; endangered animals awareness.

## Human body: digestion and teeth
Our body uses food to get energy, and digestion is the process that breaks down food into small pieces. Teeth help us bite and chew food; we have different kinds for cutting, tearing, and grinding. Food travels from our mouth down a tube called the food pipe to the stomach, where it mixes with juices. The stomach then sends the food to the intestines, which take out all the good parts. Eating healthy food like fruits, vegetables, and dal keeps our teeth strong and our body healthy. We must brush our teeth twice a day to keep them clean and strong.

Tooth types and care; digestion journey with organ roles; balanced diet planning; food safety.

## Matter and its states
Matter is everything around us that has mass and takes up space, like your school bag, the water you drink, and the air you breathe. Matter can exist in three main states: solid, which has a fixed shape and size like a book or a stone; liquid, which flows and takes the shape of its container like milk or juice; and gas, which has no fixed shape or size and fills up all the space available like the steam from a hot kettle or the air in a balloon. We can change the state of matter by heating or cooling it, for example, ice melts into water when heated and water turns into steam when boiled further.

Solid-liquid-gas properties; melting-boiling-condensing-freezing with temperature talk; mixtures and simple separation (sieving, filtering).

## Force, work and energy
We learn about push and pull forces, like kicking a football or opening a door. Work happens when a force makes something move, like lifting your school bag. Energy is the ability to do work, and we get energy from food to run and play. The sun gives us light and heat energy, helping plants grow. We see force, work, and energy all around us every day.

Push-pull effects; simple machines: lever-inclined plane; energy sources: sun, food, fuels; saving energy.

## Air, water and weather instruments
Children learn that air is all around us and we need it to live, like how we breathe air in and out. They discover that water exists in three forms: solid (ice), liquid (water we drink), and gas (water vapour in steam). Simple weather tools like thermometers tell us how hot or cold it is, and rain gauges measure how much rain falls. For example, a thermometer shows 30°C on a sunny day, and a rain gauge collects 5 centimetres of water during a heavy shower. Wind vanes help us see which way the wind blows, like when it moves from the sea to the land.

Air pressure tricks (sucker, syringe); water cycle; rain gauge and wind-vane idea; weather and farming.

## Our universe
Our universe is huge and has many things in it, like the sun, moon, stars, and planets. The sun is a big, hot star at the centre of our solar system and gives us light and heat during the day. Planets like Earth move around the sun in big paths called orbits; Earth takes one year to go around the sun once. The moon is Earth’s friend and it goes around Earth, lighting up the night sky. Stars are tiny points of light that twinkle in the dark night because they are very far away from us.

Earth-sun-moon model; day-night; seasons idea; constellations spotting.
