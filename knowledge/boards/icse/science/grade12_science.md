# ICSE Class 12 Science - CISCE (Physics-Chemistry-Biology essentials)

## Physics
In Grade 12 Physics, students learn the rules of electrostatics, including Coulomb's law for the force between charged particles and the concept of electric fields. They study the structure of atoms and energy levels, learning about Bohr's model and how electrons jump between these levels, giving out or absorbing light. The subject also covers the laws of thermodynamics, explaining how heat energy moves and why it cannot be completely converted into work. For example, a refrigerator uses the second law of thermodynamics to transfer heat from a cold area to a warm one. Students also explore the properties of magnetic fields and how an electric current can create a magnetic force, as seen in an electromagnet.

Electrostatics-current electricity-capacitors; magnetic effects-EMI-AC; electromagnetic waves preview; ray-wave optics incl. instruments; dual nature-atoms-nuclei; semiconductors-devices.

## Chemistry
In Class 12 Chemistry, you learn about the rates of chemical reactions and how concentration, temperature, and catalysts affect them. You also study equilibrium in reversible reactions, writing expressions for equilibrium constants and solving problems using Le Chatelier’s principle. The topic includes the chemistry of carbon compounds, learning about different functional groups like alcohols and carboxylic acids, and how to name them using IUPAC rules. You will draw structures for isomers, which are molecules with the same formula but different arrangements of atoms. Practical work involves experiments to test for these organic groups and observe reaction changes.

Solutions-colligative; electrochemistry-Nernst; kinetics; d-f block; coordination compounds; halogen-alcohol-phenol-ether-carbonyl-amine-carboxylic families; biomolecules-polymers-everyday chemistry.

## Biology
In Class 12 Biology, you learn about the structure and functions of plant tissues like xylem and phloem, and how they help in transport. You study human physiology, focusing on the nervous system, including reflex actions and the structure of neurons. Genetics covers Mendel’s laws of inheritance with examples like pea plant traits, and DNA’s role in heredity. The topic also explores reproduction in plants and humans, including processes like pollination and fertilization. Environmental biology introduces concepts like ecosystems, food chains, and conservation of natural resources.

Reproduction: flowering plants-humans; genetics-evolution: molecular basis; health-disease-immunity; biotechnology-applications; ecology: ecosystems-biodiversity-environment issues.
