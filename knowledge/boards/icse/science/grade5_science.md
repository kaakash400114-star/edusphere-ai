# ICSE Class 5 Science - CISCE approach

## Human body systems tour
In this topic, children learn about the main systems inside our bodies, like the digestive system which breaks down food, and the circulatory system which carries blood all over. They study the skeleton, the strong frame made of bones that supports our body, and muscles that help us move. Kids also learn about the lungs for breathing and the brain that controls everything, like thinking and feeling. For example, they understand that food travels through the stomach and intestines to give us energy, and the heart pumps blood to every part. Everything works together to keep us healthy and active every day.

Skeletal-joints; digestive organs and juices; breathing-lungs; heart-pulse; senses and nerves working together.

## Plant life: photosynthesis and interdependence
Plants make their own food using sunlight, air, and water in a process called photosynthesis. They use green parts called leaves to catch sunlight, tiny openings called stomata to take in air, and roots to drink water from the soil. This food gives plants the energy to grow tall and make beautiful flowers and fruits. Animals and humans depend on plants for food, like rice and vegetables, and also for oxygen to breathe. Plants and animals help each other live, which shows how all living things are connected in nature.

Leaf food-factory inputs-outputs; stomata idea; plants-animals gas exchange balance; crop growing basics.

## Food chains, webs and adaptations
In Grade 5 Science, we learn about food chains and how living things are connected. A food chain always starts with the sun helping plants grow, like grass. The grass is eaten by a rabbit, and the rabbit is eaten by a fox. Some animals eat both plants and other animals, making them omnivores. When many food chains link together, they form a food web, showing how all living things depend on each other. We also learn about adaptations, like how camels have humps to store water for deserts.

Producers-consumers-decomposers; draw webs; desert-aquatic-polar adaptations in plants and animals.

## Matter: changes, mixtures, solutions
Matter can change its form without becoming a new thing, like water turning into ice or steam. A mixture is made when two or more things are put together but can be separated easily, such as sand and salt in water. A solution is a special mixture where one thing dissolves completely in another, like sugar disappearing in water to make sweet tea. We can tell mixtures and solutions apart because solutions look the same all over and cannot be filtered, while mixtures often have parts we can see. Some changes are permanent, like burning paper, which turns into ash and cannot be changed back.

Physical-chemical changes with examples; solubility-saturation; separation: filter-evaporate-magnet; acids-bases everyday taste-touch safety framing.

## Energy: forms and simple machines
Energy is the ability to do work, and it comes in many forms like light, heat, sound, and motion. Simple machines make work easier by changing the direction or amount of force we use; they include the lever, pulley, wheel and axle, and inclined plane. A lever helps us lift heavy things using a pivot point, like a seesaw or a nutcracker. A pulley uses a rope and wheel to lift loads, like raising a flag up a pole. Machines help us do big jobs with less effort, making life simpler for everyone.

Kinetic-potential; light-sound-heat-electric energy forms; levers-pulleys-gears in devices; renewable vs non-renewable.

## The universe and our earth
The universe is a big space with the Sun, Moon, stars, and planets. Earth is one of the planets in our solar system and moves around the Sun. The Sun is a star that gives us light and heat, and the Moon moves around Earth and changes shapes. Stars are far away, and some look like dots in the sky at night. Planets like Earth and Mars are big and round but do not have their own light. We live on Earth, which has air, water, and land for plants and animals.

Solar system tour; phases of moon; eclipses idea; latitudes-longitudes and maps-intro.

## Environment: pollution and conservation
Students learn about pollution, which is when harmful substances make our air, water, or land dirty. They understand how throwing plastic bags, littering, and smoke from vehicles can harm plants, animals, and us. The class also explores conservation, which means taking care of nature by not wasting water, planting trees, and using less plastic. They see examples like turning off taps to save water or using cloth bags instead of plastic ones to keep our environment clean and healthy for everyone.

Air-water-land pollution causes-effects; 3Rs; conserve forests-water-fuel; community clean-up planning.
