# ICSE Class 6 Science - CISCE (Physics-Chemistry-Bio themes)

## Matter and its measurements
In Class 6, you will learn that matter is anything that has mass and takes up space, like your school bag or a glass of water. You will study how to measure the mass of an object using a balance in grams and its volume by counting how many small cubes fill it. The teacher will explain that matter exists in three forms: solids (like your pencil box), liquids (like ink in your pen), and gases (like the air you breathe). You will also learn to observe and compare these different types of matter by their shape, size, and how they feel.

States; physical quantities-units; measurement of length-mass-volume-time; density idea heavy-for-size.

## Forces around us
Forces are pushes or pulls that make things move, stop, or change shape. We learn about contact forces like friction, which stops a ball from rolling forever, and non-contact forces like magnetism, which can pull a paperclip without touching it. Gravity is another force that pulls everything down to the ground, like when a leaf falls from a tree. We also explore how force can change the shape of objects, such as squishing a soft ball or stretching a rubber band. Understanding forces helps us see how things work around us every day.

Contact forces; gravity pulls; friction helps-hinders; pressure in daily life examples.

## Light, heat and sound basics
Light helps us see things by travelling in straight lines. Heat makes things feel warm, like the sun or a hot cup of tea. Sound travels through air and other things, like when we hear a bell ringing or our voices. We learn that light can be blocked to make shadows, and heat flows from hotter places to cooler ones. Sound is made by vibrations, like when we pluck a guitar string.

Luminous sources; shadows-eclipses link; heat flows hot→cold; sound needs medium, pitch-loudness.

## Living world: classification seeds
Seeds are the tiny, baby parts of plants that grow into new plants. We learn to group seeds into different types like monocot seeds, which have one seed leaf like maize and wheat, and dicot seeds, which have two seed leaves like beans and grams. All seeds have three main parts: the baby plant called the embryo, the seed leaf or cotyledon that gives food, and a seed coat that protects it like a jacket. Some seeds have wings to fly, like the maple seed, while others have fluff to float in the air, like the cotton seed. We also learn that seeds need water, air, and warmth to start growing into a new plant.

Classification need; plant groups (mosses-ferns-conifers-flowering); animal groups with key examples; habitats.

## Cell, tissues preview
Cells are the tiny building blocks that make up all living things, like the bricks of a house. Different types of cells work together to form tissues, which are like groups of bricks that build specific parts, such as skin or muscle. Just as bricks come in different shapes for different walls, cells in tissues have special jobs to help the body function. For example, skin cells protect us, and muscle cells help us move. Understanding cells and tissues helps us know how our bodies work and stay healthy.

Cell as building block; microscope parts-use; plant-animal cell differences; tissues idea.

## Health, food and disease
Students learn about healthy eating and how different foods keep our bodies strong. They identify food groups like carbohydrates, proteins, vitamins, and minerals, and understand why each is important. For example, rice gives energy, milk helps bones grow, and fruits fight diseases. The lesson also explains how germs spread through dirty hands or bad food, making us sick. Finally, children are taught how simple habits, like washing hands and eating clean food, can protect them from falling ill and staying healthy.

Balanced diet components; deficiency diseases; microbes friends-foes; vaccines-immunity intro; water-air pollution basics.
