# ICSE Class 11 Science — CISCE (Physics-Chemistry-Biology essentials)

## Physics
Units-dimensions; kinematics-dynamics with calculus tools; work-energy-power; gravitation; properties of matter: elasticity-surface tension-viscosity; heat-thermodynamics laws; waves-SHM.

## Chemistry
Mole-stoichiometry; atomic structure-quantum numbers; periodicity; bonding-VSEPR-hybridisation; states of matter-gas laws; thermodynamics-equilibrium basics; redox; organic: nomenclature-isomerism-hydrocarbons reaction families.

## Biology
Diversity: five kingdoms sweep; morphology-anatomy of plants; cell structure-biomolecules-cell cycle; human physiology overview: digestion-circulation-respiration-excretion-neural-endocrine.
