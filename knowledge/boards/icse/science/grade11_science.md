# ICSE Class 11 Science - CISCE (Physics-Chemistry-Biology essentials)

## Physics
In Physics, Class 11 students learn the fundamental principles of motion, including the three laws of motion and how forces affect an object's speed and direction. They study the concept of energy and its different forms, such as kinetic energy, which is the energy a moving ball possesses. Important formulas like the one for calculating speed (distance divided by time) and Newton's second law (force equals mass times acceleration) are introduced with real-life examples like a car accelerating on a road. The curriculum also covers the structure of an atom, detailing how electrons move in fixed paths or orbits around the nucleus. Students explore the basic ideas of heat and temperature, learning how heat always flows from a hotter object to a cooler one, like a hot cup of tea cooling down to room temperature.

Units-dimensions; kinematics-dynamics with calculus tools; work-energy-power; gravitation; properties of matter: elasticity-surface tension-viscosity; heat-thermodynamics laws; waves-SHM.

## Chemistry
In Chemistry Class 11, you learn about the building blocks of matter: atoms, molecules, and chemical bonds. You will understand how atoms join together using different types of bonds, like ionic bonds where electrons are transferred and covalent bonds where they are shared. You will study the shapes of molecules, for example, why water is bent, and how to write chemical formulas to represent these compounds, like writing H₂O for water. The subject also teaches you about chemical reactions and how to balance chemical equations to show that matter is neither created nor destroyed. This helps you understand how substances interact and change in the world around you.

Mole-stoichiometry; atomic structure-quantum numbers; periodicity; bonding-VSEPR-hybridisation; states of matter-gas laws; thermodynamics-equilibrium basics; redox; organic: nomenclature-isomerism-hydrocarbons reaction families.

## Biology
In Class 11 Biology, students study the structure and functions of plant and animal cells, learning about organelles like the nucleus and mitochondria. They explore the processes of photosynthesis and cellular respiration, understanding how plants make food and how all living things produce energy. The topic covers the classification of living organisms and the basics of plant physiology, including the transport of water and nutrients. Students also learn about human anatomy, focusing on the skeletal system and the functions of different bones and joints. Genetics is introduced, with concepts like Mendel’s laws of inheritance and the structure of DNA.

Diversity: five kingdoms sweep; morphology-anatomy of plants; cell structure-biomolecules-cell cycle; human physiology overview: digestion-circulation-respiration-excretion-neural-endocrine.
