# ICSE Class 1 Science - CISCE approach

## Living and non-living with sorting activities
In this topic, children learn to tell the difference between living and non-living things by observing simple rules. Living things need food, water, air, and grow, while non-living things do not. They practice sorting by placing examples like plants, animals, toys, and books into two groups. Students also observe that living things move on their own, like a dog running or a bird flying, whereas non-living things need help to move. This helps them understand the basic characteristics of life in a simple way.

Movement, food, growth as life signs; sort pictures and objects into groups.

## Body parts and keeping clean
Children learn about different body parts like hands, feet, eyes, and nose. They are taught to keep their body clean by washing their hands with soap before eating and after playing. They also learn to brush their teeth every morning and night to keep them clean and strong. Children understand the importance of taking a bath daily to remove dirt and stay fresh. They are told to use a clean handkerchief to wipe their nose and keep their face tidy.

Head-to-toe naming; sense organs with games (touch-bag, sound box); hand-wash steps.

## Animals: homes, food, babies, sounds
Animals live in different homes like nests, dens, or burrows to stay safe. They eat different foods such as plants, meat, or insects to grow big. Many animals have babies that look small and need their parents to care for them. Animals make sounds like barks, meows, or chirps to talk to each other. Some animals give birth to live babies, while others come from eggs.

Domestic-wild-water birds; match animal to home and young one; what each eats.

## Plants: parts, needs, uses
Plants have different parts like roots, stems, leaves, flowers, and fruits. Roots hold the plant in the ground and take in water from the soil. Stems carry water to the leaves and help the plant stand straight. Leaves make food for the plants using sunlight, water, and air. Flowers make seeds that grow into new plants. We need plants for food like fruits and vegetables, and for clean air to breathe. Trees give us wood to make furniture and houses. Plants are very important for all living things.

Root-stem-leaf-flower; water-sun-air needs; shade, fruit, flowers we enjoy.

## Water and air around us
Water is all around us in many forms, like rain, rivers, and the water we drink. Air is also all around us, filling the space around us and helping us breathe. We see water turn into ice when it gets very cold and into steam when it gets very hot. Air moves and makes things like flags and leaves flutter on a windy day. Both water and air are very important for plants, animals, and us to live.

Water for drinking-washing-growing plants; keep water clean; air is everywhere, wind moves leaves.

## Weather, seasons and safety
Children observe different types of weather like sunny days, rainy days, and windy days. They learn about the four seasons—summer, winter, monsoon, and spring—and how the weather changes in each season, such as hot weather in summer and cold weather in winter. Children understand the importance of staying safe during different weathers, like carrying an umbrella in the rain and wearing warm clothes in winter. They also learn about safety rules, such as playing indoors when it is very windy and drinking water on sunny days to stay healthy.

Sunny-rainy-windy days and clothes; safety at home and on the road.
