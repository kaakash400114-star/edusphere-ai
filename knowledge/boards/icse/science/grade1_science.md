# ICSE Class 1 Science — CISCE approach

## Living and non-living with sorting activities
Movement, food, growth as life signs; sort pictures and objects into groups.

## Body parts and keeping clean
Head-to-toe naming; sense organs with games (touch-bag, sound box); hand-wash steps.

## Animals: homes, food, babies, sounds
Domestic-wild-water birds; match animal to home and young one; what each eats.

## Plants: parts, needs, uses
Root-stem-leaf-flower; water-sun-air needs; shade, fruit, flowers we enjoy.

## Water and air around us
Water for drinking-washing-growing plants; keep water clean; air is everywhere, wind moves leaves.

## Weather, seasons and safety
Sunny-rainy-windy days and clothes; safety at home and on the road.
