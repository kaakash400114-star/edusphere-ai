# ICSE Class 8 Science - CISCE

## Physics: matter-force-energy
In Class 8 Science, you learn about matter, force, and energy. Matter is anything that takes up space and has mass, like a book or a chair, and it can be solid, liquid, or gas. Force is a push or pull that makes objects move or change shape, such as kicking a football or squeezing a sponge. Energy is the ability to do work, and it comes in many forms like light from a bulb or heat from a stove. You discover how forces and energy are related when you see a ball bouncing or a simple machine like a pulley lifting a heavy load.

Kinetic theory of matter; density-relative density; pressure-upthrust-floatation; work-power-energy transformations; simple machines efficiency idea.

## Physics: light-sound-electricity
In Class 8, you will learn that light travels in straight lines and can get reflected or bent when it moves from one medium to another, like when a pencil looks bent in a glass of water. For sound, you will discover that it needs a medium like air or water to travel, and it can be reflected, which is how echoes are made. In electricity, you will study about conductors and insulators, understanding that metals allow electricity to pass while materials like rubber stop it, and you will learn to draw simple electric circuits using symbols for batteries, bulbs, and wires.

Reflection-refraction laws; lenses ray diagrams qualitative; human eye; sound: wave ideas, echo; static and current electricity; circuits-Ohm law intro; electromagnets.

## Chemistry: atomic structure and bonding
Students learn that tiny particles called atoms make up everything, and each atom has a centre with protons and neutrons, surrounded by electrons. They understand that atoms bond together to form molecules, like two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom making a water molecule. Atoms can bond by sharing electrons, as in oxygen gas, or by losing or gaining electrons, like in sodium chloride or common salt. The topic helps them see how different types of bonds create the substances we see and use every day.

Atoms-molecules-ions; atomicity; valency table; chemical formulas-equations balancing; types of reactions.

## Chemistry: elements around us
In this topic, students learn that everything around us is made of tiny building blocks called elements, like the iron in a nail and the oxygen we breathe. They discover that elements are arranged in a big chart called the periodic table, where each one has a unique name and a short symbol, such as ‘O’ for oxygen and ‘Fe’ for iron. The lesson explains that elements combine to form everything, including the water (H₂O) we drink and the carbon dioxide (CO₂) in the air, helping us understand the simple materials that make up our world.

Hydrogen-oxygen-carbon dioxide properties-tests; metals-non-metals reactivity; acids-bases-salts with indicators pH intro; water as solvent, hardness idea.

## Biology: transport, reproduction, health
In Class 8, you learn how transport happens in plants through roots, stems, and leaves, with water moving up through xylem and food moving down through phloem. You study human reproduction, learning about the male and female reproductive organs and how they help in making a baby. You also understand health and hygiene, discovering how diseases spread and why it is important to eat balanced food, stay clean, and exercise to keep your body strong and healthy. For example, washing hands helps stop germs from making you sick, and eating fruits gives you vitamins to fight diseases.

Plant transport-xylem-phloem; transpiration; human circulatory-respiratory detail; reproduction in plants-animals; adolescence changes; diseases: causes-control; crop production basics.
