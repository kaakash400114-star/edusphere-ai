# Kenyan CBC Grade 11 Mathematics
## Core Calculus and its Applications
The learner is guided to understand differentiation as the rate of change. They learn to find the derivative of algebraic, trigonometric, and exponential functions. The learner is guided to apply differentiation to solve problems involving rates of change, maximum and minimum values, such as calculating the maximum profit for a business in Nairobi or the minimum amount of flour needed to make ugali for a community event. Key formulas include the power rule, `d/dx (x^n) = nx^(n-1)`, and the chain rule for composite functions.

## Integral Calculus and Area Approximation
The learner is guided to understand integration as the reverse of differentiation, known as anti-differentiation. They are guided to find the indefinite integral of basic functions and definite integrals to calculate the exact area under a curve. The learner is guided to apply integration to find areas and volumes, for instance, calculating the area of a plot of land near Nairobi National Park or the volume of a water tank. The learner is guided to use the trapezoidal rule for numerical approximation when an exact integral is difficult to find.

## Advanced Coordinate Geometry and Vectors
The learner is guided to find the equation of a straight line in various forms, including point-slope and two-point form, and to calculate the angle between two intersecting lines. They are guided to use the distance and midpoint formulas to solve spatial problems. The learner is guided to perform basic operations on vectors, including addition, subtraction, and scalar multiplication. They are guided to apply vectors to represent forces or directions, such as plotting the route of a boda boda on a map using vector components.

## Statistical Analysis and Probability Distributions
The learner is guided to calculate measures of dispersion, including variance and standard deviation, for both grouped and ungrouped data. They are guided to understand and apply the properties of the normal distribution curve. The learner is guided to use the normal distribution to estimate probabilities, for example, determining the probability of a farmer's coffee yield falling within a certain range of bags or the probability of a boda boda driver's daily earnings being above a certain amount of Kenyan shillings.

## Advanced Trigonometry, Identities and Equations
The learner is guided to recall and use trigonometric identities, including the Pythagorean, compound angle, and double-angle formulas. They are guided to solve trigonometric equations within a given domain, such as finding all angles `θ` between 0° and 360° that satisfy an equation. The learner is guided to apply these concepts to practical problems, like calculating the height of a building using angles of elevation and depression or determining the most efficient angle for a tuk-tuk ramp to be built.

## Financial Mathematics and Annuities
The learner is guided to solve problems involving simple and compound interest. They are guided to use the formula for the future value of an annuity to calculate the total amount saved after regular deposits, such as a cooperative society's monthly contribution for buying a matatu. The learner is guided to find the present value of an annuity, which is used to determine the lump sum needed to fund a future stream of income, like calculating the capital required to provide a retirement stipend in Kenyan shillings.
