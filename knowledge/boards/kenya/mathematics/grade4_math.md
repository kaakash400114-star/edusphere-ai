# Kenyan CBC Grade 4 Mathematics
## Numbers and Operations
The learner is guided to read, write, and order numbers up to 99,999. They are taught to use place value to determine the value of each digit in a number. The learner is guided to add and subtract four-digit numbers using the column method, for example, 4,578 + 2,123 = 6,701. They are taught to multiply a three-digit number by a one-digit number, for example, 345 x 3 = 1,035. The learner is guided to solve simple division problems by sharing or grouping, using real-life examples like dividing 24 mangoes among 6 friends.

## Measurement
The learner is guided to measure length, mass, and capacity using appropriate units. They are taught to measure the length of a desk in metres and centimetres. The learner is guided to measure the weight of a bag of maize in kilograms and grams. They are taught to measure the capacity of a water container in litres and millilitres. The learner is guided to solve problems involving time, such as calculating the duration of a bus journey from Nairobi to Mombasa or converting between hours and minutes.

## Geometry
The learner is guided to identify and name properties of 2D and 3D shapes. They are taught to identify circles, squares, rectangles, and triangles and count their sides and corners. The learner is guided to identify cubes, cuboids, cylinders, and spheres. They are taught to create patterns using different shapes and colours. The learner is guided to find lines of symmetry in simple shapes, like the line of symmetry in an ugali plate or the Kenyan flag.

## Data Handling
The learner is guided to collect, organize, and interpret data using tally marks and tables. They are taught to conduct a simple survey, such as asking classmates about their favourite wild animal in Nairobi National Park. The learner is guided to record this information in a tally table. They are taught to create and interpret simple bar graphs to display their findings. The learner is guided to answer questions based on the data, such as "Which animal is the most popular?"

## Fractions
The learner is guided to recognize and name fractions like halves, thirds, and quarters. They are taught that a fraction represents a part of a whole, like half a loaf of bread. The learner is guided to find fractions of a set or a whole number, for example, finding one-quarter of 20 shillings. They are taught to identify equivalent fractions, such as recognizing that 1/2 is the same as 2/4. The learner is guided to solve simple word problems involving fractions in everyday contexts.

## Algebra and Patterns
The learner is guided to create, extend, and describe number patterns. They are taught to follow a rule, such as adding 5 each time (e.g., 2, 7, 12, 17). The learner is guided to identify and continue shape patterns. They are taught to solve simple number sentences with a missing number, for example, 12 + _ = 20. The learner is guided to use letters to represent unknown values in simple real-life problems, like calculating the fare for a boda boda trip if the fare is 50 shillings per kilometre.
