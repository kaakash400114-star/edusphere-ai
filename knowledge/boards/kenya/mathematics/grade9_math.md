# Kenyan CBC Grade 9 Mathematics
## Numerical Skills and Number Theory
The learner is guided to apply the four operations to integers and fractions, including mixed numbers, with emphasis on accuracy. They are taught to find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) and Highest Common Factor (HCF) of two numbers using prime factorisation. Learners explore laws of indices, specifically expressing numbers in standard form and performing calculations. They are guided to solve problems involving real-world quantities such as calculating the total cost of 3.5kg of flour at Ksh 120 per kg or determining how many people can share 50 mandazis equally.

## Algebraic Expressions and Equations
The learner is guided to simplify algebraic expressions by expanding brackets and collecting like terms. They are taught to solve linear equations in one unknown, including equations with variables on both sides. Learners explore factorisation of expressions with common factors, difference of two squares, and trinomials. They are guided to form and solve linear equations from real-life situations, such as finding the number of boda boda trips needed to earn Ksh 5,000 if each trip pays Ksh 250.

## Commercial Arithmetic
The learner is guided to calculate percentage increase and decrease in various contexts like price changes or profit and loss. They are taught to compute simple and compound interest, using the formula A = P(1 + r/100)^n, and compare the two. Learners explore rates, ratios, and proportions, such as mixing concrete in a ratio of 4:2:1 (cement, sand, ballast). They are guided to solve problems involving hire purchase, calculating instalment amounts and total cost for items like a fridge.

## Geometry and Measurement
The learner is guided to calculate the area and perimeter of various shapes including triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, and composite figures. They are taught to find the volume and surface area of prisms, cylinders, and pyramids. Learners explore the properties of angles in circles, including subtended angles and cyclic quadrilaterals. They are guided to solve problems by calculating the cost of fencing a rectangular plot measuring 40m by 30m at Ksh 800 per metre.

## Trigonometry
The learner is guided to identify and use the three basic trigonometric ratios (sine, cosine, tangent) in right-angled triangles. They are taught to find the size of an unknown side or angle in a right-angled triangle using these ratios. Learners explore applications of trigonometry in real-life scenarios, such as calculating the height of a tree or the distance across a river like the Athi. They are guided to solve problems involving angles of elevation and depression in contexts like viewing animals at Nairobi National Park from a hilltop.

## Data Handling and Probability
The learner is guided to collect, organise, and represent data using frequency tables, bar charts, line graphs, and pie charts. They are taught to calculate measures of central tendency, including mean, mode, and median for both grouped and ungrouped data. Learners explore simple probability as a measure of chance, expressed as a fraction or decimal, using examples like finding the probability of picking a sweet ugali flour packet from a basket containing different items.
