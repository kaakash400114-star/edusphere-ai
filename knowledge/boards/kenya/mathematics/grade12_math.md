# Kenyan CBC Grade 12 Mathematics

## Advanced Algebraic Equations and Inequalities
The learner is guided to solve complex equations involving rational expressions and indices, including fractional and negative exponents. They will master the method of solving quadratic equations using the quadratic formula, `x = [-b ± √(b² - 4ac)] / 2a`, and apply it to real-world problems such as calculating the profit margin for a matatu business. The learner is also guided to solve simultaneous equations with two variables and graphically represent inequalities, using a budget for family ugali consumption as a practical example.

## Calculus: Differentiation and Its Applications
The learner is guided to understand differentiation as a process of finding the rate of change of a function. They are guided to find the derivatives of algebraic functions using first principles and standard rules. The learner is then guided to apply differentiation to solve problems, including calculating the instantaneous speed of a boda boda on a Nairobi highway and determining the maximum area of a rectangular plot of land near Nairobi National Park given a fixed perimeter.

## Calculus: Integration and Its Applications
The learner is guided to understand integration as the reverse process of differentiation. They are guided to find the indefinite integral of algebraic functions and apply definite integrals to find the area under a curve. The learner is guided to solve practical problems such as calculating the total distance travelled by a commuter train from Mombasa to Nairobi and finding the total cost of producing a certain number of items given a marginal cost function, all in Kenyan shillings.

## Vectors in Two and Three Dimensions
The learner is guided to define and perform operations on vectors, including addition, subtraction, and scalar multiplication in both two and three dimensions. They are guided to represent vectors using magnitude and direction. The learner is guided to apply vector concepts in solving problems involving forces and displacement, such as calculating the resultant force on a construction site crane or determining the shortest flight path from Kisumu to a game lodge in the Maasai Mara.

## Probability and Statistics
The learner is guided to calculate and interpret probabilities for both discrete and continuous random variables, using binomial and normal distributions. They are guided to distinguish between theoretical and experimental probability. The learner is guided to apply statistical methods to analyse data, for instance, calculating the average daily revenue for a small shop in a Kenyan market and using this data to make predictions about future earnings and stock requirements.

## Trigonometry, 3D Geometry, and Loci
The learner is guided to solve problems in three-dimensional geometry, including finding angles and lengths in pyramids and prisms. They are guided to apply trigonometric identities and the sine and cosine rules to solve triangles. The learner is guided to find the locus of points satisfying given conditions, such as the path taken by a security light sweeping a section of a compound or the set of all points within a 500-metre radius of a community borehole in a rural Kenyan village.
