# Kenyan CBC Grade 7 Mathematics
## Numeration and Place Value
The learner is guided to understand place value up to billions. They identify the value of each digit in a number, such as in 24,581,396. The learner is guided to read and write numbers in words and figures, for example, writing "Five million, seven hundred and twenty thousand" as 5,720,000. They are guided to use the symbols >, <, and = to compare numbers. In Kenya, they can compare the cost of a boda boda ride (KSh 100) with a school trip to Nairobi National Park (KSh 2,500), understanding that 100 is less than 2,500.

## Operations on Whole Numbers
The learner is guided to perform the four operations on whole numbers: addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. They are guided to solve word problems involving these operations. For example, calculating the total cost of 50 kg of maize flour at KSh 120 per kg. The learner is guided to apply the order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS) to evaluate expressions like 20 + 5 × 4. They are also guided to find the average of numbers, such as finding the average daily sales of 100 bags of maize over a week.

## Fractions and Decimals
The learner is guided to compare, order, and simplify fractions. They are guided to perform addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division on fractions using common denominators. For example, calculating the amount of flour used for ugali from 2 kg if 1/4 is used for ugali and 1/2 for other purposes. The learner is guided to convert fractions to decimals and percentages. They are guided to solve problems like, what percentage of a 500m sprint has been run after 250 meters?

## Measurement and Money
The learner is guided to use standard units of length (metre, kilometre), mass (gram, kilogram), and capacity (litre, millilitre). They are guided to convert between units, for example, converting 3,500 grams to kilograms. The learner is guided to understand and use the Kenyan currency, the shilling (KSh), in real-life contexts. They are guided to calculate total costs, such as the price of 5 exercise books at KSh 150 each and 2 pens at KSh 50 each, finding the total bill is KSh 850.

## Geometry and Shapes
The learner is guided to identify and describe properties of 2D and 3D shapes. They are guided to name polygons up to decagon, regular and irregular polygons, and solids like prisms and pyramids. The learner is guided to understand lines, angles (acute, obtuse, right), and parallel lines. They are guided to calculate the perimeter of rectangles, triangles, and squares. For example, calculating the perimeter of a rectangular garden measuring 20 metres by 15 metres.

## Data Handling and Graphs
The learner is guided to collect, record, and organize data. They are guided to represent data using bar graphs, line graphs, and pie charts. For instance, showing the number of tourists visiting Nairobi National Park each month. The learner is guided to interpret information from graphs and charts to make simple conclusions. They are guided to find the mean, mode, and median of a set of data, such as finding the average number of boda boda passengers passing a specific point in one hour.
