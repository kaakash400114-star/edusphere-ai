# Kenyan CBC Grade 2 English
## Reading and Comprehension of Simple Texts
The learner is guided to read aloud simple sentences and short texts using the basic skills of phonics. They learn to blend letter sounds to form words like *cat*, *mat*, and *sun*, and to identify common sight words. The learner is guided to use knowledge of letters and sounds to read and understand level-appropriate texts. They read simple stories and answer basic questions about the main character or an event. For example, they can read "Mama bought ugali" and tell you what Mama bought.

## Writing and Spelling of Common Words
The learner is guided to write letters of the alphabet in both uppercase and lowercase forms, ensuring correct formation. They are guided to write their own name and other simple, high-frequency words correctly. Using phonics skills, the learner is guided to listen to the sounds in words and write them down, like *pen*, *dog*, and *kitten*. For example, they can write the sentence "The dog is big" by sounding out each word and matching it to the correct letters.

## Listening and Speaking Skills
The learner is guided to listen carefully to short stories, poems, and simple instructions from the teacher. They are guided to show understanding by following the instructions, such as "Give the book to your friend." The learner is also guided to speak clearly to share their own ideas and experiences in complete sentences. For example, they can say, "I visited Nairobi National Park," or "I saw a tall giraffe."

## Vocabulary Development
The learner is guided to understand and use new words that describe objects, people, places, and actions. They are guided to name common objects found at school and at home, like *pencil*, *book*, and *plate*. They are also guided to understand and use simple money words, identifying coins like a one-shilling coin and a five-shilling coin. For example, they can say, "This coin is one shilling."

## Basic Grammar and Sentence Structure
The learner is guided to form simple sentences to express a complete idea. They are guided to use capital letters at the beginning of a sentence and a full stop at the end. The learner is guided to understand and use common words that show position, such as *on*, *in*, *under*, and *next to*. For example, they can write or say, "The ball is under the chair."

## Appreciation of Stories and Rhymes
The learner is guided to listen to and enjoy folk tales, stories, and poems. They are guided to identify characters, settings, and simple events in a story. The learner is guided to show appreciation for the story by asking questions or retelling the main parts in their own words. For example, after hearing a story about a helpful boda boda rider, they can say, "The man was kind."
