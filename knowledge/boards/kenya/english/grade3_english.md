# Kenyan CBC Grade 3 English

## Reading for Meaning with Familiar Stories
The learner is guided to read and understand short, familiar stories and texts with simple pictures and predictable events. They will identify the main idea and specific details from a story. They will use pictures and context clues to understand unfamiliar words. For example, after reading a story about a family eating ugali for dinner, the learner can state the main event and point to the picture showing the food. They will read simple words with consonant-vowel-consonant (CVC) patterns like 'cat', 'dog', and 'sun'.

## Writing Simple Sentences About My World
The learner is guided to write short, complete sentences to express their own ideas and experiences. They will learn to form letters correctly and use basic capitalization and full stops. They will use their knowledge of sounds to write words. For example, they can write, "I see a lion at Nairobi National Park" or "My father uses 100 shillings to buy mandazi." They will write sentences in response to questions about their life, family, and community.

## Listening Attentively to Stories and Instructions
The learner is guided to listen carefully to stories, poems, and simple instructions. They will be able to identify the main character and the setting in a short story. They will follow two-step oral instructions given by the teacher. For example, when told, "First, stand up. Second, touch your head," the learner can perform both actions correctly. They will listen politely when their friends are speaking during group activities and recall key details from a story, like what the main character did.

## Speaking Clearly to Share My Ideas
The learner is guided to speak clearly and express their ideas in complete sentences. They will describe familiar objects, people, and events using simple descriptive words. For example, they can say, "The boda boda is fast," or "This banana is yellow." They will ask and answer questions about pictures of everyday life. They will participate in group discussions, sharing their thoughts on topics like their favourite games or food, and use appropriate volume and speed when talking to their teacher and classmates.

## Using Common Words in My Language
The learner is guided to recognize, read, and use common high-frequency words in simple sentences. These are sight words that are often used. They will practice reading words like 'the', 'and', 'I', 'go', and 'see'. They will write these words correctly in their sentences. For example, they can complete a sentence frame by writing, "I can see a car." They will identify these words in their reading books and begin to understand their meaning in context without having to sound them out.

## Understanding Basic Punctuation and Sounds
The learner is guided to understand the purpose of basic punctuation marks when reading. They will recognize that a full stop (.) ends a sentence and a question mark (?) asks a question. They will apply this knowledge when reading aloud. They will also identify and produce basic sounds of the English language, including short vowel sounds. For example, they can distinguish the short 'a' sound in 'apple' from the short 'e' sound in 'egg'. They will clap the number of syllables in simple words like 'fam-i-ly'.
