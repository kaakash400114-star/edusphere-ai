# Kenyan CBC Grade 4 English

## Listening and Speaking Skills
The learner is guided to listen attentively to oral texts, identify the main idea, and sequence events. They will use appropriate gestures and maintain eye contact when speaking. Learners will express themselves clearly in complete sentences, sharing experiences like a trip to Nairobi National Park. They will ask and answer questions using polite forms. They will listen to and follow multi-step instructions, such as how to prepare a simple meal. Examples include giving directions from one classroom to another and describing the process of making ugali for a family meal.

## Reading for Comprehension
The learner is guided to read and understand a variety of texts with increasing fluency. They will identify the main idea and supporting details in a paragraph. Learners will make simple inferences, predicting what might happen next in a story. They will distinguish between fiction and non-fiction texts. They will use contextual clues to understand the meaning of new words, for example, guessing the meaning of 'safari' from a picture book. They will read to follow instructions, like a recipe for making chapatis or a list of rules for visiting the Nairobi Railway Museum.

## Writing and Creative Expression
The learner is guided to write clear and well-structured short paragraphs. They will write on a given topic, such as 'A Day at the Beach' or 'My Fruits', using appropriate punctuation and capitalization. Learners will use descriptive words to make their writing more interesting. They will write simple stories with a beginning, middle, and end. They will write simple poems and messages. Examples include writing a letter to a pen pal describing a boda boda ride or creating a poster about the importance of saving shillings for a new toy.

## Grammar and Language Structure
The learner is guided to use basic parts of speech correctly. They will identify and use nouns for people, places, and things (e.g., teacher, Kenya, desk). They will use action verbs to describe what someone or something does (e.g., run, eat, read). Learners will use adjectives to describe nouns (e.g., tall building, sweet mango). They will use common adverbs of time and place (e.g., yesterday, at home). They will use conjunctions like 'and', 'but', and 'or' to join simple sentences, for example, 'We visited the park and we saw many animals.'

## Vocabulary Development
The learner is guided to learn and use new words in different contexts. They will identify common prefixes and suffixes like 'un-', 'dis-', and '-ful', '-less' to understand word meanings. Learners will group words into categories, such as animals, food, and transport. They will match words with their meanings and opposites. They will use a simple dictionary to find the meaning of unfamiliar words. Examples include learning words related to Kenyan culture, like 'kiondo', 'matatu', and 'nyama choma', and using them in sentences.

## Study Skills and Information Handling
The learner is guided to use reference materials to find information. They will use a dictionary, an atlas, and a simple encyclopedia to locate facts. Learners will identify the difference between facts and opinions in texts. They will use alphabetical order to find words in a glossary. They will identify parts of a book, such as the title page, table of contents, and index. Examples include finding information about the Kenyan flag, looking up the price of common items in a supermarket in shillings, or locating the Maasai Mara on a map of Kenya.
