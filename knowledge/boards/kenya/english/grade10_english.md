# Kenyan CBC Grade 10 English

## Literary Appreciation of Prose
The learner is guided to analyse characterisation and themes in a prescribed prose text. They learn to identify static and dynamic characters and provide textual evidence to support analysis. Character motivation is explored using specific examples from the text. Learners are expected to evaluate how characters' actions advance the plot and develop central themes, such as conflict and resolution within a Kenyan societal context.

## Appreciation and Interpretation of Poetry
The learner is guided to understand the structure and devices in classical and modern poetry. They learn to identify rhyme scheme, stanzas, and figurative language like metaphor, simile, and personification. Learners interpret poems by analysing the speaker's tone and mood using specific lines as evidence. They connect the poet's message to universal human experiences, drawing parallels to contemporary Kenyan social issues.

## Reading Skills for Advanced Comprehension
The learner is guided to apply reading strategies to complex texts. They learn to determine the main idea and distinguish between supporting details and primary arguments. Learners infer unstated meanings using contextual clues and identify the author's purpose and bias. They evaluate the credibility of sources and synthesise information from different paragraphs to form a coherent understanding of challenging passages, potentially related to topics like conservation or Kenyan history.

## Writing Skills: Formal and Functional Communication
The learner is guided to write functional and creative pieces with precision. They learn to compose well-structured formal letters of complaint or inquiry using correct salutations and closings. For creative writing, they develop narratives focusing on plot development, setting, and character dialogue, using local contexts like a bustling Nairobi market or a journey through Nairobi National Park as inspiration for their stories.

## Communication Skills and Oral Literature
The learner is guided to engage in effective verbal communication and appreciate oral traditions. They learn to prepare and deliver a persuasive speech with a clear introduction, body, and conclusion. They practise using appropriate non-verbal cues to reinforce their message. Learners also study a Kenyan oral narrative, identifying its moral lesson and stylistic devices, and can recite a short excerpt with clarity and expression.

## Grammar, Usage, and Mechanics
The learner is guided to master grammatical structures for effective expression. They learn to use complex sentences correctly, including subordinate clauses, and apply punctuation like semicolons and colons. Subject-verb agreement in complex sentences is reinforced. Vocabulary expansion focuses on precise word choice using synonyms and antonyms, including Kenyan-specific terms like 'boda boda' or 'matatu', and learners learn to use the comma correctly to separate introductory phrases and items in a series.
