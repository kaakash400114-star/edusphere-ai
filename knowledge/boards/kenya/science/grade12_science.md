# Kenyan CBC Grade 12 Science

## Electrochemical Cells and Electrolysis
The learner is guided to explain the operation of simple voltaic cells, identifying the anode (oxidation) and cathode (reduction). They learn to construct cells using different electrodes like zinc and copper, calculating the standard electrode potential (E°) to determine cell voltage. Using an electrolyte of potassium chloride (KCl), they are shown how to measure current flow in a circuit. For example, connecting a copper anode and a zinc cathode generates a voltage of approximately 1.10 volts, sufficient to power a small LED light, demonstrating practical energy conversion.

## Inheritance and Genetic Variation
The learner is guided to analyse patterns of inheritance using Mendelian laws. They apply concepts of genotype and phenotype to solve Punnett square problems involving dihybrid crosses. They learn about mutations, using sickle cell anaemia as a prime example of a gene mutation that affects red blood cells. Students are guided to calculate the probability of offspring inheriting a trait, such as the likelihood of a child having a certain blood group from parents with genotypes AO and BO, making it applicable in clinical scenarios like those found in Kenyan hospitals.

## Kinematics and Dynamics in Motion
The learner is guided to apply Newton's laws of motion to solve problems of linear and rotational movement. They calculate displacement, velocity, and acceleration using the equations of motion, for instance, determining how long it takes a boda boda to accelerate from 0 to 30 km/h. Using a force diagram, they calculate the net force required to push a 100 kg bag of maize flour up a ramp at an angle of 30 degrees, linking theoretical physics to practical, everyday activities in Kenyan life.

## Organic Chemistry and Industrial Processes
The learner is guided to name and draw the structures of key organic compounds, including alkanes, alkenes, and alcohols. They learn about fractional distillation of crude oil, identifying products like petrol, kerosene, and diesel used across Kenya. They study the addition polymerisation process, understanding how polyethene is manufactured for making water pipes. Students also learn about fermentation, explaining how yeast converts sugarcane juice to ethanol, a process relevant to local industries.

## The Human Endocrine System
The learner is guided to identify major endocrine glands, such as the pituitary, thyroid, and adrenal glands, and explain their hormone secretion. They study the feedback mechanism of insulin and glucagon in blood sugar regulation, using a scenario of a student's glucose level after a meal of ugali and sukuma wiki. The learner learns to explain the role of adrenaline in the 'fight or flight' response, which could be triggered by a sudden encounter with a lion at Nairobi National Park.

## Wave Properties and Optics
The learner is guided to apply the wave equation v = fλ to calculate properties of sound and light waves. They study the formation of images using lenses and mirrors, solving problems to find the image distance for an object placed 20 cm from a converging lens with a 15 cm focal length. They learn about the Doppler effect, explaining how the pitch of a siren changes as a police vehicle drives past, connecting wave theory to real-life auditory experiences familiar to Kenyan students.
