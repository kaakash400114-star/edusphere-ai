# Kenyan CBC Grade 8 Science

## Understanding Cells and their Organisation
The learner is guided to identify the cell as the basic structural and functional unit of living organisms. They will learn to differentiate between plant and animal cells, identifying the nucleus, cytoplasm, and cell membrane in both, plus the cell wall and chloroplasts in plant cells. The learner is guided to understand the levels of organisation from cells forming tissues, tissues forming organs, and organs forming organ systems. For example, skin cells form skin tissue, which is part of the integumentary organ system protecting the body.

## Energy Transfer in Food Chains and Food Webs
The learner is guided to define a food chain as a sequence showing how energy is transferred from one organism to another, using arrows to indicate the direction of energy flow. They will construct simple food chains, for instance, grass (producer) → grasshopper (primary consumer) → lizard (secondary consumer) → hawk (tertiary consumer). The learner is guided to describe a food web as a network of interconnected food chains, demonstrating that organisms can occupy multiple trophic levels, like a lizard eating both grasshoppers and flies.

## Types of Mixtures and their Separation Techniques
The learner is guided to define a mixture as a combination of two or more substances that are not chemically combined. They will distinguish between heterogeneous mixtures, like a pile of sand and pebbles, and homogeneous mixtures, like saltwater. The learner is guided to use appropriate separation techniques: using a sieve to separate sand and gravel, decantation to separate mud from water, evaporation to obtain salt from seawater, and filtration to separate dust from air in a boda boda workshop.

## Properties and Characteristics of Sound Waves
The learner is guided to define sound as a form of energy produced by vibrating objects that travels as a wave through a medium like air, water, or solids. They will learn that sound waves have properties including pitch (determined by frequency) and loudness (determined by amplitude). The learner is guided to give examples: a high-pitched sound from a whistle and a low-pitched sound from a drum. They will learn that sound cannot travel through a vacuum, such as in outer space.

## Principles of Electricity and Circuits
The learner is guided to define electricity as the flow of electric charge, typically through a conductor like a metal wire. They will learn that a complete circuit, consisting of a power source (like a battery), connecting wires, and a load (like a bulb), is required for an electric current to flow. The learner is guided to identify symbols for components in a circuit diagram, such as a cell, bulb, switch, and connecting wires, and to draw simple series circuits, for example, lighting two bulbs from a single battery source.

## Reproduction in Plants and its Importance
The learner is guided to describe sexual and asexual reproduction in flowering plants. In sexual reproduction, pollen from the anther is transferred to the stigma, leading to fertilisation and the formation of seeds within a fruit. In asexual reproduction, plants like the sugarcane plant can reproduce from stem cuttings. The learner is guided to explain the importance of seed dispersal by agents like wind, water, and animals. For example, the seeds of the African mahogany tree have wings and are dispersed by wind.
