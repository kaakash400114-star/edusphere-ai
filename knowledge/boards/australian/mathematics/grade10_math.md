# Australian Curriculum (ACARA) Grade 10 Mathematics

## Algebraic Techniques
Students expand and simplify a variety of algebraic expressions, including binomial products, perfect squares, and the difference of two squares. They apply the four operations to simple algebraic fractions with numerical denominators and factorise monic quadratic expressions to solve equations of the form x² + bx + c = 0. Pupils use the distributive law to expand expressions like 3(a + 2b) or (x + 4)² and find the product of two linear binomials such as (x + 5)(x - 2).

## Linear and Non-linear Relationships
Students graph linear relations and solve linear and quadratic equations, including those involving simple algebraic fractions. They find the gradient and equation of a straight line from its graph and use the quadratic formula to find solutions for equations of the form ax² + bx + c = 0. Learners solve simultaneous linear equations graphically and algebraically, and determine the x- and y-intercepts of parabolas and straight lines to sketch accurate representations of these functions.

## Measurement and Geometry
Students apply formulas to calculate the areas of composite shapes and the surface areas and volumes of prisms, cylinders, and spheres. They use Pythagoras’ theorem and trigonometric ratios to solve problems involving right-angled triangles in real-world contexts, such as calculating the height of a flagpole or the distance across a river. Pupils convert between units of measurement for volume and capacity, including cubic centimetres to litres, and apply these skills to problems involving the cost of materials for construction projects.

## Probability and Statistics
Students list outcomes for multi-step experiments and assign probabilities to these outcomes. They construct two-way frequency tables and use them to calculate conditional probabilities and identify associations between categorical variables. Learners investigate the effect of individual data values, including outliers, on measures of spread such as the range and standard deviation. Pupils also critique reports in the media that may misrepresent statistical data, ensuring a critical understanding of information presented to the public.

## Money and Financial Mathematics
Students model and solve problems involving compound interest, using repeated application of simple interest or the formula for compound interest. They compare the cost of different loan options, including those with varying interest rates and fees, and calculate the effective annual interest rate. Learners solve budgeting problems for personal or business finance, such as planning a school fete or managing a household budget, and use tables and graphs to represent financial data and analyse trends over time.

## Trigonometry
Students use trigonometric ratios to solve problems involving angles of elevation and depression, and apply the sine and cosine rules to find unknown sides and angles in non-right-angled triangles. They use Pythagoras’ theorem and trigonometry to solve practical problems, such as calculating the length of a diagonal support for a roof or the distance between two points on a map. Pupils also explore the relationship between the unit circle and the trigonometric functions, connecting abstract concepts to real-world applications like surveying and navigation.
