# Singapore Curriculum (MOE) Grade 8 Mathematics  
## Algebraic Expressions and Formulae  
Students will learn to simplify algebraic fractions involving linear and quadratic expressions. They will expand and factorise quadratic expressions, including the difference of two squares. Students will solve quadratic equations by factorisation, completing the square, and using the quadratic formula. They will manipulate algebraic formulae, including finding the subject of a formula, to solve for unknowns. Real-life contexts, such as calculating the area of a plot of HDB land, are used to demonstrate application.  

## Linear Equations and Inequalities  
Students will solve linear equations with fractional coefficients and fractional answers. They will formulate and solve linear equations from word problems, using the bar method to represent relationships. For inequalities, students will solve simple linear inequalities in one variable and represent the solution set on a number line. They will also solve simultaneous linear equations by substitution and elimination, applying these to problems involving costs at a hawker centre.  

## Angles, Triangles, and Polygons  
Students will apply the properties of angles associated with parallel lines and transversals. They will understand and use the angle sum properties of triangles and quadrilaterals. Students will solve problems involving congruent and similar triangles using their properties and the concept of scale factors. The properties of special quadrilaterals are explored, and problems involving bearings and angles of elevation and depression are solved, often related to navigation within the MRT network.  

## Mensuration  
Students will solve problems involving the area of plane figures, including circles, sectors, and composite shapes. They will find the volume and surface area of prisms, pyramids, cones, and spheres. Problems are frequently set in real-world scenarios, such as determining the volume of water in a cylindrical HDB reservoir or calculating the material needed for a new pyramid-shaped landmark in Singapore.  

## Data Analysis and Probability  
Students will calculate mean, median, and mode for ungrouped data and use these to make inferences. They will interpret and construct box-and-whisker plots to analyse data distribution. For probability, students will understand the basic principles, calculate probabilities of single events, and use relative frequency for experimental probability. A typical example involves analysing the popularity of different hawker centre dishes based on sales data.  

## Coordinate Geometry and Graphs  
Students will plot and interpret linear and quadratic graphs on the coordinate plane. They will find the gradient of a line and the equation of a straight line given two points. Students will use linear graphs to solve simultaneous equations graphically. Quadratic graphs are used to model real-life situations, such as the trajectory of a lift in a HDB building or the profit curve of a business at a local market.
