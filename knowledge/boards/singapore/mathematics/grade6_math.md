# Singapore Curriculum (MOE) Grade 6 Mathematics

## Whole Numbers
Grade 6 students extend their understanding of whole numbers, mastering operations with numbers up to 10 million. They learn to solve multi-step problems involving the four operations, applying the order of operations (BODMAS) correctly. Students are taught to use estimation, including rounding off to the nearest tens, hundreds, thousands, or ten thousands, to check the reasonableness of their answers. For instance, a problem could involve calculating the total number of Singaporean commuters using the North-South MRT line over a week, given the daily passenger figures.

## Fractions
Students deepen their knowledge of fractions by learning to perform the four operations on mixed numbers and improper fractions. They are taught to simplify fractions to their lowest terms and find the common denominator for addition and subtraction. Key concepts include multiplying a fraction by a whole number and dividing a fraction by another fraction or a whole number. A practical example is determining the fraction of a plate of HDB heritage chicken rice left after sharing it among four family members, and then calculating how much more is needed to feed an additional two people.

## Decimals
In this topic, students master decimal operations, including addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. They learn to round decimals to a specified number of decimal places and express answers in the correct degree of accuracy. The concept of standard index form (scientific notation) for very large or small numbers is also introduced. An example problem involves calculating the total cost of buying 2.5 kilograms of chilli crab and 0.75 kilograms of black pepper crab at market prices, ensuring the final answer is rounded to two decimal places for currency.

## Measurement and Geometry
Students explore area and volume of composite shapes, learning to calculate the area of triangles, parallelograms, and trapeziums, as well as the volume of cubes and cuboids. They are taught to convert metric units for length, mass, and volume. The relationship between area and perimeter is reinforced. For example, a student might calculate the area of an HDB void deck to determine the number of tables needed for a festive market, or find the volume of a water tank in a HDB block to ensure sufficient water supply.

## Data Analysis
Grade 6 students learn to read, interpret, and analyse data from tables, bar graphs, line graphs, and pie charts. They are taught to find the average (mean) of a set of data and understand its significance. Students solve problems involving the comparison of data sets and identify trends. An application is analysing the average number of bowls of laksa sold by a hawker centre stall each day to determine the weekly revenue and project future sales.

## Ratio and Percentage
Students solidify their understanding of ratios by solving problems involving three quantities and converting between ratios, fractions, and percentages. They learn to find percentages of a quantity and express one quantity as a percentage of another. Concepts include percentage increase and decrease applied to real-world contexts such as calculating GST on purchases or discounts during a Great Singapore Sale. A typical problem would involve finding the ratio of prices of different items at a local wet market and determining the percentage change in the price of chicken rice over a year.
