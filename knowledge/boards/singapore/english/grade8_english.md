# Singapore Curriculum (MOE) Grade 8 English
## Reading and Viewing for Meaning
Students will develop the ability to comprehend and evaluate complex written and visual texts. They will learn to infer authors' intentions, evaluate arguments, and identify persuasive techniques. Students will analyse how language is used in texts, such as news articles and public notices, to convey specific messages. They will learn to differentiate between fact and opinion and to understand the impact of cultural and social contexts on meaning, using local examples like public notices on MRT stations or articles on heritage conservation.
## Writing for a Purpose and Audience
Students will learn to write for a variety of purposes and audiences, focusing on clarity, organisation, and appropriate tone. They will structure expository and argumentative texts effectively using clear topic sentences and supporting details. Students will expand their vocabulary to use precise and engaging language. They will learn to adapt their writing style for different contexts, such as a formal letter to a town council about an HDB estate issue or a persuasive article for a school newsletter on hawker centre preservation.
## Grammar and Vocabulary Development
Students will refine their understanding of English grammar, focusing on complex sentence structures and varied syntax. They will learn to use subordinate clauses, participial phrases, and conditional sentences to add sophistication to their writing. Vocabulary building is key, with an emphasis on using precise, context-specific words and avoiding colloquialisms. Students will be taught to use contextual clues to understand and infer the meaning of new words, such as those found in instructions for cooking a local dish or descriptions of a cultural festival.
## Listening and Speaking Skills
Students will develop effective listening and speaking skills for academic and social contexts. They will learn to listen actively to understand main ideas, key details, and speakers' attitudes. They will practise articulating their thoughts clearly and confidently in group discussions and oral presentations. Students will learn to use appropriate rhetorical devices and persuasive language. They will be taught to present viewpoints on relevant topics, such as the importance of national service or the challenges of urban living in Singapore, with clarity and logical structure.
## Critical and Creative Thinking
Students will learn to engage in critical and creative thinking when analysing and producing texts. They will evaluate information from multiple sources and form reasoned judgments. They will learn to generate and explore original ideas, fostering creativity in writing and discussion. Students will be encouraged to connect themes and ideas from texts to their own experiences and the wider Singaporean context, such as exploring the cultural significance of food courts or the urban planning of their neighbourhoods.
## Language Use in Context
Students will understand how language varies according to context, purpose, and audience. They will learn to distinguish between formal and informal registers and to use them appropriately. They will explore the features of different text types, such as reports, speeches, and online communications. Students will examine how language shapes perceptions of identity and community, analysing examples from local media, government publications, or everyday conversations to understand the nuances of communication in a multicultural society like Singapore.
