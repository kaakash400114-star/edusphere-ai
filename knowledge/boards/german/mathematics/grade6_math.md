# German (Lehrplan) Grade 6 Mathematics

## Natural Numbers and Calculations
Students solidify their ability to perform extended calculations with natural numbers. This includes multi-digit multiplication and division using written algorithms. They learn the rules for order of operations (Punkt vor Strich), ensuring correct results in complex expressions like 3 + 4 × 5. Students practice estimating outcomes to check reasonableness. Real-world application involves calculating the total distance of a multi-day Autobahn trip or determining the total cost of several Bundesliga match tickets, requiring precise addition and multiplication.

## Fractions and Decimal Numbers
This section introduces mixed numbers and improper fractions, focusing on converting between them. Students learn to find the least common denominator for adding and subtracting fractions with different denominators, such as calculating the total weight of ingredients for a Christmas market recipe. They also multiply and divide fractions by whole numbers and other fractions. Decimal numbers are extended to thousandths, with an emphasis on ordering them and performing all four basic arithmetic operations, including a practical example of calculating the change from a €50 note for multiple purchases.

## Geometry and Spatial Sense
Students explore the properties of two-dimensional shapes, including the circle, calculating its area and circumference using the formulas A = πr² and U = 2πr. They classify triangles and quadrilaterals based on angles and side lengths. The concept of scale and scale drawings is introduced, applying it to create a map of a city park or interpret the scale of a model Autobanhnsign. Basic compass-and-ruler constructions of perpendicular lines and angle bisectors are practiced to develop precision.

## Ratio and Percentage
The unit focuses on understanding ratios as a comparison of two quantities, often using examples from the Bundesliga such as the ratio of wins to losses for a team. Students learn to express ratios in simplest form. They are introduced to percentages as fractions with a denominator of 100, focusing on converting between fractions, decimals, and percentages. Real-world applications include calculating percentage discounts on items at a Christmas market or determining what percentage of students in a class prefer a certain football club.

## Equations and Their Solutions
Students solve simple linear equations in one variable, such as 2x + 5 = 15. They learn to isolate the variable by performing inverse operations on both sides of the equation. The focus is on understanding the principle of balance in an equation. Problems are framed in real-world contexts, for instance, finding the original price of an item after a percentage discount or determining how many tickets each person must sell to reach a fundraising goal, translating the situation into an algebraic equation.

## Data and Probability
This section covers creating and interpreting various data displays, particularly bar charts and pie charts, to represent information like the favourite types of food at a school event. Students learn to calculate the arithmetic mean (average) from a set of data. The basic concept of probability is introduced as a measure of likelihood, described using words like 'certain', 'likely', 'unlikely', and 'impossible'. A simple example could be determining the probability of picking a red marble from a bag containing a known number of coloured marbles.
