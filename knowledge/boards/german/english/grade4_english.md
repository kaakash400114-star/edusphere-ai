# German (Lehrplan) Grade 4 English
## Introducing Myself
Pupils learn to introduce themselves and others using full sentences. They practice saying their name, age, and where they live, for example: "My name is Tim. I am nine years old. I live in Berlin." They also learn to ask others about their family, using simple questions like "Do you have a brother?" and responding with "Yes, I have one brother. His name is Tom." The class uses maps of Germany to point out their home cities and neighbouring states like Brandenburg.

## Daily Routines and Time
Students learn to talk about their daily schedule using the simple present tense. They master key phrases for telling time, focusing on full and half hours. They learn to say "I get up at 7 o'clock" and "I have lunch at 12:30". Pupils practice using both the 12-hour clock and the 24-hour clock, common for timetables. They create a visual timeline of their school day, from "aufwachen" (waking up) to "ins Bett gehen" (going to bed), and use real examples like the 8:15 start time of their school.

## Food and Shopping
This unit focuses on food vocabulary and basic shopping dialogue. Students learn the names of common foods, including "Brötchen", "Wurst", and "Schokolade". They practice phrases for buying items, such as "I would like two rolls, please." and "How much does it cost?" The lesson integrates the euro currency, with pupils calculating simple prices, for example: "One apple is 60 cents. Two apples are 1.20 euros." They role-play buying snacks at a Christmas market stall.

## Hobbies and Free Time
Pupils learn to discuss their free time activities using the sentence structure "I play...", "I watch...", and "I like...". They acquire vocabulary for popular hobbies, including "Fußball spielen" (playing football), "Bundesliga schauen" (watching the Bundesliga), and "Radfahren" (cycling). Students create short sentences about themselves, like "I like riding my bike in the park." The class also discusses famous German athletes and teams to provide cultural context.

## Transport and Travel
This unit introduces vocabulary for different modes of transport, focusing on common German examples. Students learn words like "Auto", "Zug" (train), "Bus", and "Fahrrad". They practice forming sentences about travelling, for example: "I go to school by bus." A key learning point is the word "Autobahn", with pupils learning the correct pronunciation. They compare travel times using a simple map, calculating distances like "The train to Hamburg takes two hours."

## Seasons and Weather
Students learn to name the four seasons and describe typical weather for each. They master phrases such as "In winter, it is cold and snowy." and "In summer, the sun shines and it is warm." The vocabulary includes "sonnig" (sunny), "regnerisch" (rainy), and "neblig" (foggy). They connect weather to German traditions, like visiting Christmas markets in winter or going to the "Freibad" (outdoor pool) in summer. Pupils create a weather diary for a week.
