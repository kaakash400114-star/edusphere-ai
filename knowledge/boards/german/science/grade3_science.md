# German (Lehrplan) Grade 3 Science

## The Senses
Humans have five senses to perceive the world: sight, hearing, touch, taste, and smell. Sight uses eyes to see light and colour. Hearing uses ears to detect sound waves. Touch uses skin to feel texture, temperature, and pressure. Taste uses the tongue to identify sweet, sour, salty, and bitter flavours. Smell uses the nose to recognize scents. For example, you can see the red of a Christmas market stall, hear the sound of a brass band, feel the smooth surface of a euro coin, taste the sweetness of Lebkuchen, and smell the scent of mulled wine, or Glühwein.

## Materials and Their Properties
Materials have different properties like hardness, flexibility, and transparency. Hard materials, like wood or stone, resist scratching. Flexible materials, like rubber or fabric, can bend without breaking. Transparent materials, like glass or clear plastic, allow light to pass through, so you can see objects on the other side. Opaque materials, like metal or a brick, block light. For example, the Autobahn is made of hard, opaque asphalt, while a football jersey is flexible and often made of a fabric that may be slightly transparent.

## Basic Forces and Motion
Forces are pushes or pulls that make objects move or change shape. Gravity is a force that pulls everything down towards the Earth's surface. Friction is a force that opposes motion, like when you rub your hands together. A force can make a stationary object move, like kicking a football. It can also change the speed of a moving object, like slowing down a bicycle. For example, a Bundesliga player uses a strong force with their foot to kick a football, and friction between the ball and the grass helps it slow down.

## Day and Night
The Earth's rotation on its axis causes day and night. As the Earth spins, different parts face the Sun. The side facing the Sun experiences daylight, while the side facing away experiences night. One complete rotation takes about 24 hours, which is one day. For example, when it is daytime and sunny in Munich, allowing people to visit a Christmas market, it is nighttime in a city on the opposite side of the world.

## Plants and Their Needs
Plants are living organisms that need sunlight, water, and air to grow and stay healthy. Sunlight is used by the leaves in a process called photosynthesis to make food. Water is absorbed by the roots from the soil. Air, specifically a gas called carbon dioxide, is taken in by the leaves. A plant without any of these will not survive. For example, a sunflower in a field needs sun to grow tall, needs rainwater for its roots, and takes in carbon dioxide from the air to create its own food.

## Weather and Seasons
Weather is the condition of the air at a certain time, such as sunny, rainy, windy, or snowy. Seasons are the four main periods of the year: spring, summer, autumn (fall), and winter. The seasons change because the Earth is tilted as it orbits the Sun. In Germany, summer is usually warm and sunny, good for a Bundesliga match, while winter can be cold with snow, perfect for a traditional Christmas market.
