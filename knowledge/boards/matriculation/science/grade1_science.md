# Matriculation Class 1 Science

## Living and non-living

Living things grow, eat, and reproduce. Non-living things like rocks and chairs do not grow or eat. We can identify living things by checking if they move and breathe. A plant is living because it grows and makes seeds, while a stone is non-living because it cannot move on its own. Animals like cats and dogs are living because they eat food and have babies. Water and soil are non-living, but they are important for living things to grow.

## Our body and sense organs

Our body has different parts like the head, hands, legs, and stomach. We use our sense organs—eyes, ears, nose, tongue, and skin—to see, hear, smell, taste, and feel things. Our eyes help us see colours and shapes, while our ears help us listen to sounds. The nose helps us smell good and bad things, and the tongue helps us taste sweet, salty, or bitter food. Our skin helps us feel if something is soft, hard, hot, or cold. We must take care of our body and sense organs to stay healthy.

## Animals around us

Animals are living things that move, eat, grow, and need air, water, and food just like us. We learn about different animals based on where they live, such as farm animals like cows and goats, and wild animals like lions and tigers. Some animals have feathers, like parrots and chickens, while others have fur, like cats and dogs. Many animals give birth to live babies, called young ones, like a mother cat giving birth to kittens. We also learn about helpful animals like the cow that gives us milk, which we drink every day.

## Plants around us

Plants are living things that grow in soil and need sunlight, water, and air to live. They have roots to hold them in the ground and absorb water, a stem to stand tall, and leaves that make food from the sun. We see different plants around us like small grass, tall trees, and colourful flowers. Plants give us many useful things like fruits to eat, wood to make furniture, and clean air to breathe. They are very important for all living things on our Earth.

Plants are living things that grow in gardens, pots, and forests. They have roots, a stem, branches, and leaves to help them grow. Plants need sunlight, water, and air to make their own food. Trees, bushes, and small plants are all around us, giving us oxygen. We see flowers, fruits, and vegetables coming from different plants.

## Food and water

Food is the thing we eat to grow strong and give us energy. Water is the thing we drink to keep our bodies healthy and cool. We must eat different kinds of food, like dal, roti, rice, vegetables, and fruits, to stay healthy. We should always wash our hands before eating food to keep germs away. We need to drink clean water every day to stop us from getting sick.

## Clothes we wear

We wear clothes to cover our bodies and stay safe from heat, cold, and dirt. Different clothes are worn for different seasons like woollen sweaters in winter and cotton clothes in summer. We also wear special clothes for different occasions, such as a uniform to school or a festive dress for celebrations. Clothes keep us clean and protect our skin from getting hurt or sunburnt. It is important to keep our clothes clean by washing them regularly.

## Home and school

Children learn about different places in their school, such as the classroom, playground, and library, and the rules to follow in each, like sitting quietly in class and sharing toys in the play area. They identify common objects at home and school, like tables, chairs, and books, and understand their uses, such as reading books and eating on plates. Students also learn about simple safety rules, like holding an adult’s hand when crossing the road and not touching sharp or hot objects. They explore the roles of people in their school, such as teachers who teach and helpers who clean, and how everyone works together to keep the school safe and happy.

## Air, water, weather

Air is all around us and we need it to live, just like we need water. We see water as rain in the sky, in rivers on the ground, and in a glass to drink. Sun makes air warm and cold, which we feel as hot and cold weather, and the wind is moving air. We also see clouds in the sky which are made of tiny drops of water, and rain comes from these clouds. Different kinds of weather are sunny, rainy, and windy days.

## Safety and health

Children learn basic safety rules to keep themselves healthy and safe. They understand why washing hands with soap before eating and after playing is important to stay well. They are taught to cross the road only at zebra crossings while holding an adult's hand. Children also learn about healthy foods like fruits and vegetables that help them grow strong and stay healthy. They know to tell a trusted adult if they feel hurt or see something dangerous to stay safe.

## Helpers and transport

Children learn about different helpers who assist us in our daily lives, like doctors who keep us healthy, teachers who teach us in school, and postmen who deliver letters and parcels. They understand the importance of transport for moving from place to place, including buses, trains, autos, and aeroplanes, each serving a specific purpose. Students are introduced to basic safety rules, such as looking for zebra crossings to cross roads safely and wearing seatbelts in cars. This topic helps young children identify community helpers and various modes of transport, fostering an awareness of how people and vehicles contribute to our community.
