# Matriculation Class 2 Science

## Food groups and health

Children learn about five main food groups: grains, pulses, vegetables, fruits, milk, and meat. These groups help our body grow big and strong. Grains like rice and chapatti give us energy. Vegetables like carrots and spinach make us healthy. Fruits like apples and bananas give us vitamins. Milk helps our bones become strong. We must eat foods from all groups every day to stay well and not get sick.

## Body and movement

We learn about our body parts and how they help us move. We can touch our head, hands, legs, and feet, and know their names. Our bones give our body shape and support, like the small bones in our fingers and the big bones in our legs. Muscles help us move our body parts, for example, we use muscles to walk, run, and pick up toys. We also learn how to take care of our body by eating healthy food, drinking milk, and sleeping well to stay strong and active.

## Animal homes and young ones

Children learn about different homes that animals live in to protect themselves and their babies. They study examples like a bird’s nest in a tree, a rabbit’s burrow in the ground, and a spider’s web on plants. The topic also teaches about animal babies, such as kittens from cats, puppies from dogs, and chicks from hens, and how their parents take care of them. Simple vocabulary is used, focusing on familiar animals and their living places. Children are encouraged to observe and describe these homes and young ones in their surroundings.

Animals live in different homes called shelters. Some animals live in burrows like rabbits, while others build nests on trees, like birds. The young ones of animals are called babies. For example, a cat has kittens and a cow has calves. Animals care for their babies to keep them safe and help them grow.

## Plant types and uses

Plants are living things that grow and need sunlight, water, and air to live. There are different types of plants: trees are tall and have thick trunks, like neem and mango; herbs are small and soft, like coriander and mint; and shrubs are medium-sized with woody branches, like rose and bougainvillea. We use plants for many things, such as food like fruits and vegetables, medicine like tulsi for coughs, and materials like cotton for clothes. Plants also give us oxygen to breathe and provide homes for animals.

## Water: sources and safety

Water is a very important resource for all living things. We get water from different sources like rivers, lakes, wells, and underground tanks. Water from these sources is not always clean and can have germs that make us sick. We learn to keep water safe by boiling it or adding two drops of chlorine liquid before drinking. We always wash our hands with clean water and soap before eating food to stay healthy and avoid getting sick from dirty water.

## Air around us

Air is all around us, filling the empty spaces in our environment. It is a mixture of gases, including oxygen which helps us breathe and live, and carbon dioxide which plants take in for making food. We cannot see air, but we can feel it when it moves as wind, or when it blows our hair and the leaves on trees. Air occupies space; for example, when we blow air into a balloon, it gets bigger because the air fills it up. Without air, there would be no life on Earth.

## Seasons of Tamil Nadu: monsoon

Tamil Nadu has two main monsoon seasons: the northeast monsoon and the southwest monsoon. The northeast monsoon brings most of the rain to Tamil Nadu, usually from October to December. This heavy rainfall fills rivers, lakes, and ponds, helping farmers grow crops like rice and sugarcane. The southwest monsoon from June to September gives less rain to Tamil Nadu but helps in keeping the land green. Children learn how rain is important for all living things and how different seasons change the weather around us.

Tamil Nadu gets rain from two monsoons. The northeast monsoon brings heavy rain from October to December, helping crops grow. The southwest monsoon gives less rain from June to September. We feel cool air during the monsoon season. Trees and plants look green and fresh after the rain. Rain fills our rivers, ponds, and lakes with water.

## Safety and first aid

We learn about safety at home and school, like not touching hot things or electrical sockets. We understand that we must always walk carefully on the stairs and never run near water. For first aid, we are taught to tell a grown-up immediately if we get a small cut or a bruise, and we learn that we should put a clean bandage on it only after a mother or teacher checks it. We also practice making a small noise like shouting "help!" if we are in trouble and need assistance from an adult.

## Our neighbourhood

Children learn to identify different places in their neighbourhood, such as homes, schools, shops, and parks. They understand the importance of staying safe by following simple rules, like holding a grown-up's hand while crossing the road. They recognise the roles of people in the community, like the postman who delivers letters and the doctor who helps us when we are sick. Children also learn to keep their neighbourhood clean by not littering and using dustbins. They are introduced to maps to find their way around familiar places, like locating their classroom or the playground.

## Festivals and clean surroundings

Festivals are happy times when families come together to celebrate. Clean surroundings are important for our health and to keep germs away. We should not throw garbage on the roads or in parks. We always use a dustbin to throw things like banana peels, used paper, and plastic bags. During festivals like Diwali, we clean our homes to welcome guests and make the environment tidy. Keeping our surroundings clean helps everyone stay healthy and happy.
