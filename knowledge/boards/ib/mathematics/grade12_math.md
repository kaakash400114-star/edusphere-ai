# International Baccalaureate (IB) Grade 12 Mathematics

## Further Calculus and Differential Equations
Learners investigate the advanced techniques of integration, including integration by parts, trigonometric substitution, and the use of partial fractions. They apply these methods to solve problems involving areas, volumes, and centers of mass. The study of differential equations is deepened, focusing on first and second-order linear equations, their solutions, and applications in modeling real-world phenomena like population growth or mechanical vibrations, with a global example in epidemiological modeling.

## Discrete Mathematics
Learners investigate the fundamental concepts of graph theory, including properties of graphs, trees, and planar graphs. They study algorithms for finding shortest paths and minimum spanning trees, with a key example being the optimization of global shipping routes or internet data flow. This section also covers combinatorial principles such as permutations, combinations, and the Binomial Theorem, and their application in probability and coding theory for secure international communications.

## Mathematical Induction and Proof
Learners investigate the principle of mathematical induction as a formal proof technique for statements involving positive integers. They construct proofs for divisibility rules, summation formulas, and inequalities. This skill is essential for establishing the validity of mathematical theorems. The analysis extends to proof by contradiction and counterexamples, examining classic problems like the infinitude of primes, fostering a deep appreciation for rigorous, logical reasoning in a global mathematical discourse.

## Vectors and Matrices in Three Dimensions
Learners investigate vector operations, including the scalar (dot) and vector (cross) products, and their geometric interpretations in three-dimensional space. They use these to determine angles, areas, and volumes. The study of matrices extends to finding eigenvalues and eigenvectors, which are crucial for understanding transformations and systems of linear differential equations, with an application in computer graphics for three-dimensional modeling and animation used worldwide.

## Probability and Distributions
Learners investigate advanced probability, focusing on conditional probability, Bayes' Theorem, and discrete and continuous probability distributions, including the binomial, Poisson, and normal distributions. They apply these concepts to analyze real-world data, such as assessing financial risk or modeling sensor accuracy in scientific research. This equips them to interpret data and make informed predictions, a key skill in fields ranging from actuarial science to global climate modeling.

## Mathematical Applications in a Global Context
Learners investigate how mathematical modeling is applied to solve complex global challenges. They analyze functions, sequences, and series to model phenomena like compound interest in international finance or carbon emission trends over time. This culminating section emphasizes the power of mathematics as a universal language, connecting abstract concepts to tangible global issues such as sustainable development, resource management, and economic policy analysis.
