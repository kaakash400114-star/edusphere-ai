# International Baccalaureate (IB) Grade 7 Mathematics

## Number Systems and Operations
Students expand their understanding of numbers to include positive and negative integers, fractions, and decimals. They learn to perform all four operations with these types of numbers, including multi-step calculations and the use of the order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS). Learners investigate the concept of absolute value and apply it to solve problems involving distance on a number line, such as calculating temperature variations from a baseline point in different global cities like Moscow and Cairo.

## Algebraic Expressions and Equations
Learners explore how to use variables to represent unknown quantities in real-world and mathematical contexts. They simplify and expand algebraic expressions, combining like terms using the distributive property. Students investigate how to formulate and solve one-variable linear equations, translating word problems into algebraic statements. For example, they determine the cost of several items when given the total cost and the price of one item, applying inverse operations to isolate the variable.

## Ratio, Proportion, and Percentages
Students deepen their understanding of proportional reasoning through ratios, rates, and percentages. They solve problems involving scale drawings, map distances, and unit conversions between measurement systems. Learners investigate how percentages represent parts of a whole and are used in contexts such as calculating discounts, taxes, and statistical data. They apply their knowledge to interpret demographic data, such as literacy rates in different countries, and solve problems involving simple and compound interest.

## Geometric Properties and Measurement
Students analyze properties of two-dimensional and three-dimensional shapes, focusing on angle relationships, area, and surface area. They learn to calculate the circumference and area of circles and the volume of prisms and cylinders. Learners investigate how geometric concepts are applied in design and architecture, such as calculating the amount of material needed to build a structure. They also explore the properties of parallel and transversal lines, identifying angles formed and applying these principles to solve for unknown angle measures.

## Data Handling and Probability
Students learn to collect, organize, and represent data using various methods, including bar graphs, line graphs, and pie charts. They calculate and interpret measures of central tendency, such as mean, median, and mode. Learners investigate the concept of probability through experiments and simulations, determining the likelihood of events occurring. They analyze global datasets, like climate statistics or migration patterns, and use their data analysis skills to draw informed conclusions and make predictions based on evidence.

## Integers, Rational Numbers, and Coordinates
Learners extend the number line to include all integers and rational numbers, understanding their order and relative magnitude. They plot ordered pairs on a coordinate grid to represent geometric shapes and solve problems involving distance and direction. Students investigate the concept of slope and its application to real-world contexts, such as calculating the gradient of a hill. They use coordinate geometry to analyze routes on a world map, calculating distances between major international cities using the Cartesian plane.
