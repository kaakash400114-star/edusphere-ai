# South African (CAPS) Grade 4 Science

## Life and Living: Life Cycles of Animals
Learners are able to describe the life cycles of different animals. They learn that insects like butterflies go through four stages: egg, larva (caterpillar), pupa (chrysalis), and adult (butterfly). Frogs also change, starting as eggs that hatch into tadpoles with tails. Tadpoles grow legs and lose their tails to become adult frogs. These changes are called metamorphosis. Birds like the Proteas lay eggs that hatch into chicks. A chick grows feathers until it is a fully grown adult bird. All animals grow and change to become adults.

## Life and Living: Plants and Their Parts
Learners are able to identify and explain the functions of the main parts of a plant. A plant has roots, a stem, leaves, and sometimes flowers or fruit. Roots anchor the plant in the soil and absorb water. The stem carries water and food to other parts of the plant and holds it up. Leaves make food for the plant using sunlight, water, and air through a process called photosynthesis. Flowers help the plant make seeds, which can grow into new plants. Plants like those found near Table Mountain have all these parts to help them live and grow.

## Materials and Change: States of Matter
Learners are able to describe the three states of matter: solid, liquid, and gas. A solid, like a rock or a desk, keeps its shape. A liquid, like water or milk, can be poured and takes the shape of its container. A gas, like the air we breathe, fills all the space it is in. Matter can change from one state to another. When you heat ice (solid), it melts to become water (liquid). If you heat water enough, it boils and turns into steam (gas). When you cool steam, it turns back into water.

## Materials and Change: Properties of Materials
Learners are able to group materials based on their properties. Properties are how materials look, feel, or behave. Materials can be hard or soft. Metals are usually hard and shiny, while cloth is soft. Some materials, like plastic and wood, can be bent. Materials can also be waterproof, like the roof of a house, or absorbent, like a sponge used to clean up a spill. A braai grid is made of metal because metal is strong and can handle high heat. These properties help us choose the best material for a job.

## Energy and Change: Energy from the Sun
Learners are able to explain that the sun is a source of energy. The sun gives us heat and light energy. We use this energy in different ways. Solar panels turn sunlight into electricity that can power lights or a small TV. The sun helps plants grow, which gives us food. We can also feel the sun's warmth on a cool day. We must be careful not to stay in the sun for too long without protection, like a hat or sunscreen, because too much sun can harm our skin.

## Earth and Beyond: The Day and Night Cycle
Learners are able to describe how day and night are caused. The Earth spins around an imaginary line called an axis. It takes 24 hours for the Earth to make one full spin. While the Earth spins, one side faces the sun, and it is daytime there. The other side is away from the sun, and it is nighttime. This is why we have day and night. When it is daytime in South Africa, it is nighttime in countries on the other side of the world, like in Europe. The cycle repeats every day, giving us regular periods of light and dark.
