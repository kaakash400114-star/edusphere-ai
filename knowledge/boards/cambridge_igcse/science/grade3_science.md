# Cambridge IGCSE Grade 3 Science  
## Living Things and Their Needs  
Living things are plants and animals. They need food, water, air, and shelter to live and grow. Plants make their own food using sunlight, water, and air. Animals get food by eating plants or other animals. A plant needs sunlight for photosynthesis. A bird needs a nest for shelter. Water helps plants stay upright. A fish needs water to breathe with its gills. Living things change as they grow, like a seed becoming a tall tree or a tadpole becoming a frog.  

## Materials and Their Properties  
Materials have different properties. Wood is hard and comes from trees. Plastic is flexible and used to make bottles. Metal is strong and good for making pots. Glass is transparent and used for windows. We group materials by their uses. A metal spoon is good for stirring hot soup because it is strong. A wool sweater keeps us warm because it is soft. Paper comes from trees and is used for writing. Some materials, like cotton, come from plants and are good for making clothes.  

## Forces and Movement  
Forces make things move or change shape. Push and pull are types of force. A push can make a ball roll away. A pull can open a door. Gravity is a force that pulls things down, making a ball fall to the ground. Magnets can push or pull without touching. They attract some materials, like iron. Friction is a force that slows things down. Rubbing your hands together creates friction and makes them warm. A bicycle uses force to move the wheels.  

## Light and Shadows  
Light helps us see. It comes from the sun, a lamp, or a fire. Light travels in straight lines. When an object blocks light, it makes a shadow. Shadows are dark shapes. The size of a shadow changes if the light source is closer or farther. A torch held close to a toy makes a big shadow. A held far away makes a small shadow. Opaque objects, like a book, block all light. Transparent objects, like clear glass, let light pass through.  

## Sound and Hearing  
Sound is made when things vibrate. A guitar string vibrates to make music. A drum vibrates when hit. Sound travels through air, water, or solids. We hear with our ears. The outer ear catches sound. The sound travels to the eardrum, which vibrates. Loud sounds are like a drum; quiet sounds are like a whisper. High sounds are like a bird chirping; low sounds are like a dog barking. Sound cannot travel in space because there is no air.  

## The Earth and Sky  
The Earth is a large planet with land, water, and air. Land has hills, mountains, and plains. Water has oceans, rivers, and lakes. The sun heats the Earth during the day. At night, it gets cooler. The moon orbits the Earth and changes its shape over time. Stars are far away in the sky and twinkle at night. Weather changes daily, like sunny, rainy, or windy days. Rain helps plants grow, and wind can make flags move.
