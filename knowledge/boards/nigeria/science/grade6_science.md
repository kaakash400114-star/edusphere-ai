# Nigerian (NERDC) Grade 6 Science

## The Human Body and Health
The human body is made up of systems that work together. The skeletal system provides support and protection, with 206 bones in adults. The circulatory system transports oxygen using the heart and blood vessels. A healthy diet rich in local foods like yam, beans, and fish provides energy and nutrients. Regular exercise strengthens muscles and the heart. Good hygiene, such as washing hands with clean water and soap, prevents the spread of germs. This helps prevent diseases like malaria and typhoid fever, ensuring the body functions well.

## Matter and its Properties
Matter is anything that has weight and occupies space. It exists in three main states: solid, liquid, and gas. A solid has a definite shape and volume, like a stone. A liquid has a definite volume but takes the shape of its container, like water in a calabash. A gas has neither a definite shape nor volume, like the air we breathe. Changes of state involve adding or removing heat; for example, water (liquid) turns to steam (gas) when boiled, which is essential for cooking jollof rice.

## Forces and Motion
A force is a push or a pull that can make an object move, stop, or change direction. Gravity is a force that pulls objects towards the Earth's centre. Friction is a force that opposes motion between surfaces; it allows us to walk and grip a steering wheel. Simple machines like levers, pulleys, and inclined planes make work easier. For example, using a long plank as a lever can help lift a heavy load, reducing the effort needed compared to lifting it directly with bare hands.

## Energy and its Sources
Energy is the ability to do work. It exists in different forms, including heat, light, sound, and electrical energy. The main sources of energy are renewable, like solar and wind, and non-renewable, like crude oil and coal. In Nigeria, crude oil is a major energy source that is refined to power vehicles and generators. The sun is a primary source of energy for plants during photosynthesis. We must conserve energy by switching off lights and electronics when not in use to preserve these resources for the future.

## Weather and Climate
Weather is the condition of the atmosphere at a specific time, including temperature, rainfall, and wind. In Nigeria, the harmattan season brings cold, dry winds and dusty haze from the Sahara. The rainy season brings heavy downpours essential for agriculture. Climate is the average weather pattern of a region over many years. Lagos, in the south, has a tropical climate with high temperatures and rainfall all year. Observing weather patterns helps farmers decide the best time to plant crops like maize and millet.

## Living Organisms and their Environment
All living things interact with their environment in ecosystems. An ecosystem includes all the plants, animals, and non-living things in an area, like the water, soil, and rocks. Producers, such as grass and trees, make their own food. Consumers, like grasshoppers and goats, eat other organisms. Decomposers, such as fungi and bacteria, break down dead matter. For example, a kola nut tree provides food for people and animals, while its fallen leaves are broken down by decomposers, returning nutrients to the soil.
