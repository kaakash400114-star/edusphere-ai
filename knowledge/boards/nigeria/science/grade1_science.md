# Nigerian (NERDC) Grade 1 Science

## Our Body and the Senses
Children learn the main external body parts. They identify the head, neck, shoulders, arms, hands, chest, waist, legs, and feet. They understand the five senses: sight (eyes), hearing (ears), smell (nose), touch (skin), and taste (tongue). Children observe that eyes help us see objects like a ball, ears help us hear sounds like a bell, skin helps us feel things like rain, a nose helps us smell food like jollof rice, and the tongue helps us taste things like yam.

## Living and Non-Living Things
Children learn to classify things as living or non-living. Living things grow, eat, move, and reproduce. Examples include plants like maize in a garden and animals like goats. Non-living things do not grow, eat, or move. Examples include stones, tables, and a school bag. Children can identify that a kola nut is a living thing that grows on a tree, while a naira coin is a non-living thing used for buying things at the market.

## Our Environment and Weather
Children observe different types of weather in their environment. They learn sunny, rainy, and windy (harmattan) days. They associate weather with activities; we play football on sunny days, use umbrellas on rainy days, and wear warm clothes during harmattan. Children describe the sky, for example, "The sky is grey today because it is going to rain in Lagos," or "The harmattan wind is blowing and the air is dry."

## Materials and Their Uses
Children identify common materials and their properties. They learn about wood (hard, from trees) used for doors, metal (hard, shiny) used for pots, plastic (light, colourful) used for cups, and cloth (soft) used for uniforms. They understand materials are chosen based on what they are used for. A plastic cup is light and safe for drinking water, while a metal pot is strong for cooking soup on a fire.

## Plants Around Us
Children observe that plants grow from seeds. They identify parts of a plant, including the roots, stem, leaves, and flowers. They know plants need sunlight, water, and soil to grow. Children can name common plants found in Nigeria, such as maize, yam, and mango trees. They understand that trees provide shade, fruits to eat like mangoes, and are homes for birds.

## Caring for Our Environment
Children learn that keeping our environment clean and healthy is important. They understand that we should not drop litter like sweet wrappers or plastic bottles on the ground. They know dirty water can make people sick, so we should not throw rubbish into rivers. Children practice sorting waste, for example, putting paper and plastic into a waste bin to help keep their school and community clean.
