# Nigerian (NERDC) Grade 10 Science

## Energy Transformation
Energy cannot be created or destroyed but can be transformed from one form to another. A burning kola nut’s chemical energy transforms into thermal energy and light. In a hydroelectric dam like the Shiroro Dam, the gravitational potential energy of water transforms into kinetic energy, which then spins a turbine to generate electrical energy. This electrical energy then powers our homes and appliances in Lagos. This principle is known as the law of conservation of energy.

## Chemical Bonds and Reactions
Atoms form chemical bonds to achieve stable electron configurations. An ionic bond forms when sodium (Na) transfers an electron to chlorine (Cl), creating sodium chloride (NaCl), common table salt. A covalent bond forms when atoms like nitrogen (N) and hydrogen (H) share electrons to create ammonia (NH₃). In Nigeria, the production of soap involves the saponification reaction, where fats react with an alkali like sodium hydroxide to form soap and glycerol.

## Harmattan and Weather Patterns
The Harmattan is a seasonal, dry and dusty wind that blows from the Sahara Desert into West Africa, including Nigeria, typically from November to March. It brings cold, dry air with a significant amount of dust, reducing visibility. This wind mass forms due to the pressure difference between the cold, dense air over the Sahara and the warmer, moist air over the Gulf of Guinea. The Harmattan affects agriculture by causing harm to crops and can also impact respiratory health.

## The Nervous System
The nervous system is the body's control and communication network. It consists of the Central Nervous System (CNS), made up of the brain and spinal cord, and the Peripheral Nervous System (PNS), which includes all other nerves. The CNS processes information, while the PNS relays sensory information to the CNS and motor commands from the CNS to muscles. When you touch a hot pot, sensory nerves send a signal through the spinal cord to the brain, which instantly sends a motor signal back to your hand to pull away, preventing a burn.

## Electric Circuits
An electric circuit is a complete path through which electric current can flow. It requires a power source (like a battery), connecting wires, a load (such as a bulb), and a switch. According to Ohm’s Law, the current (I) in a circuit is directly proportional to the voltage (V) and inversely proportional to the resistance (R), expressed as I = V/R. For example, if a circuit with a 12V battery has a resistance of 4 ohms, the current flowing through it is 3 amperes (A).

## Jollof Rice and Nutritional Science
Jollof rice is a Nigerian dish whose nutritional value comes from its components. Rice provides carbohydrates, the body's main source of energy for daily activities. The tomato, pepper, and onion base supplies vitamins A and C and antioxidants. The addition of palm oil introduces fats, which are essential for absorbing fat-soluble vitamins and providing long-term energy. A balanced meal with protein like chicken or fish provides amino acids for growth and repair, making Jollof rice a complete meal when served with a side dish.
