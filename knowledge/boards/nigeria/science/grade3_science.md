# Nigerian (NERDC) Grade 3 Science
## Properties of Materials
Materials can be grouped by their properties. A material's property is how it looks, feels, or acts. Some materials are hard, like the stone in Lagos roads. Others are soft, like the cloth of your school uniform. Materials can also be smooth or rough. A piece of sandpaper is rough, while a metal spoon is smooth. We use these properties to choose the right material for a job. For example, a plastic cup is used for water because it does not let water pass through.

## Sources of Water
Water is a very important resource for life. We get water from different sources. Rain is a source of water; it fills our rivers and lakes during the Harmattan season. Rivers, like the River Niger, are also sources of water. Wells are holes in the ground from which we fetch water. Some water is stored in dams, like the Kainji Dam. We use this water for drinking, cooking jollof rice, and washing our clothes. It is important to keep our water sources clean.

## Living Things and Their Needs
All living things need certain things to stay alive. Plants, animals, and people are all living things. Living things need air to breathe. They need water to drink and to stay healthy. Living things also need food for energy. A plant needs sunlight to make its own food. A chicken needs grains to eat. People eat food like rice and beans. Living things also need a safe place to live. A snail lives in a shell to protect itself from harm during the dry season.

## Care for Our Environment
Our environment is everything around us, including the land, water, and air. We must keep our environment clean. We should not drop rubbish on the ground in places like Lagos. Rubbish can make people and animals sick. We should use a bin. We should not pollute our rivers. Keeping the environment clean helps us have fresh air and clean water to drink. It helps all living things, including the trees that give us shade.

## Simple Machines Around Us
Simple machines help us to do work more easily. A lever is a simple machine. A seesaw in the playground is a lever. It helps to lift a heavy person with less effort. An inclined plane is another simple machine. A ramp is an inclined plane. It helps us push a heavy box up to a higher place. We use simple machines every day to make tasks easier. A wheelbarrow helps us carry heavy loads of soil or kola nuts from the farm.

## Our Body Parts and Their Functions
Our body has many parts that help us to live and work. Our legs help us to walk and run to play football. Our arms and hands help us to carry things, like a bag of books or a plate of food. Our eyes help us to see our friends and the beautiful colours around us. Our ears help us to hear sounds, like the bell for school. Our mouth helps us to eat food and to talk to our family. We must take good care of our body parts.
