# Nigerian (NERDC) Grade 8 Science

## Structure and Functions of the Respiratory System
The respiratory system enables gas exchange in the human body. It includes the nasal passages, pharynx, larynx, trachea, bronchi, and lungs. Air travels down the trachea, which is supported by C-shaped cartilage rings, into the bronchi and then into tiny air sacs called alveoli where oxygen enters the blood and carbon dioxide leaves. The diaphragm and intercostal muscles control breathing. During the harmattan season, dusty air can irritate the airways, leading to coughing and respiratory issues, highlighting the system's vulnerability to environmental factors.

## Chemical Reactions and Equations
A chemical reaction involves the formation of new chemical substances with different properties. The law of conservation of mass states that matter is neither created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. For example, when magnesium burns in air, it combines with oxygen to form magnesium oxide. The balanced chemical equation is 2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO. This equation shows that two atoms of magnesium react with one molecule of oxygen gas to produce two units of magnesium oxide, ensuring the number of atoms is equal on both sides of the reaction.

## Electric Circuits and Ohm's Law
An electric circuit is a complete path along which electric current flows. It consists of a power source like a battery, connecting wires, and components such as resistors or bulbs. Ohm's Law states that the current (I) in a circuit is directly proportional to the potential difference (V) and inversely proportional to the resistance (R), expressed as I = V/R. For instance, if a 12-volt battery is connected to a 6-ohm resistor, the current flowing will be 2 amperes (I = 12V / 6Ω), demonstrating how voltage, current, and resistance are interrelated.

## Reproductive Health and Hygiene
Reproductive health involves the well-being of the male and female reproductive systems. Good hygiene practices, such as regular bathing and wearing clean undergarments, are essential for preventing infections. For boys, it is important to clean under the foreskin daily, while girls should wipe from front to back after using the toilet to avoid transferring bacteria. Access to accurate information helps adolescents make informed decisions about their bodies and health, reducing the risk of sexually transmitted infections and unwanted pregnancies.

## Force, Pressure, and Fluid Pressure
Force is a push or pull that can change an object's state of motion, measured in newtons (N). Pressure is the force applied per unit area, calculated as Pressure = Force / Area. Fluid pressure increases with depth due to the weight of the fluid above. For example, the pressure at the bottom of a swimming pool is greater than at the surface. This principle explains why dams are built thicker at the base to withstand the high water pressure, ensuring structural integrity and preventing collapse.

## Nutrition and Balanced Diet
A balanced diet provides all the essential nutrients for growth and energy. These include carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, and water. Carbohydrates like yams and rice provide energy, while proteins from beans and fish support body repair. In Nigeria, a meal of jollof rice with chicken and a side of fried plantains offers a balance of carbohydrates, proteins, and fats. A deficiency in vitamins can lead to health problems such as scurvy, emphasizing the importance of consuming a variety of foods to maintain good health.
