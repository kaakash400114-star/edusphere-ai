# Nigerian (NERDC) Grade 2 Science

## Living Things Around Us
Living things are plants and animals. We can identify them by their life processes. All living things grow, eat to get energy, and have young ones. For example, a mango seed grows into a mango tree. A hen eats maize and lays eggs to produce chicks. A dog gives birth to puppies. Plants like maize need water and sunlight to grow into tall plants. Animals like goats eat grass to grow big and strong. Both plants and animals are very important in our environment.

## Non-Living Things
Non-living things are things that are not alive. They do not grow, eat, or have babies. Examples include stones, books, tables, and chairs. A stone on the ground stays there and does not grow bigger. A book is used for reading and does not move by itself. Water is a non-living thing; it is used for drinking and bathing. A car is a non-living thing that is driven by people. We use both living and non-living things every day to make our lives better.

## The Harmattan Season
The Harmattan is a season we experience in Nigeria. It is a dry, dusty wind that blows from the Sahara desert. During Harmattan, the air is very dry and cold. We often see a lot of dust in the air, which makes the sky look brown or grey. Many people wear warm clothes like sweaters and cardigans to stay warm. Harmattan can make our skin and lips dry, so we use creams and drink lots of water. Sometimes, the dust can stop aeroplanes from flying safely.

## Our Five Senses
We use our five senses to learn about the world around us. Our sense of sight helps us see. For example, we see the green leaves on a tree or the bright colours of our uniform. Our sense of hearing helps us hear sounds, like the sound of a bell ringing or our friend's voice. Our sense of touch helps us feel things, like feeling the rough surface of a table or the smooth skin of a mango. Our sense of taste helps us taste food, like the sweet taste of jollof rice. Our sense of smell helps us smell, like the smell of cooking plantain.

## Caring for Our Body
We must care for our body to stay healthy. We wash our hands with soap and clean water, especially before eating and after using the toilet. This helps to remove germs. We eat healthy foods like fruits, vegetables, and rice to give our bodies energy. We also drink clean water to stay hydrated. We need to rest and sleep well so our bodies can grow strong. Brushing our teeth every morning and night keeps our mouth clean and prevents toothache. A healthy body helps us learn and play better.

## Uses of Water
Water is very useful in our daily lives. We drink water to stay alive and healthy. We use water to cook our food, like making jollof rice or bean soup. Farmers use water to water their crops, like maize and tomatoes, so they can grow well. We use water to bathe and wash our bodies to stay clean. We also use water to wash our clothes and dishes. In our homes, we get water from taps or wells. We must always be careful not to waste water and keep it clean for everyone to use.
