# Nigerian (NERDC) Grade 5 Science
## Basic Science Process Skills
Observation is using our senses to see, hear, smell, taste, and feel things around us. It is the first step in science. We look carefully at objects to find details. A scientist might observe that the harmattan wind brings cold, dry dust. We measure to find out how much or how long. We can measure the length of a desk with a ruler or the amount of water in a beaker. All good scientific work starts with careful observation and accurate measurement.
## Properties of Matter
Matter is anything that has weight and takes up space. All things around us are matter. Matter can be solid, liquid, or gas. A solid has a fixed shape and volume; a block of wood is solid. A liquid flows and takes the shape of its container; water is a liquid. A gas has no fixed shape or volume; the air we breathe is a gas. A kola nut is a solid. When you break it, it is still solid.
## Living and Non-living Things
Living things are things that are alive. They grow, eat, breathe, and reproduce. Non-living things are not alive. They do not grow, eat, breathe, or reproduce. A mango tree is living because it grows taller and produces fruit. A stone is non-living because it does not grow or eat. A chicken is living because it lays eggs. A desk is non-living because it is made of wood and cannot make more desks.
## Our Body and Health
Our bodies are made up of different parts that work together to keep us alive. We have a head, a trunk, and two arms and legs. It is important to keep our bodies clean and healthy. We should wash our hands with soap and clean water after using the toilet. We should eat good food like yam, rice, and beans to get strong. We should also drink clean water to avoid getting sick.
## Simple Machines
A machine is anything that makes work easier. A simple machine has few or no moving parts. Levers, wheels, and pulleys are simple machines. A seesaw is a lever that helps to lift a heavy person. A wheelbarrow is a wheel and axle that helps us carry heavy loads. We use simple machines every day. They help us do work with less effort, like pushing a heavy load in Lagos traffic.
## Safety and First Aid
Safety is very important to prevent accidents at home and in school. We must be careful with sharp objects like knives and scissors. To treat a small cut, we should wash the area with clean water, apply an antiseptic like Dettol, and cover it with a clean plaster. If we see a person injured, we should not move them but call for help from an adult or teacher. Knowing basic first aid can save lives.
