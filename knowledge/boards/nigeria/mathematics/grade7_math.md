# Nigerian (NERDC) Grade 7 Mathematics
## Whole Number Operations
Students will master advanced operations on whole numbers, including long multiplication and division with large dividends and divisors. They will apply the order of operations (BODMAS) to solve multi-step problems involving addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. For example, to calculate the total cost of 24 bags of rice at ₦3,500 per bag, they multiply 24 by 3,500. They will also solve word problems involving sharing money or items, such as dividing ₦180,000 equally among 12 family members for a new Jollof rice business.

## Fractions and Decimal Operations
Learners will perform all four operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division) on fractions and decimals. They will find the Lowest Common Multiple (LCM) to add and subtract fractions with different denominators. Students will convert between fractions and decimals, understanding that ½ is equivalent to 0.5. They will solve practical problems, like calculating the total cost of 2.5kg of kola nuts at ₦1,200 per kilogram or determining the decimal equivalent of the fraction of a Lagos traffic jam that lasts three-quarters of an hour.

## Basic Algebra and Equations
Students will be introduced to algebraic concepts using letters to represent unknown numbers (variables). They will write and solve simple linear equations in one variable. For instance, if 'b' represents the number of books and you have 3 books less than your friend who has 15, the equation is b = 15 - 3. Students will learn to isolate the variable to find its value, solving problems like finding the original price of an item sold at a discount, such as 'x - 500 = 2,000', to determine the cost before the Harmattan sale reduction.

## Measurement and Units
This topic covers the metric system of measurement for length, mass, and capacity. Students will convert between units, such as kilometres to metres and tonnes to kilograms. They will calculate the perimeter and area of rectangles and squares using formulas (Perimeter = 2(l + w), Area = l × w). Practical examples include finding the area of a rectangular piece of land in Lagos or calculating the volume of a large water tank in cubic metres to determine its capacity in litres.

## Ratio, Proportion, and Percentage
Learners will understand and solve problems involving ratios and proportions. They will express ratios in their simplest form and use them to share quantities, like dividing ₦90,000 in the ratio 2:3 among business partners. Students will convert between fractions, decimals, and percentages. For example, they will calculate what percentage of 200 students in a school, like one in Abuja, prefer Jollof rice over other dishes, or find 15% of ₦50,000 as a sales tax.

## Data Presentation and Probability
Students will collect, organise, and represent data using pictograms, bar charts, and pie charts. They will interpret information from these graphs, such as comparing the number of kola nuts sold in different markets. In probability, they will describe the likelihood of events using words like 'impossible', 'unlikely', 'likely', and 'certain'. For example, they might determine the probability that the Harmattan wind will blow on a given day in the North or the chance of picking a blue marble from a bag containing 3 blue and 7 red marbles.
