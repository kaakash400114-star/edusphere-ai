# Foundation English (Pre-KG)


## The alphabet: A to Z
Children learn the name and shape of each letter from A to Z. We use big, bright flashcards so they can see the letters clearly. We also find objects that start with each sound, like an apple for A. We sing the alphabet song every day to help them remember the order. We play a fun game where we clap our hands for each letter we say. This makes learning active and exciting. We also trace the letters in sand with our fingers to learn the shape. Children love to touch and play while they learn.

## Letter sounds: phonics first steps
Children learn the first sounds letters make, like a says "ah" and b says "buh." Teachers show them a toy apple and say, "Apple starts with /a/." They use actions, such as bouncing a ball for the letter 'b' and making its sound. Fun songs help them remember, like singing the alphabet tune slowly. Games, such as hiding objects and having children find ones starting with the sound of the day, make learning exciting. This hands-on play helps tiny children connect letters to sounds in a happy and simple way.

## My first words: naming things
Tiny children learn to name things around them. They start with simple words like ball, cup, and book. A teacher helps by showing them real things, like a big red ball. The teacher can also say words while doing actions, like clap your hands or stand up. Singing songs with words, like "Head, Shoulders, Knees, and Toes," is fun. A teacher can play a game where the child finds an object and says its name. Using toys and pictures makes learning new words easy and happy for everyone.

## Talking in sentences
Little children learn to talk in sentences. They learn to say, "The red ball is big." A teacher shows them toys. She says, "See the big ball. The ball is red." The children copy the words. They play games with action words. They run, jump, and clap. The teacher sings a song, "I am clapping my hands." The children clap and sing too. They learn to make sentences, "I am jumping high." Playing with toys and actions helps them talk. The teacher makes it fun and easy for them.

## Colours and describing words
We teach little children about colours and words that describe things. We use red, blue, and yellow blocks for them to touch and see. The teacher says, "Look at the big, red block!" We sing a song about a big balloon and a small ball. We play a game where children find something soft and something hard. We give them a fluffy teddy bear and a smooth stone to feel. The children love to show what they can find. We clap and smile when they use the new words. This makes learning fun and helps them remember.

## Rhymes and stories
Tiny children learn about words that sound the same through rhymes and stories. The teacher shows them fun objects like a cat and a hat to connect the rhyme. She uses big actions, like hopping or clapping, while saying rhyming words to help children move and learn. The teacher sings simple songs with rhymes, letting the children join in with motions and words. Games like finding things that rhyme help make learning a happy game. This fun play helps children listen for sounds and get ready for reading later.

## Writing: marks to letters
Little children learn to turn scribbles into letters. They first make random marks on paper, just like drawing. The teacher can help them by using fun activities. We can trace letters in sand with our fingers or in flour on a tray. Singing the ABC song makes learning letters happy and easy. A fun game is to find a letter and circle it on a big chart. We can also form letters with our bodies or with play dough. Using our hands and singing makes letters exciting to learn.
